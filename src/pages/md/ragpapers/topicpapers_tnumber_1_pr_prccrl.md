$16 \times 15 = 240$

$16 \times \boxed{\phantom{15}} = 480$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing  number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
16 \times a &= 480 \\\\
a           &= \dfrac{480}{16} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac{30 \times 16}{16} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac{30 \times \cancel{16}}{\cancel{16}} \\\\
a           &= 30 
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
