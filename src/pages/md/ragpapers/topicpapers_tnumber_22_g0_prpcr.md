$…………..\div 8 = 1.05$ cm

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \div 8       &= 1.05 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\dfrac {a} {8} &= 1.05 \ \text{cm} \\\\
a              &= 1.05 \times 8 \\\\
a              &= 8.40 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

The missing number $= 8.40 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8.40 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
</div>
