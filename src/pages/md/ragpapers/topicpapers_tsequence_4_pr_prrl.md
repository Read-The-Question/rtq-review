How many rectangles would there be in a pattern with $34$ 
triangles?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of rectangles $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 + (a-1) \times 2   &= 34 \\\\
4 + 2a - 2           &= 34 \\\\
4 - 2 + 2a           &= 34 \\\\
2 + 2a               &= 34 \\\\
2a                   &= 34 - 2 \\\\
2a                   &= 32 \\\\
a                    &= \dfrac{32}{2} \\\\
a                    &= \dfrac{16 \times 2}{2} \\\\
a                    &= \dfrac{16 \times \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2}} \\\\
a                    &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

Number of rectangles $= 16$

</div>

\end{aligned}

$

\end{aligned}
