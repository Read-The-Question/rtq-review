<div class='question subquestion'>

How much flour will she need to make $24$ scones?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the flour needed $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
12 \ \text{scones} : 2 \ \text{cups}                      &= 24 \ \text{scones} : a \ \text{cups} \\\\
\dfrac {12} {2}                                           &= \dfrac {24} {a} \\\\
12 \times a                                               &= 24 \times 2 \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {24 \times 2} {12} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {12 \times 2 \times 2} {12} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {\cancel {12} \times 2 \times 2} {\cancel {12}} \\\\
a                                                         &= 2 \times 2 \\\\
a                                                         &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

Flour needed $= 4 \ \text{cups}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{scones}                           &:& \text{flour} \\\\
= & 12                                      &:& 2 \ \text{cups} \\\\
= & 12 \times 2                             &:& 2 \times 2 \\\\
= & 24                                      &:& 4 \ \text{cups}
\end{array}
$

Flour needed $= 4 \ \text{cups}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4 \ \text{cups}$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

How much salt will she need to make $60$ scones?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the salt needed $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
12 \ \text{scones} : \dfrac {1} {2} \ \text{teaspoons}    &= 60 \ \text{scones} : a \ \text{teaspoons} \\\\
\dfrac {12} {\dfrac {1} {2}}                              &= \dfrac {60} {a} \\\\
12 \times a                                               &= 60 \times \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {60} {2 \times 12} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {12 \times 5} {2 \times 12} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {\cancel {12} \times 5} {2 \times \cancel {12}} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {5} {2} \\\\
a                                                         &= 2.5
\end{aligned}
$

Salt needed $= 2.5 \ \text{teaspoons}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{scones}                           &:& \text{salt} \\\\
= & 12                                      &:& \dfrac {1} {2} \ \text{teaspoons} \\\\
= & 12 \times 5                             &:& \dfrac {5} {2} \\\\
= & 60                                      &:& 2.5 \ \text{teaspoons}
\end{array}
$

Salt needed $= 2.5 \ \text{teaspoons}$

<div class='question subsubquestion'>

What is the maximum number of trays of scones which she can make?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the maximum number of scones made $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
150 \ \text{ml} : 12 \ \text{scones}                      &= 500 \ \text{ml} : a \ \text{scones} \\\\
\dfrac {150} {12}                                         &= \dfrac {500} {a} \\\\
150 \times a                                              &= 500 \times 12 \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {500 \times 12} {150} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {50 \times 10 \times 3 \times 4} {50 \times 3} \\\\
a                                                         &= \dfrac {\cancel {50} \times 10 \times \cancel 3 \times 4} {\cancel {50} \times \cancel 3} \\\\
a                                                         &= 10 \times 4 \\\\
a                                                         &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

Number of scones made $= 40$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{milk}                             &:& \text{scones} \\\\
= & 150 \ \text{ml}                         &:& 12 \\\\
= & 1                                       &:& \dfrac {12} {150} \\\\
= & 1 \times 500                            &:& \dfrac {12 \times 500} {150} \\\\
= & 500                                     &:& \dfrac {4 \times 3 \times 50 \times 10} {3 \times 50} \\\\
= & 500                                     &:& \dfrac {4 \times \cancel 3 \times \cancel {50} \times 10} {\cancel 3 \times \cancel {50}} \\\\
= & 500                                     &:& 4 \times 10 \\\\
= & 500 \ \text{ml}                         &:& 40
\end{array}
$

Number of scones made $= 40$
