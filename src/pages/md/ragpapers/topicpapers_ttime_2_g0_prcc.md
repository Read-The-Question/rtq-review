<div class='question question'>

The same coach takes $3 \ \text{h}$ to get to Bristol, a journey of $120 \ \text{miles}$. What is its average speed in miles per hour?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


ABBR: $\text {Speed} = \dfrac {\text{Distance}} {\text{Time}}$


ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
. The time is calculated in minutes, but the final speed has to be in miles per hour.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Speed}    &= \dfrac {120 \ \text {miles}} {3 \ \text {hour}} \\\\
                 &= \dfrac {120} {3} \ \text {miles/hour} \\\\
                 &= \dfrac {3 \times 40 } {3} \\\\
                 &= \dfrac { \cancel 3 \times 40} {\cancel 3} \\\\
                 &= 40 \ \text {miles/hour}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40 \ \text {miles/hour}$

</div>
