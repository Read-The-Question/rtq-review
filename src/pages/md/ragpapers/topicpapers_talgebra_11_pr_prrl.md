<div class='question subquestion'>

Using your answers from parts a. and b. find three numbers $A, B$ and $C$ such that

$
\begin{aligned}
A - B &= C\\ \text{and}\\
A^2 - B^2 &= C^3
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$A - B = C$

$A^2 - B^2 = C^3$

$
\begin{aligned}
A^2 - B^2           &= C^3 \\\
(A + B)(A - B)      &= C^3 \\\\
(A + B) \times C    &= C^3 \\\\
(A + B)             &= \dfrac{C^3}{C} \\\\
A + B               &= C^2 \\\\
3 + 1               &= 2^2
\end{aligned}
$

A,B and C would be $3,1 \ \text{and} \ 2$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3,1 \ \text{and} \ 2$

</div>
</div>
