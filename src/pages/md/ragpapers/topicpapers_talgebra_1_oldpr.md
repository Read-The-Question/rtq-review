<div class='question question'>

There are $24$ pupils in $7$S.

- Two thirds of them are boys.
- Half of the boys have brown hair.

How many of pupils in $7$S are boys with brown hair?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of boys $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of boys}      &= \dfrac {2} {3} \times 24 \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {2 \times 24} {3} \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {2 \times 8 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {2 \times 8 \times \cancel 3} {\cancel 3} \\\\
a                          &= 2 \times 8 \\\\
a                          &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Boys with brown hair}       &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times a\\\\
                                  &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 16 \\\\
                                  &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

$8$

Which equation gives the **same** value of $y$ for both $x=4$ and $x=-4$?

Put a ring round the correct equation.

$
y = 2x         \quad
y = 2 + x     \quad
y = x^2     \quad
y = \dfrac{x}{2}
$

| $y = 2 + x$          | $y = 2 + (-4) = -2$       | $y = 2 + 4 = 6$         |     No       |

$y = x ^ 2$ gives the same value of $y$ for both $x = 4$ and $x = -4$.
