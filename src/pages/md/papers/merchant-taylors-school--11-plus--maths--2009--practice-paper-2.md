Y                                       &= 360 - 279 \\\\

\end {aligned}

If a bag of $2 \ \text{pence}$ coins weighs $350 \ \text{g}$, how many coins are there?

</div>

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of coins} &= \dfrac {350} {7} \\\\

                       &= 50
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

In a barn there are only horses and people. If there are $12$ heads and $34$ legs how many horses and how many people are there?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let number of people $= a$

Let number of horses $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b   &= 12 \\\\
2a + 4b &= 34
\end {aligned}
$

We have got 2 variables and we have 2 equations to solve them .Let's use elimination to reduce it to just one variable. 

Multiply first equation by 2.

$
\begin {aligned}
2 \times a + 2 \times b &= 2 \times 12 \\\\
2a + 2b                 &= 24
\end {aligned}
$

Subtract the above equation from the second equation.

$
\begin {aligned}
2a + 4b - (2a + 2b)        &= 34 - 24 \\\\
2a + 4b - 2a - 2b          &= 10 \\\\
4b - 2b                    &= 10 \\\\
2b                         &= 10 \\\\
b                          &= \dfrac{10} {2} \\\\
b                          &= \dfrac{2 \times 5} {2} \\\\
b                          &= \dfrac{\cancel 2 \times 5} {\cancel 2} \\\\
b                          &= 5
\end {aligned}
$

Let's substitute the value of $b$ in one of the equations.

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 5  &= 12 \\\\
a      &= 12 - 5 \\\\
a      &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Number of people $= 7$

Number of horses $= 5$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

People $=$ $7$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Horses $=$ $5$

</div>

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Volume of a cuboid} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$


ABBR: $\text{Volume of a cube} = (\text{Side}) ^ { 3 }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of cubes} &= \dfrac {\text{Volume of block}} {\text{Volume of cube}} \\\\
                       &= \dfrac {12 \times 24 \times 18} {3 \times 3 \times 3} \\\\
                       &= \dfrac {3 \times 4 \times 3 \times 8 \times 6 \times 3} {3 \times 3 \times 3} \\\\
                       &= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 4 \times \cancel {3} \times 8 \times 6 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel {3} \times \cancel {3} \times \cancel {3}} \\\\
                       &= 4 \times 8 \times 6 \\\\
                       &= 192
\end{aligned}
$

$192$
