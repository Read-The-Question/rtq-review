How much change did they get?

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}

                              &= 4.50 + 0.45 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 4.95 \\\\
\text {Change they got}       &= 20 - 3 \times 4.95 \\\\
                              &= 20 - 14.85 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 5.15
\end {aligned}
$
