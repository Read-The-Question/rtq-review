Write down a decimal between $90\%$ and $95\%$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 90 \%, \quad \underline{91\%}, \quad \underline{92\%}, \quad \underline{93\%}, \quad \underline{94\%}, \quad 95\% \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Let's convert 91\% into decimal.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {91} {100}\\\\
&= 0.91
\end{aligned}
$

\begin{aligned}

&= 0.925
\end{aligned}
