An equilateral triangle has three sides the same length. What is the size of one of the angles? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


ABBR: $\text{All angles of an equilateral triangle are equal}$.


Let the angle $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + a              &= 180 \degree \\\\
3a                     &= 180 \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {180} {3} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {3 \times 60} {3} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 60} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
a                      &= 60 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Angle $= 60 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$60 \degree$

</div>
