Work out $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of $72$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{3}{8} \ \text{of}\ 72 \\\\
&= \dfrac{3}{8} \times 72 \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 72}{8} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 8 \times 9} {8} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 9 \times \cancel{8}} {\cancel{8}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 9 \\\\
&= 27
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$27$

<div class='question question'>

What is the difference between a tenth of $5$ and a fifth of $10$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Difference}  &=  \dfrac {1} {10} \ \text {of} \ 5 - \dfrac {1} {5} \ \text {of} \ 10 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {1} {10} \times 5 - \dfrac {1} {5} \times 10 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5} {10} - \dfrac {10} {5} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5} {10} - \dfrac {10 \times 2} {5 \times 2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5} {10} - \dfrac {20} {5 \times 2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 - 20} {10} \\\\
                    &= - \dfrac {15} {10} \\\\
                    &= - \dfrac {5 \times 3} {5 \times 2} \\\\
                    &= - \dfrac {\cancel{5} \times 3} {\cancel{5} \times 2} \\\\
                    &= - \dfrac {3} {2}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$- \dfrac {3} {2}$

</div>
