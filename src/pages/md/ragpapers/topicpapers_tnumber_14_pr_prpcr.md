<div class='question question'>

Which of the following distances is closest to $6 \ \text{feet} \text{?}$ 
Circle the letter of your answer.

- $110 \ \text{cm}$

- $1.8 \ \text{m}$

- $12 \ \text{m}$

- $1500 \ \text{mm}$

<div class='working'>

& = 3.60892 \ \text{feet}\ \\\\
1.8\ \text{m}&= 1.8\times 3.28084\ \text{feet}\ \\\\
& = 5.90551 \ \text{feet}\ \\\\
12\ \text{m}&= 12 \times 3.28084\ \text{feet}\ \\\\ 
& = 39.3701 \ \text{feet}\ \\\\
1500\ \text{mm}&= 1500 \times 3.28084\ \text{feet}\ \\\\ 
& = 4.92126 \ \text{feet}\ \\\\
&\therefore \text{closest distance is 1.8 m}\
\end{aligned}
