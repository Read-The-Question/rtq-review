<div class='question question'>

There are $4$ red counters and $3$ black counters in bag $A$. 
There are $4$ red counters and $5$ black counters in bag $B$. 
Pam picks a counter from bag $A$ and places it in bag. 
She does not know what colour counter she picked from bag $A$. 
She then picks a counter from bag $B$. Which of the following 
could be the probability of Pam picking a red counter from bag $B$.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(Not yellow)} &= \dfrac {4} {9} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {48} {60} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {4\times 12} {5\times 12} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel {12}} {5\times \cancel {12}} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {4} {5}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {4} {5}$

</div>
