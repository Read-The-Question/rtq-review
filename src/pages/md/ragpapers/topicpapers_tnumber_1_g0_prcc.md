<div class='question question'>

Write $2.4 \ \text{kg}$ in $\text{g}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&=  2.4   \  \text{kg}\\\\
&=  2.4   \  \times 1000 \  \text{g}\\\\
&= \dfrac{24 \times 100 \times 10}{10} \  \text{g}\\\\
&= \dfrac{24 \times 100 \times \cancel{10}}{\cancel{10}} \  \text{g}\\\\
&= 24 \times 100 \  \text{g}\\\\
&=  2400  \  \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
