<div class='question question'>

What number is halfway between $137$ and $859$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

The number that is halfway between two numbers is the average of two numbers.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {137 + 859} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {996} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 498} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 498} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
&= 498
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$498$

</div>
</div>
