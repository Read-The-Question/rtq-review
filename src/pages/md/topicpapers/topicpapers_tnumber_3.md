<div class='question question'>

Write down a number between $0.3$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= (0.3 + \dfrac {2} {5}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac {3} {10} + \dfrac {2} {5}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac {3} {10} + \dfrac {2 \times 2} {5 \times 2}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac {3} {10} + \dfrac {4} {10}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac {3 + 4} {10}) \div 2 \\\\
&= \dfrac {7} {10} \times \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7} {10 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7} {20} \\\\
&= 0.35
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>
