<div class='question question'>

$x$ and $y$ are positive whole numbers. The ratio of $x$ to $y$ is $7:3$. The difference between $x$ and $y$ is $24$.

Find the value of $x$ and the value of $y$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
x - y                                                       &= 24 \\\\
x                                                           &= 24 + y
\end{aligned}
$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
7 \ \text{x} : 3 \ \text{y}                     &= (24 + y) \ \text{x} : y \ \text{y} \\\\
\dfrac{7}{3}                                    &= \dfrac {24 + y} {y} \\\\
7 \times y                                      &= (24 + y) \times 3 \\\\
7y                                              &= 24 \times 3 + 3y \\\\
7y                                              &= 72+ 3y \\\\
7y - 3y                                         &= 72 \\\\
4y                                              &= 72 \\\\
y                                               &= \dfrac {72} {4} \\\\
y                                               &= \dfrac {4 \times 18} {4} \\\\
y                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 18} {\cancel 4} \\\\
y                                               &= 18
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of y to find x.

$
\begin{aligned}
x           &= 24 + y \\\\
            &= 24 + 18 \\\\
            &= 42
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$x= 42,y= 18$

<div class='question question'>

In a group of $48$ children, the ratio of boys to girls is $3:5$.

How many boys must join the group to make the ratio of boys to girls $5 : 3$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of boys before adding new boys $= b$

Number of girls before adding new boys $= 48 - b$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
b \ \text{boys} : (48 - b) \ \text{girls}       &= 3 \ \text{boys} : 5 \ \text{girls} \\\\
\dfrac {b} {48 - b}                             &= \dfrac {3} {5} \\\\
b \times 5                                      &= 3 \times (48 - b) \\\\
5b                                              &= 3 \times 48 - 3b \\\\
5b                                              &= 144 - 3b \\\\
5b + 3b                                         &= 144 \\\\
8b                                              &= 144 \\\\
b                                               &= \dfrac {144} {8} \\\\
b                                               &= \dfrac {8 \times 18} {8} \\\\
b                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 8 \times 18} {\cancel 8} \\\\
b                                               &= 18
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of boys}       &= 18 \\\\
\text{Number of girls}      &= 48 - 18 \\\\
                            &= 30
\end{aligned}
$

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{girls}        &:& \text{boys} \\\\
= & 3                   &:& 5 \\\\
= & 1                   &:& \dfrac{5} {3} \\\\
= & 30                  &:& \dfrac{5 \times 30} {3} \\\\
= & 30                  &:& \dfrac{5 \times 3 \times 10} {3} \\\\
= & 30                  &:& \dfrac{5 \times \cancel 3 \times 10} {\cancel 3} \\\\
= & 30                  &:& 5 \times 10 \\\\
= & 30                  &:& 50
\end{array}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of boys added}       &= 50 - 18 \\\\
                                  &= 32
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$32$

</div>
</div>

A drink contains lime, orange and apple juices in the ratio $2:7:6$. Find the volume of orange juice contained in $300 \ \text{ml}$ of the drink.

</div>
<div class='workings'>

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
(2 + 7 + 6) \ \text{ml} : 7 \ \text{ml}         &= 300 \ \text{ml} : a \ \text{ml} \\\\
\dfrac {2 + 7 + 6} {7}                          &= \dfrac {300} {a} \\\\
\dfrac {15} {7}                                 &= \dfrac {300} {a} \\\\
15 \times a                                     &= 300 \times 7 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {300 \times 7} {15} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {20 \times 15 \times 7} {15} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {20 \times \cancel {15} \times 7} {\cancel {15}} \\\\
a                                               &= 20 \times 7 \\\\
a                                               &= 140 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

Orange juice $= 140 \ \text{ml}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{drink}              &:& \text{orange juice} \\\\
= & (2 + 7 + 6) \ \text{ml}   &:& 7 \ \text{ml} \\\\
= & 15                        &:& 7 \\\\
= & 15 \times 20              &:& 7 \times 20 \\\\
= & 300 \ \text{ml}           &:& 140 \ \text{ml}
\end{array}
$

Orange juice $= 140 \ \text{ml}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

$140 \text { ml}$

<div class='question question'>

Jason and Patrick win a prize of $\pounds 75$ for their entry into an inventing competition. For every $2$ hours that Patrick worked on the project Jason worked $3$, so it is decided that for every $\pounds 2$ that Patrick receives Jason should receive $\pounds 3$. How much will Jason and Patrick receive? Write your 
answers in the boxes below. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money received by Jason $= a$

Money received by Patrick $= 75 - a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
\pounds 2 : \pounds 3          &= \pounds (75 - a) : \pounds a \\\\
\dfrac {2} {3}                 &= \dfrac {75 - a} {a} \\\\
2a                             &= 3 \times (75 - a) \\\\
2a                             &= 3 \times 75 - 3a \\\\
2a                             &= 225 - 3a \\\\
2a + 3a                        &= 225 \\\\
5a                             &= 225 \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {225} {5} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {5 \times 45} {5} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 45} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                              &= \pounds 45
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Money received by Jason}        &= \pounds 45 \\\\
\text{Money received by Patrick}      &= 75 - 45 \\\\
                                      &= \pounds 30
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \pounds                     &:& \pounds                     &:& \pounds \\\\
= & 2 + 3                       &:& 2                           &:& 3 \\\\
= & 5                           &:& 2                           &:& 3 \\\\
= & 5 \times 15                 &:& 2 \times 15                 &:& 3 \times 15 \\\\
= & \pounds 75                  &:& \pounds 30                  &:& \pounds 45
\end{array}
$

Money received by Patrick $= \pounds 30$

Money received by Jason $= \pounds 45$

$\pounds 45, \pounds 30$
