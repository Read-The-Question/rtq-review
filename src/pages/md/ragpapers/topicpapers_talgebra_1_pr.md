<div class='question question'>

A box contains bags of crisps.

Each bag of crisps weighs $25 \ \text{grams}$.

Altogether, the bags of crisps inside weigh $1 \ \text{kg}$.

How many bags of crisps are inside the box?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of bags of crisps $= b$

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required.


Total weight of the bags of crisps $= 1 \ \text{kg} = 1000 \ \text{grams}$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
b \times 25                 &= 1000 \\\\
b                           &= \dfrac {1000} {25} \\\\
b                           &= \dfrac {25 \times 40} {25} \\\\
b                           &= \dfrac {\cancel {25} \times 40} {\cancel {25}} \\\\
b                           &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

Number of bags of crisps inside the box $= 40$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$

</div>

</div>

$

a                                   &= \dfrac {25 \times \cancel 2} {3 \times \cancel 2} \\\\
a                                   &= \dfrac {25} {3} \\\\
a                                   &= 8 \dfrac {1} {3} \\\\
a                                   &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

Number of egg boxes needed $= 9$.

<div class='question question'>

A ruler costs $k$ pence.

A pen costs $m$ pence.

Match each statement with the correct expression for the amount in pence.

The first on is done for you.

![missing table](/papers/missing_table.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of 5 rulers and 5 pens}              &= \text{Cost of 5 rulers} + \text{Cost of 5 pens} \\\\
                                                &= 5k + 5m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                &= 5 (k + m) \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Difference}                               &= \text{Cost of 5 pens} - \text{Cost of 5 rulers} \\\\
                                                &= 5m - 5k \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The change}                               &= \pounds 5 - \text{Cost of 5 pens} \\\\
                                                &= 5 \times 100 \ \text{pence} - 5m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                &= 500 - 5m \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Total cost of $5$ rulers and $5$ pens $= 5 (k + m) \ \text{pence}$.

The cost of $5$ pens is $5m - 5k \ \text{pence}$ more than the cost of $5$ rulers.

The change from $\pounds 5$, when you buy $5$ pens $= 500 - 5m \ \text{pence}$.

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

Seven *consecutive* whole numbers add up to $7357$.  What is the largest of the seven numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the seven whole numbers $= a, a+1, a+2, a+3, a+4, a+5, a+6$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + 1 + a + 2 + a + 3 + a + 4 + a + 5 + a + 6   &= 7357 \\\\
7a + 21                                             &= 7357 \\\\
7a                                                  &= 7357 - 21 \\\\
7a                                                  &= 7336 \\\\
a                                                   &= \dfrac {7336} {7} \\\\
a                                                   &= \dfrac {7 \times 1048} {7} \\\\
a                                                   &= \dfrac {\cancel 7 \times 1048} {\cancel 7} \\\\
a                                                   &= 1048
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The largest whole number}     &= 1048 + 6 \\\\
                                    &= 1054
\end{aligned}
$
