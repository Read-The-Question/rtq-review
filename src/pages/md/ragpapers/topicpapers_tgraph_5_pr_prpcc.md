<div class='question subquestion'>

How many hamsters were there in the survey?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the total number of pets $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {120} {360} \times a     &= 48 \\\\
a                               &= 48 \times \dfrac {360} {120} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {48 \times 360} {120} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {48 \times 120 \times 3} {120} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {48 \times \cancel {120} \times 3} {\cancel {120}} \\\\
a                               &= 48 \times 3 \\\\
a                               &= 144
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of hamsters}       &= \dfrac {60} {360} \times 144 \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {60 \times 144} {360} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {60 \times 6 \times 24} {60 \times 6} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel {60} \times \cancel 6 \times 24} {\cancel {60} \times \cancel 6} \\\\
                                &= 24
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{cats}                             &:& \text{hamsters} \\\\
= & 120 \degree                             &:& 60 \degree \\\\
= & 1                                       &:& \dfrac {60} {120} \\\\
= & 1 \times 48                             &:& \dfrac {60} {120} \times 48 \\\\
= & 48                                      &:& \dfrac {60 \times 48} {120} \\\\
= & 48                                      &:& \dfrac {60 \times 2 \times 24} {60 \times 2} \\\\
= & 48                                      &:& \dfrac {\cancel {60} \times \cancel 2 \times 24} {\cancel {60} \times \cancel 2} \\\\
= & 48                                      &:& 24
\end{array}
$
