<div class='question question'>

How many seconds in a day?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As we know, there are $24 \ \text {hours}$ in a day. so,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 1 \ \text{day} \\\\
&= 24 \ \text{hours} \\\\
&= 24 \times 60 \times 60 \ \text {seconds} \\\\
&= 24 \times 3600 \\\\
&= 86400 \ \text {seconds}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
