The charity spends $60\%$ of the donation on buying equipment. How much of the donation is not spent?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

If the charity spends $60 \%$ on equipments, then the donation not spent is $100 - 60 = 40 \%$.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Donation not spent}   &= 40 \% \ \text {of} \ \pounds 12 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {40} {100} \times 12 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {40 \times 12} {100} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {480} {100} \\\\
                            &= \pounds 4.80
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
