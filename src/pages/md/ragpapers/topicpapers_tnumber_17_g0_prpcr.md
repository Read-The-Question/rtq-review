$7 \ \text{inches}$ in centimetres

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 7 \ \text{inches} \\\\

\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

$70  \ \text{inches}$ in metres

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 70 \ \text{inches} \\\\
&= 70 \times 2.5 \ \text {centimeters} \\\\
&= 70 \times \dfrac {2.5} {100} \ \text {meters} \\\\
&= 70 \times 0.025 \\\\
&= 0.175 \ \text {meters}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$0.175 \ \text{meters}$

</div>
