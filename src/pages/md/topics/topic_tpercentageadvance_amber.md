&= 2338.3
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

What percentage of Mark's counters are blue?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {4} \times 100 \\\\
&= \dfrac {1 \times 100} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1 \times 25 \times 4} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1 \times 25 \times \cancel{4}} {\cancel{4}} \\\\
&= 25 \%
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$25 \%$

<div class='question subquestion'>

Mark has 40 counters in his bag.

How many of his counters are not blue?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Green,
$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 40 \\\\
&= \dfrac {40} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {20 \times 2} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {20 \times \cancel{2}} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
&= 20
\end{aligned}
$

Red,
$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {4} \times 40 \\\\
&= \dfrac {40} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times 4} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times \cancel{4}} {\cancel{4}} \\\\
&= 10
\end{aligned}
$

Not blue,
$
\begin{aligned}
&= 20 + 10 \\\\
&= 30
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$30$

</div>
