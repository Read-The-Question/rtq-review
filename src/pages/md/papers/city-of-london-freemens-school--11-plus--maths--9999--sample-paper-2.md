<div class='question question'>

A card is picked from a pack of ordinary $52$ playing cards.
What is the probability of getting a king, queen or jack?

- $\dfrac{3}{13}$

- $\dfrac{3}{52}$

- $\dfrac{12}{13}$

- $\dfrac{13}{52}$


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin {aligned}
\text {P(king or queen or jack)}     &= \dfrac {4 + 4 + 4} {52} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {12} {52} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {3 \times 4} {4 \times 13} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {4} \times 13} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {3} {13}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>A</p>
</div>

$\dfrac {3} {13}$

\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {7} {12} < \dfrac {8} {12} < \dfrac {10} {12} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7} {12} < \dfrac {2} {3} < \dfrac {5} {6}
\end{aligned}
