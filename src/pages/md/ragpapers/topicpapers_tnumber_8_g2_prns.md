a                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {120} \times 6 \times \cancel {8} \times \cancel {10}} {\cancel {120} \times \cancel {8} \times \cancel {10}} \\\\

\end{aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Here are three numbers
and two operations on five cards.

$\huge{ \boxed{\phantom{}3} \ \boxed{\phantom{}7} \ \boxed{24} \ \boxed{\phantom{}+} \ \boxed{\phantom{}\div} } $

Which of these gives the answer $15?$

- $24 \div 7 + 3$
- $3 + 7 \div 24$
- $7 + 3 \div 24$
- $3 \div 7 + 24$
- $24 \div 3 + 7$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text{A}    &= 24 \div 7 + 3 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {24} {7} + 3 \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24} {7} + \dfrac {7 \times 3} {7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24} {7} + \dfrac {21} {7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24 + 21}{7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{45}{7} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{B}    &= 3 + 7 \div 24 \\\\
            &= 3 + \dfrac {7} {24} \\\\
            &= 3 \dfrac{7}{24} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{C}    &= 7 + 3 \div 24 \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {3} {24} \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {3} {3 \times 8} \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {\cancel {3}} {\cancel {3} \times 8} \\\\
