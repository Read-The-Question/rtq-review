Work out $\dfrac{2}{5} + \dfrac{3}{8}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {2} {5} + \dfrac {3} {8} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 8} {5 \times 8} + \dfrac {3 \times 5} {8 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac{ 16} {40} + \dfrac {15} {40} \\\\
&= \dfrac {16 + 15} {40} \\\\
&= \dfrac {31} {40}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

Simplify $\dfrac{12}{60}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {12} {60} \\\\
&= \dfrac {12} {12 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{12}} {\cancel{12} \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {5} 
\end{aligned}
$
