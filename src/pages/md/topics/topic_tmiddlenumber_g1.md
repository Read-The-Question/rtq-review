<div class='question question'>

$0.126$ is halfway between two numbers.

One of the numbers is $0.11$.

What is the other number? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {0.11 + a} {2}                &= 0.126 \\\\
0.11 + a                             &= 0.126 \times 2 \\\\
0.11 + a                             &= 0.252 \\\\
a                                    &= 0.252 - 0.11 \\\\
a                                    &= 0.142
\end{aligned}
$

Other number $= 0.142$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$0.142$

</div>
