<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

It is possible to move from point $A$ to point $B$ by combining some $K$-steps and some $T$-steps.

Write down how many $K$-steps and $T$-steps are needed.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of K step $= a$

Let the number of T step $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 3b    &= 9    &  (1) \\\\
2a + b    &= 8    &  (2)
\end {aligned}
$

Lets subtract equation $2$ from twice of equation $1$ to get the value of b.

$
\begin {aligned}
2 \times (a + 3b) - (2a + b)      &= 2 \times 9 - 8 \\\\
2a + 6b - 2a - b                  &= 18 - 8 \\\\
2a - 2a + 6b - b                  &= 10 \\\\
5b                                &= 10 \\\\
b                                 &= \dfrac {10} {5} \\\\
b                                 &= \dfrac {5 \times 2} {5} \\\\
b                                 &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 2} {\cancel 5} \\\\
b                                 &= 2
\end {aligned}
$

Lets substitute value of b in equation $1$ to get the value of a.

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 3b            &= 9 \\\\
a + 3 \times 2    &= 9 \\\\
a + 6             &= 9 \\\\
a                 &= 9 - 6 \\\\
a                 &= 3
\end {aligned}
$

$3$ K-steps

$2$ T-steps

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$K$-step $=$ $3$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

$T$-step $=$ $2$
