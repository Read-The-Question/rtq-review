$h = 80 \degree$

Let's use the value of $h$ to find $a$.

$
\begin{aligned}
h + a              &= 180 \\\\
80 + a             &= 180 \\\\
a                  &= 180 - 80 \\\\
a                  &= 100 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $a$ to find $b$.

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 80 + 60 + b           &= 360 \\\\
100 + 80 + 60 + b         &= 360 \\\\
b + 240                   &= 360 \\\\
b                         &= 360 - 240 \\\\
b                         &= 120 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

$j$ and $b$ are opposite angles of a parallelogram.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Therefore,} \ j &= b \\\\
                    j &= 120 \degree
\end{aligned}
