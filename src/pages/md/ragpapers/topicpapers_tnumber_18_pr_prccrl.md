<div class='question subquestion'>

A different number machine produces the input and output table below.

![missing table](/papers/missing_table.svg)


What is the rule for this number machine?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the output $= x$

Let the rule $= a \times x + b$

From Row 1, where $x = 2$,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 2 + b &= 6 \\\\
2a + b &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

From Row 2, where $x = 4$,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 4 + b &= 7 \\\\
4a + b &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve the equations for \' a \'

$
\begin{aligned}
4a + b &= 7 \\\\
4a + (6 - 2a) &= 7 \\\\
4a - 2a &= 7 - 6 \\\\
2a &= 1 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve the equations for \' b \'

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 6 \\\\
2 \times \dfrac{1}{2} + b &= 6 \\\\
1 + b &= 6 \\\\
b &= 5
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the rule $= \dfrac{x}{2} + 5$

Lets verify the same for Row 3

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{x}{2} + 5 \\\\
&= \dfrac{8}{2} + 5 \\\\
&= 4 + 5 \\\\
&= 9 = 9
\end{aligned}
$

Lets verify the same for Row 4

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{x}{2} + 5 \\\\
&= \dfrac{12}{2} + 5 \\\\
&= 6 + 5 \\\\
&= 11 = 11
\end{aligned}
$

The rule is **Divide the number by 2 and add 5**
