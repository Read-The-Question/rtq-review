Write down a fraction between $60\%$ and $70\%$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {60\% + 70\%} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {130\%} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {130} {100 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {130} {200} \\\\
&= \dfrac {130} {200} \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times 13} {10 \times 20} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{10} \times 13} {\cancel{10} \times 20} \\\\
&= \dfrac {13} {20}
\end{aligned}
