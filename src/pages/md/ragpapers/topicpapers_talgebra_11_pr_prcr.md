What does $\boxed{s} \ \boxed{a} \ \boxed{l} \ \boxed{t}$ add up to?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Substitute $a = 1$ in equation $2$

$
\begin{aligned}
1 + a + p           &= 9 \\\\
a + p               &= 9 - 1 \\\\
a + p               &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

Substitute $a + p$ in equation $3$

$
\begin{aligned}
l + a + p           &= 11 \\\\
l + 8               &= 11 \\\\
l                   &= 11 - 8 \\\\
l                   &= 3 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

From equation $1$

$
\begin{aligned}
s + a + t           &= 8 \\\\
s + a + t + l       &= 8 + l \\\\
s + a + l + t       &= 8 + l \\\\
s + a + l + t       &= 8 + 3 \\\\
s + a + l + t       &= 11 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
