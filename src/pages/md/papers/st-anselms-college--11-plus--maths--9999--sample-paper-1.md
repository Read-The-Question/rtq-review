<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}

8x + 56           &= 22x \\\\
56                &= 22x - 8x \\\\
56                &= 14x \\\\
14x               &= 56 \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {56} {14} \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {4 \times 14} {14} \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel {14}} {\cancel {14}} \\\\
x                 &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

\end {aligned}
