A piece of wire $36 \ \text{cm}$ long is bent into the shape of a rectangle. If the rectangle is twice as long as it is wide, what is the area?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the width of the rectangle $= w$

Length of the rectangle $= 2w$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \times (w + 2w)           &= 36 \\\\
2 \times 3w                 &= 36 \\\\
6w                          &= 36 \\\\
w                           &= \dfrac {36} {6} \\\\
w                           &= \dfrac {6 \times 6} {6} \\\\
w                           &= \dfrac {\cancel {6} \times 6} {\cancel {6}} \\\\
w                           &= 6 \ \text {cm} \\\\
\text {Width}               &= 6 \ \text {cm} \\\\
\text {Length}              &= 2 \times 6 \\\\
                            &= 12 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find the area of the rectangle.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Area}            &= 6 \times 12 \\\\
                        &= 72 \ \text {cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

The ratio of height to width on an old television screen is $3:4$.

If the height of a television screen was $21 \ \text{cm}$, find the perimeter of the screen.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of rectangle} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$


Let the width of the television $= w$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {21} {w}                                     &= \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\
21 \times 4                                         &= 3 \times w \\\\
3 \times w                                          &= 21 \times 4 \\\\
3w                                                  &= 21 \times 4 \\\\
w                                                   &= \dfrac {21 \times 4} {3} \\\\
w                                                   &= \dfrac {3 \times 7 \times 4} {3} \\\\
w                                                   &= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 7 \times 4} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
w                                                   &= 7 \times 4 \\\\
w                                                   &= 28 \ \text {cm} \\\\
\text{Perimeter of television}                      &= 2 \times (21 + 28) \\\\
                                                    &= 2 \times 49 \\\\
                                                    &= 98 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

$98 \ \text {cm}$
