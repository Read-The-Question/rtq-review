<div class='question question'>

A bag contains $3$ red and $6$ green marbles. If you pick out a marble at random, what is the chance that it will be red?

-  $\dfrac{1}{3}$
-  $\dfrac{1}{2}$
-  $\dfrac{1}{9}$
-  $\dfrac{2}{3}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Probability of red marble}   &= \dfrac {\text {Number of red marbles}} {\text {Total number of marbles}} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {3} {3 + 6} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {3} {9} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {3} {3 \times 3} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {3}} {\cancel {3} \times 3} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>A</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {3}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

Issy tosses a **fair coin** four times. She gets Heads, Heads, Heads, Tails. If she tosses it a
fifth time, is she **more likely** to get Heads or Tails?

-  equally likely
-  Heads
-  Tails
-  not enough information

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Probability of getting Heads}    &= \dfrac {Favourable outcomes} {Total possible outcomes} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
\text {Probability of getting Heads}    &= \dfrac {Favourable outcomes} {Total possible outcomes} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {1} {2}
\end {aligned}
$

It is equally likely to get Heads or Tails fifth time.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>A</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$\text {Equally likely}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='workingcodeblock'>

<pre class='language-latex'><code class='language-latex'>$

                                        &= \dfrac {1} {2}
