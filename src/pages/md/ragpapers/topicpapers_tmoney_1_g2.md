                        &= \dfrac {5 \times 371} {5} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel {5} \times 371} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
                        &= 371
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

A group of $30$ adults and $16$ children paid  $\pounds 408$ in total to 
watch a football match. Each child ticket cost  $\pounds 3$.

What was the cost of ach adult ticket?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of an adult ticket (in $\pounds$) $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
30 \times a + 16 \times 3   &= 408 \\\\
30a + 48                    &= 408 \\\\
30a                         &= 408 - 48 \\\\
30a                         &= 360 \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {360} {30} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {12 \times 30} {30} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {12 \times \cancel {30}} {\cancel {30}} \\\\
a                           &= \pounds 12
\end {aligned}
$

Cost of an adult ticket $= \pounds 12$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

In a sale, prices are reduced by $15 \%$.

What is the sale price of a hoody that originally cost  $\pounds 30?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

If the price is reduced by $15 \%$, then the sale price will be $100 - 15 = 85 \%$ of the original price.

Using that, we get

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Sale price}      &= 85 \% \ \text {of} \ 30 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac{85}{100} \times 30 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac{85 \times 30}{100} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac{2550}{100} \\\\
                        &= \pounds 25.50
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>

$\pounds 25.50$

<div class='question subquestion'>

Guy has twice as many stickers as Asif and five more than Kayal.

They have $35$ stickers altogether.

How many stickers does Guy have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of stickers Asif has $= a$

Number of stickers Guy has $= 2a$

Number of stickers Kyal has $= 2a - 5$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 2a + 2a - 5             &= 35 \\\\
5a                          &= 35 + 5 \\\\
5a                          &= 40 \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {40}{5} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {5 \times 8}{5} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {\cancel{5} \times 8}{\cancel{5}} \\\\
a                           &= 8 \\\\
\text{Stickers Guy has}     &= 2a \\\\
                            &= 2 \times 8 \\\\
                            &= 16
\end {aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

How much change would you get from $\pounds 10$ if you bought $7$ $\text{Chocco}^{\text{TM}}$ bars at $78 \ \text{p}$ each?

</div>

<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text {Money spent}             &= 7 \times 78 \\\\
                                &= 546 \ \text{p} \\\\
                                &= \pounds 5.46 \\\\
\text {Change recieved}         &= 10 - 5.46 \\\\
                                &= \pounds 4.54
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
