Calculate $11\%$ of $32600$.

</div>

<div class='working'>

\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{11}{100} \times 32600 \\\\
&= \dfrac{11 \times 32600}{100} \\\\
&= \dfrac{11 \times 326\cancel{00}}{1\cancel{00}} \\\\
&= 11 \times 326 \\\\
&= 3586
\end{aligned}
$
