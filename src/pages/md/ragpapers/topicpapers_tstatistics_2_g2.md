What was the mean number of runs he scored per innings?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Mean runs scored}        &= \dfrac {0 + 25 + 15 + 40 + 52 + 15 + 6 + 8 + 25 + 35 + 10} {11} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {231} {11} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {11 \times 21} {11} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel {11} \times 21} {\cancel {11}} \\\\
                                &= 21
\end{aligned}
$
