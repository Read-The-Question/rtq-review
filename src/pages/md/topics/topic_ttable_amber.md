Explain how the table shows that $49 \times 343$ = $16807$

\end{aligned}

<div class='question subquestion'>

Use the table to help you work out the value of 

$\dfrac {5764801} {823543}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

According to table ,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {5764801} {823543} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7^8} {7^7} \\\\
&= 7^8-7 \\\\
&= 7^1 \\\\
&= 7
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$7$

</div>
</div>
