<div class='question subquestion'>

$9 \ \text{a.m.}$ and $10 \ \text{a.m.}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As we know the angle between every two units is equal.

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Angle between 9 a.m. and 10 a.m.}    &= \dfrac {90} {3} \\\\
                                            &= \dfrac {30 \times 3} {3} \\\\
                                            &= \dfrac {30 \times \cancel {3}} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
                                            &= 30 \degree
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$30 \degree$

</div>
