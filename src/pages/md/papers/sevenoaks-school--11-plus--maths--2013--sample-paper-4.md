As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
A + B     &= 22 \ \text{kg} \\\\
B + C     &= 20 \ \text{kg} \\\\
A + B + C &= 30 \ \text{kg}
\end{aligned}
$

Lets, first find value of A

$
\begin{aligned}
A + (B + C)  &= 30 \\\\
A + 20       &= 30 \\\\
A            &= 30 - 20 \\\\
A            &= 10
\end{aligned}
$

Lets use the value of A to find B,

$
\begin{aligned}
A + B  &= 22 \\\\
B      &= 22 - A \\\\
B      &= 22 - 10 \\\\
B      &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

Lets use the value of B to find C,

$
\begin{aligned}
B + C &= 20 \\\\
C     &= 20 - B \\\\
C     &= 20 - 11 \\\\
C     &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

Albert $=10$

Tim, David and Alison are given some money by their grandfather. Tim gets twice as much as David and three times as much as Alison. If they get $\pounds 49.50$ in total, how much do they each get?

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money Tim received $= a$

Money David recieved $= \dfrac{a}{2}$

Money Alison recieved $= \dfrac{a}{3}$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + \dfrac {a} {2} + \dfrac {a} {3}                                                                     &= 49.50 \\\\
\dfrac {6 \times a} {1 \times 6} + \dfrac {a \times 3} {2 \times 3} + \dfrac {a \times 2} {3 \times 2}  &= 49.50 \\\\
\dfrac {6a} {6} + \dfrac {3a} {6} + \dfrac {2a} {6}                                                     &= 49.50 \\\\
\dfrac {6a + 3a + 2a} {6}                                                                               &= 49.50 \\\\
11a                                                                                                     &= 49.50 \times 6 \\\\
a                                                                                                       &= \dfrac {297} {11} \\\\
a                                                                                                       &= \dfrac {27 \times 11} {11} \\\\
a                                                                                                       &= \dfrac {27 \times \cancel {11}} {\cancel {11}} \\\\
a                                                                                                       &= \pounds 27
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Money Tim received}       &= \pounds 27 \\\\
\text{Money David recieved}     &= \dfrac {27}{2} \\\\ 
                                &= \pounds 13.5 \\\\
\text{Money Alison recieved}    &= \dfrac {27}{3} \\\\ 
                                &= \pounds 9
\end{aligned}

As per the question,

$

2a + 10                                                 &= \dfrac {3} {2} \times (a + 10) \\\\
2 \times (2a + 10)                                      &= 3 \times (a + 10) \\\\
4a + 20                                                 &= 3a + 30 \\\\
4a - 3a                                                 &= 30  - 20 \\\\
a                                                       &= 10 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text{Jack's age in } 20 \text{ years}                  &= 2a + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 2 \times 10 + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 40 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text{Jill's age in } 20 \text{ years}                  &= a + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 10 + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 30 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
