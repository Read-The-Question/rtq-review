<div class='question question'>

How much will $46$ lollipops cost if they cost $\pounds 1.25$ each?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Price of 46 lollipops}    &= 46 \times \text{Price of 1 lollipop} \\\\
                                &= 46 \times 1.25 \\\\
                                &= \pounds 57.5
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{number of lollipops}                  &:& \text{price of lollipops} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \pounds 1.25 \\\\
= & 46                                          &:& 1.25 \times 46 \\\\
= & 46                                          &:& \pounds 57.5
\end{array}
$

<div class='question question'>

One quarter of the pupils in a class are girls. The rest are boys. What is the ratio of girls to boys?

-  $1:1$
-  $1:2$
-  $1:3$ 
-  $1:4$ 
-  $1:5$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{number of girls}                      &:& \text{number of boys} \\\\
= & \dfrac {1} {4}                              &:& \left( 1 - \dfrac {1} {4} \right) \\\\
= & \dfrac {1} {4}                              &:& \left( \dfrac {4} {4} - \dfrac {1} {4} \right) \\\\
= & \dfrac {1} {4}                              &:& \dfrac {4 - 1} {4} \\\\
= & \dfrac{1} {4}                               &:& \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \dfrac {3 \times 4} {4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \dfrac {3 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& 3
\end{array}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>

<p>C</p>

$1:3$

</div>

                                      &= 6 \times 1000 \ \text{g} \\\\

\end{aligned}

                          &= 1500 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}

= & 1 \ \text{month}                   &:& \pounds 16 \\\\
= & 12                                 &:& 16 \times 12 \\\\
= & 12 \ \text{months}                  &:& \pounds 192 \\\\
\end{array}
$

</div>
