<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

Note: **NOT** selecting blue is same as selecting any of the other colours instead.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Not blue}     &= \bigg(\dfrac {1} {4} + \dfrac {1} {4} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \bigg(\dfrac {1 \times 2} {4 \times 2} + \dfrac {1 \times 2} {4 \times 2} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \bigg(\dfrac {2} {8} + \dfrac {2} {8} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5} {8} \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times 32} {8} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times 8 \times 4} {8} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel{8} \times 4} {\cancel{8}} \\\\
                    &= 5 \times 4 \\\\
                    &= 20
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>

<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

Let the total number of people $=a$

Number of people that liked scarecrow $=\dfrac{a}{8}$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {4} {8} \ \text {of} \ a                 &= 24 \\\\
\dfrac {4} {8} \times a                         &= 24 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {24 \times 8} {4} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {24 \times 4 \times 2} {4} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {24 \times \cancel{4} \times 2} {\cancel{4}} \\\\
a                                               &= 24 \times  2 \\\\
a                                               &= 48
\end{aligned}
$

Total number of people $= 48$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{People that liked scarecrow}              &= \dfrac{a}{8} \\\\
                                                &= \dfrac{48}{8} \\\\
                                                &= \dfrac {6 \times 8} {8} \\\\
                                                &= \dfrac {6 \times \cancel{8}} {\cancel{8}} \\\\
                                                &= 6 \\\\
\text {Difference}                              &= 24 - 6 \\\\
                                                &= 18
\end{aligned}
$
