What is the average of $\dfrac {1}{2}$ and $\dfrac {1}{3}$ ?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= (\dfrac{1} {2} + \dfrac {1} {3}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac{3} {2 \times 3} + \dfrac {2} {3 \times 2}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac{3} {6} + \dfrac {2} {6}) \div 2 \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6} \div {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
