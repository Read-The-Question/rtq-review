<div class='question question'>

There are $329$ days until my birthday. How many weeks is this? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Weeks until birthday} &= 329 \ \text{days} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {329} {7} \ \text {Weeks}\\\\
                            &= \dfrac {7 \times 47 } {7} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel 7 \times 47 } {\cancel 7} \\\\
                            &=47 \ \text {Weeks}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$47 \ \text {Weeks}$
