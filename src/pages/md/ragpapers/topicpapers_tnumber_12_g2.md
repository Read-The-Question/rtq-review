Calculate $2.4 \times (3.7 +  1.3) \div 2$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2.4 \times (3.7 +  1.3) \div 2 \\\\
&= 2.4 \times 5 \div 2 \\\\
&= \dfrac{2.4 \times 5}{2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{2 \times 1.2 \times 5}{2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{\cancel {2} \times 1.2 \times 5}{\cancel {2}} \\\\  
&= 1.2 \times 5 \\\\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
