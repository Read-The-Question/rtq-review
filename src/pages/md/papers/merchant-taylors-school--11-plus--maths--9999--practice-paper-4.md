</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Angles on a straight line add up to} = 180 \degree$


ABBR: $\text{Angles around a point add up to} = 360 \degree$


As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
p + 48  &= 180 \\\\
p       &= 180 - 48 \\\\
p       &= 132 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

As it is an isosceles triangle, angle $r= q$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
q + 48 + r  &= 180 \\\\
q + q       &= 180 - 48 \\\\
2 q         &= 132 \\\\
q           &= \dfrac {132} {2} \\\\
q           &= \dfrac {66 \times 2} {2} \\\\
q           &= \dfrac {66 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
q           &= 66 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

$r = q = 66 \degree$

As $r$ and $t$ are opposite angles,

$t = r = 66 \degree$

Similarly,

$u$ will be equal to $s$

As we know, the sum of a complete angle is $360 \degree$. So,

$
\begin{aligned}
u + r + s + t    &= 360 \\\\
s + 66 + s + 66  &= 360 \\\\
2 s + 132        &= 360 \\\\
2 s              &= 360 - 132 \\\\
2 s              &= 228 \\\\
s                &= \dfrac {228} {2} \\\\
s                &= \dfrac {114 \times 2} {2} \\\\
s                &= \dfrac {114 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
s                &= 114 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

$s = u = 114 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

p $=$ $132 \degree$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

q $=$ $66 \degree$

r $=$ $66 \degree$

<div class='answer'>

s $=$ $114 \degree$

t $=$ $66 \degree$
