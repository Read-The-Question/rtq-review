**Question**

I need exactly $1 \ \text{litre}$ of water.

I have a measuring jug that holds $400 \ \text{ml}$ when it is full.

Explain how I can use my measuring jug to obtain $1 \ \text{litre}$ of water.

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Two full jugs} &= 400 + 400 \\\\
                     &= 800 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{One half jug} &= \dfrac {400} {2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 200} {2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 200} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
                    &= 200 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total} &= 800 + 200 \\\\
             &= 1000 \ \text{ml} \\\\
             &= \dfrac {1000} {1000} \ \text{litre} \\\\
             &= \dfrac {\cancel {1000}} {\cancel {1000}} \\\\
             &= 1 \ \text{litre}
\end{aligned}
$
</div>

\text{First number}                             &= 0 \\\\
\text{Last number}                              &= 39 \\\\

\end{aligned}

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {\text{Total numbers}} {2} \times (\text{First term + Last term}) \\\\

&= \dfrac {2 \times 20} {2} \times 39 \\\\

&= 780
\end{aligned}
