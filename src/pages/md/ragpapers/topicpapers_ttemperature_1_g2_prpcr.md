<div class='working'>

$

                                            &= 6 \times 1.5 \\\\
                                            &= 9 \degree \text{C} \\\\
\text{Temperature at} \ 18{:}00 \ \text{am} &= 11 - 9 \\\\
                                            &= 2 \degree \text{C}                       
\end{aligned}
$

$2 \degree \text{C}$
