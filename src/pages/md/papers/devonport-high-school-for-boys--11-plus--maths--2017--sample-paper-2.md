There is $5.5\ \text{g}$ of sugar in every $10\ \text{g}$ of jam. How much sugar is in $600\ \text{g}$ of jam?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the amount of sugar $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
10 \ \text{grams} : 5.5 \ \text{grams}          &= 600 \ \text{grams} : a \ \text{grams} \\\\
\dfrac {10} {5.5}                               &= \dfrac {600} {a} \\\\
10 \times a                                     &= 600 \times 5.5 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {600 \times 5.5} {10} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {60 \cancel 0 \times 5.5} {1 \cancel 0} \\\\
a                                               &= 60 \times 5.5 \\\\
a                                               &= 330 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

Sugar $= 330 \ \text{g}$

</div>

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{jam}                              &:& \text{sugar} \\\\
= & 10 \ \text{g}                           &:& 5.5 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & 10 \times 60                            &:& 5.5 \times 60 \\\\
= & 600 \ \text{g}                          &:& 330 \ \text{g}
\end{array}
