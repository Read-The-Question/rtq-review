<div class='question question'>

A drink contains lime, orange and apple juices in the ratio $2:7:6$. Find the volume of orange juice contained in $300 \ \text{ml}$ of the drink.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the volume of orange juice $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
(2 + 7 + 6) \ \text{ml} : 7 \ \text{ml}         &= 300 \ \text{ml} : a \ \text{ml} \\\\
\dfrac {2 + 7 + 6} {7}                          &= \dfrac {300} {a} \\\\
\dfrac {15} {7}                                 &= \dfrac {300} {a} \\\\
15 \times a                                     &= 300 \times 7 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {300 \times 7} {15} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {20 \times 15 \times 7} {15} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {20 \times \cancel {15} \times 7} {\cancel {15}} \\\\
a                                               &= 20 \times 7 \\\\
a                                               &= 140 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

Orange juice $= 140 \ \text{ml}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{drink}              &:& \text{orange juice} \\\\
= & (2 + 7 + 6) \ \text{ml}   &:& 7 \ \text{ml} \\\\
= & 15                        &:& 7 \\\\
= & 15 \times 20              &:& 7 \times 20 \\\\
= & 300 \ \text{ml}           &:& 140 \ \text{ml}
\end{array}
$

Orange juice $= 140 \ \text{ml}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$140 \text { ml}$

</div>
</div>

How much water should be mixed with $30 \ \text{ml}$ of cordial?

<div class='working'>

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
1 \ \text{ml} : 4 \ \text{ml}                   &= 30 \ \text{ml} : a \ \text{ml} \\\\
\dfrac {1} {4}                                  &= \dfrac {30} {a} \\\\
1 \times a                                      &= 30 \times 4 \\\\
a                                               &= 120 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

Amount of water $= 120 \ \text{ml}$

= & 1 \times 30                             &:& 4 \times 30 \\\\

How much cordial should be used to make $750 \ \text{ml}$ of the drink?

<div class='working'>

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
1 \ \text{ml} : 4 \ \text{ml}                   &= a \ \text{ml} : (750 - a)) \ \text{ml} \\\\
\dfrac {1} {4}                                  &= \dfrac {a} {750 - a} \\\\
750 - a                                         &= 4a \\\\
750                                             &= 4a + a \\\\
4a + a                                          &= 750 \\\\
5a                                              &= 750 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {750} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5 \times 150} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 150} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                                               &= 150 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

Amount of water $= 150 \ \text{ml}$

<div class='question question'>

In a fruit yoghurt weighing $117 \ \text{g}$, the ratio of weight of fruit to weight of yoghurt is $2:7$. Calculate the weight of fruit.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the weight of fruit $= a$

Weight of yoghurt $= 117 - a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \ \text{g} : 7 \ \text{g}                     &= a \ \text{g} : (117 - a) \ \text{g} \\\\
\dfrac {2} {7}                                  &= \dfrac {a} {117 - a} \\\\
2 \times (117 - a)                              &= 7a \\\\
2 \times 117 - 2a                               &= 7a \\\\
234 - 2a                                        &= 7a \\\\
234                                             &= 7a + 2a \\\\
2a + 7a                                         &= 234 \\\\
9a                                              &= 234 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {234} {9} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {9 \times 26} {9} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 9 \times 26} {\cancel 9} \\\\
a                                               &= 26 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

Weight of fruit $= 26 \ \text{g}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{fruit yoghurt}                        &:& \text{fruit} \\\\
= & (2 + 7) \ \text{g}                          &:& 2 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & 9                                           &:& 2 \\\\
= & 9 \times 13                                 &:& 2 \times 13 \\\\
= & 117 \ \text{g}                              &:& 26 \ \text{g}
\end{array}
$

Weight of fruit $= 26 \ \text{g}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$26 \ \text{g}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

Jason and Patrick win a prize of $\pounds 75$ for their entry into an inventing competition. For every $2$ hours that Patrick worked on the project Jason worked $3$, so it is decided that for every $\pounds 2$ that Patrick receives Jason should receive $\pounds 3$. How much will Jason and Patrick receive? Write your 
answers in the boxes below. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money received by Jason $= a$

Money received by Patrick $= 75 - a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
\pounds 2 : \pounds 3          &= \pounds (75 - a) : \pounds a \\\\
\dfrac {2} {3}                 &= \dfrac {75 - a} {a} \\\\
2a                             &= 3 \times (75 - a) \\\\
2a                             &= 3 \times 75 - 3a \\\\
2a                             &= 225 - 3a \\\\
2a + 3a                        &= 225 \\\\
5a                             &= 225 \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {225} {5} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {5 \times 45} {5} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 45} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                              &= \pounds 45
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Money received by Jason}        &= \pounds 45 \\\\
\text{Money received by Patrick}      &= 75 - 45 \\\\
                                      &= \pounds 30
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \pounds                     &:& \pounds                     &:& \pounds \\\\
= & 2 + 3                       &:& 2                           &:& 3 \\\\
= & 5                           &:& 2                           &:& 3 \\\\
= & 5 \times 15                 &:& 2 \times 15                 &:& 3 \times 15 \\\\
= & \pounds 75                  &:& \pounds 30                  &:& \pounds 45
\end{array}
$

Money received by Patrick $= \pounds 30$

Money received by Jason $= \pounds 45$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 45, \pounds 30$

</div>
