<div class='question question'>

Calculate the total length marked with $x$.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Length}(x)         &= \text{Length}(\text{Lower figure}) + \dfrac{1}{3} \times \text{Length}(\text{Upper figure}) \\\\
x                        &= 15 + \dfrac{1}{3} \times 15  \\\\
x                        &= 15 + \dfrac {5 \times 3} {3}  \\\\
x                        &= 15 + \dfrac {5 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3}}  \\\\
x                        &= 15 + 5  \\\\
x                        &= 20 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

The total length of marked $x$ is $20 \ \text {cm}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$20 \ \text {cm}$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

Find the height of the cuboid.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Volume of cuboid $=$ length $\times$ width $\times$ height.

Let the height of the cuboid $= h$.

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Volume of cuboid}  &= \text {Volume of cube} \\\\
10 \times 5 \times h      &= 125 \ \text {cm} ^ 3 \\\
h                         &= \dfrac {125} {10 \times 5} \\\\
h                         &= \dfrac {5 \times 5 \times 5} {2 \times 5 \times 5} \\\\
h                         &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel {5} \times \cancel {5}} {2 \times \cancel {5} \times \cancel {5}} \\\\
h                         &= \dfrac {5} {2} \\\\
h                         &= 2.5 \ \text {cm}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$2.5 \ \text{cm}$
