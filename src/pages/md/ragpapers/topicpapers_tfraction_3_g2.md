&= \dfrac {9} {24} - \dfrac {1} {24} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9 - 1} {24} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8} {24} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8} {3 \times 8} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{8}} {3 \times \cancel{8}} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {3}$
