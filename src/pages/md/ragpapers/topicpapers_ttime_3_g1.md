<div class='working'>

&= \text{Friday} + 208 \ \text{weeks} + \dfrac {5} {7} \ \text{weeks} \\\\
&= \text{Friday} + 5 \ \text{days} \\\\
&=\text {Wednesday}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='answer'>

$\text {Wednesday}$
