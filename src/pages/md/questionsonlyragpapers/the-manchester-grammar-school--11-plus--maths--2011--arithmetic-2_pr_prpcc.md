$\text{Sum of angles} = 180 \degree \times \text{number of sides} - 360 \degree$

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text{a} + \text{b} + \text{c} + \text{d} + \text{e} &= 180 \degree \times 5 - 360 \degree \\
                  &= 900 \degree - 360 \degree \\
                  &= 540 \degree
\end{aligned}
$
