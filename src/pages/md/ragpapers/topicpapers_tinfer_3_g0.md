Every digit is an odd number.

None of the digits is a $9$. 

Every digit in the number is different.

The smallest digit is in the thousands place.

The greatest digit is in the ones place.

This describes two possible numbers. 

The mystery number is the greater of those two numbers.

What is the mystery number?
