<div class='question question'>

The diagram shows a design formed by drawing six lines in a regular hexagon. The lines divide each edge of the hexagon into three equal parts. What fraction of the hexagon is shaded?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {number of triangles in one hexagon} &= 6 \\\\
\text {total triangles}                    &= 6 \times 7 + 12 \\\\
                                           &= 42 + 12 \\\\
                                           &= 54 \\\\
\text {fraction of hexagon shaded}         &= \dfrac {12} {54} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {6 \times 9} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {2 \times \cancel{6}} {\cancel{6} \times 9} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {2} {9}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {2} {9}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

What fraction is bigger: two thirds or three quarters?
You must justify your answer clearly. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's make both of the denominator equal to compare.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {2} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 4} {3 \times 4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 3} {4 \times 3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

Since 9 greater than 8. $\dfrac {9} {12}$ is greater than $\dfrac {8} {12}$.

Therefore $\dfrac {3} {4}$ or three quarters is greater than $\dfrac {2} {3}$ or two thirds.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

three quarters

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

Work out three-sevenths of $91$

A: $42$

B: $39$

C: $36$

D: $26$

E: $13$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {3} {7} \times 91 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 91} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 13 \times 7} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 13 \times \cancel{7}} {\cancel{7}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 13 \\\\
&= 39
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>B</p>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

What number is halfway between $4\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $7\dfrac{1}{2}$ ?

A: $5\dfrac{1}{4}$

B: $5\dfrac{1}{2}$

C: $5\dfrac{3}{4}$

D:  6

E: $6\dfrac{1}{4}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(4 \dfrac {1} {2} + 7 \dfrac {1} {2} \bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(\dfrac {9} {2} + \dfrac {15} {2}\bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(\dfrac {9 + 15} {2}\bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(\dfrac {24} {2}\bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(\dfrac {2 \times 12} {2}\bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg(\dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 12} {\cancel{2}}\bigg) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 12 \\\\
&= \dfrac {12} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 6} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>D</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$6$

</div>
</div>
