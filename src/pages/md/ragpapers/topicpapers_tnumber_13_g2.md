<div class='question question'>

What number do you multiply $0.2$ by to get an answer of $6$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 0.2    &= 6 \\\\
a \times \dfrac{2}{10}    &= 6 \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {6 \times 10} {2} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {6 \times 2 \times 5} {2} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {6 \times \cancel {2} \times 5} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a               &= 6 \times 5 \\\\
a               &= 30
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$30$

</div>
</div>
