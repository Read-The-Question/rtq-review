I think of a number, divide it by $7$ and add $9$. The result is $17$. What was the number I first thought of?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \div 7 + 9      &= 17 \\\\
\dfrac {a} {7}    &= 17 - 9 \\\\
\dfrac {a} {7}    &= 8 \\\\
a                 &= 8 \times 7 \\\\
a                 &= 56
\end{aligned}
$

The number $= 56$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$56$

<div class='question subquestion'>

There is a number, *n*. When I add 3 to *n* then multiply the answer by 4, the overall result is the same as just multiplying *n* by 6. What is *n*?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
n \times 6  &= (n + 3) \times 4 \\\\
6n          &= (n + 3) \times 4 \\\\
6n          &= 4n + 12 \\\\
6n - 4n     &= 12 \\\\
2n          &= 12 \\\\
n           &= \dfrac {12} {2} \\\\
n           &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2} \\\\
n           &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 6} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
n           &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6$  

</div>
</div>
