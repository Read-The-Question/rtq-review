<div class='question question'>

A card is picked from a pack of ordinary 52 playing cards.
What is the probability of getting a king, queen or jack?

%format% - 3 13
%format% - 3 52
%format% - 12 13
%format% - 13 52

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Probability}     &= \dfrac {\text {Favourable outcomes}} {\text {Total possible outcomes}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {4 + 4 + 4} {52} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {12} {52} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 4} {4 \times 13} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {4} \times 13} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3} {13}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
