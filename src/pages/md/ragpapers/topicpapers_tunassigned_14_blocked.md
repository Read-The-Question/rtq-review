<div class='question question'>

The diagram shows Anthony the ant setting off for a walk in search of food. 
On this journey Anthony walked a certain distance, and turned right. 
Each time he turned right he walked $2 \ \text{metres}$ less than he did the time before.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


On the last part of his journey before stopping he walked one metre. How far did he walk altogether?

- $38$
- $14$
- $51$
- $45$
- $49$  

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

The given pattern follow an arthemetic progression 

$6$ matches increase $1$ on next one

$ a = 1 , n = 7 , d = 2 $

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The arthemetic progression} \\\\
a_n &= a + (n-1)d \\\\
&= 1 + (7-1) \times 2 \\\\
&= 1 + 6 \times 2 \\\\
&= 1 + 12 \\\\
&= 13 \\\\
\sum a_n &= \dfrac{n}{2} \times (a + b) \\\\
&= \dfrac{7}{2} \times (1 + 13) \\\\
&= \dfrac{7}{2} \times (14) \\\\
&= \dfrac{98}{2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{49 \times 2}{2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{49 \times \cancel 2}{ \cancel 2} \\\\
&= 49
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>E</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$49$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

The numbers $34$ and $55$ are two examples of two-digit numbers which are made up using the digits $3, 4$ and $5$. 
How many two-digit numbers in total can be made using some or all of the digits $3, 4$ and $5$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's find all two-digit numbers that can be made using the digits $3, 4$ and $5$.

The possible arrangements are:

- $33$
- $34$
- $35$
- $43$
- $44$
- $45$
- $53$
- $54$
- $55$

Count $= 9$

</div>
<div class='working'>

We can solve this problem using permutation instead of manually counting all the arrangements.

Count of digits that can be used in tens place        $= 3$

Count of digits that can be used in unit place        $= 3$

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text{Total Arrangements}                           &= 3 \times 3 \\\\\
                                                    &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$9$

</div>
