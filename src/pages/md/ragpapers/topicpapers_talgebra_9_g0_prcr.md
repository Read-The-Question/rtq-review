What values of $𝑎$ makes $𝑎 ⋆ 7 = 42$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
a \ \text{*} \ 7                                                   &= 42 \\\\
(a - 3) \times 7                                                   &= 42 \\\\
a - 3                                                              &= \dfrac {42} {7} \\\\
a - 3                                                              &= \dfrac {6 \times 7} {7} \\\\
a - 3                                                              &= \dfrac {6 \times \cancel{7}} {\cancel{7}} \\\\
a - 3                                                              &= 6 \\\\
a                                                                  &= 6 + 3 \\\\
a                                                                  &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

Find $𝑏$ if $𝑏 ⋆ 𝑏 = 28$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
b \ \text{*} \ b                                                   &= 28 \\\\
(b - 3) \times b                                                   &= 28 \\\\
b^{2} - 3b                                                         &= 28 \\\\
b^{2} - 3b - 28                                                    &= 0 \\\\
b^{2} - (7b - 4b) - 28                                             &= 0 \\\\
b^{2} - 7b + 4b - 28                                               &= 0 \\\\
b(b - 7) + 4(b - 7)                                                &= 0 \\\\
(b - 7) \times (b + 4)                                             &= 0 \\\\
b                                                                  &= 7 \\\\
b                                                                  &= -4 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
