<div class='question subquestion'>

The number of boys who fail the test is $15$.

How many boys pass the test?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of boys $= b$

As per the pie chart,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{1}{4} \times b &= 15 \\\\
b &= 15 \times 4 \\\\
b &= 60
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Boys who passed the test}     &= 60 - 15 \\\\
                                            &= 45
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$45$
