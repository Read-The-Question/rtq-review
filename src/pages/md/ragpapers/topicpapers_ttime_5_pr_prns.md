<div class='question question'>

The instructions below are used to work out the cooking time for a turkey.

$50 \ \text{minutes per kilogram}$ plus $20 \ \text{minutes}$

Claire wants to cook a turkey with a mass of $2.5 \ \text{kilograms}$.

For how long should Claire cook the turkey?

*Give your answer in hours and minutes.*

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {time she should cook} &= 2.5 \times 50 + 20 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                             &= 125 + 20 \\\\
                             &= 145 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                             &= 120 \ \text {minutes} + 25 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                             &= 2 \ \text {hours} \ 25 \ \text {minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$2 \ \text {hours} \ 25 \ \text {minutes}$

</div>

$01{:}12$ hours

                     &= 22{:}15
\end{aligned}
$
