<div class='question question'>

A man is $30$ years older than his youngest son. In $17$ years he will be twice his son's age. How old is the son?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let man's age $= a$

His son's  age $= a - 30$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 17              &= 2 \times (a - 30 + 17) \\\\
a + 17              &= 2 \times (a - 13) \\\\
a + 17              &= 2a - 26 \\\\
2a - 26             &= a + 17 \\\\
2a - a              &= 17 + 26 \\\\
a                   &= 43 \\\\
\text {Son's age}   &= a - 30 \\\\
                    &= 43 - 30 \\\\
                    &= 13 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
