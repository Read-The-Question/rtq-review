Find a perfect number which is less than $10$.

</div>

                                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 6} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
                                        &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

$6$
