Calculate the area of $ABCD$. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Height of triangle ABC}   &= 5 - 3 \\\\
                                &= 2 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\text{Base of triangle ABC}     &= 6 - 0 \\\\
                                &= 6 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\text{Area of triangle ABC}     &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \text{Height of triangle ABC} \times \text{Base of triangle ABC} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 2 \times 6 \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel 2 \times 6} {\cancel 2} \\\\
                                &= 6 \ {\text{cm}} ^ 2 \\\\
\text{Area of kite ABCD}        &= 2 \times \text{Area of triangle ABC} \\\\
                                &= 2 \times 6 \\\\
                                &= 12 \ {\text{cm}} ^ 2
\end{aligned}
$
