How many kroner would I get for $90 \text{p}$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
= & \pounds 1                      &:& 18 \ \text{kroner} \\\\
= & 1 \times 100 \text{p}          &:& 18 \ \text{kroner} \\\\
= & 100 \text{p}                   &:& 18 \ \text{kroner} \\\\
= & 1                              &:& \dfrac {18} {100} \\\\
= & 90                             &:& \dfrac {18 \times 90} {100} \\\\
= & 90                             &:& \dfrac {1620} {100} \\\\
= & 90 \text{p}                    &:& 16.2 \ \text{kroner}
\end{array}
$

</div>
