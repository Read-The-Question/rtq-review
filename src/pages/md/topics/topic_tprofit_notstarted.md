**Working**

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {New price}       &= \text {Original cost} - \text {Sale Discount} \\\\
                        &= \pounds 125 - 20 \% \ \text {of} \ \pounds 125 \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20} {100} \times 125 \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20 \times 125} {100} \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20 \times 5 \times 25} {20 \times 5} \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {\cancel {20} \times \cancel {5} \times 25} {\cancel {20} \times \cancel {5}} \\\\
                        &= 125 - 25 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 100
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$\pounds 100$
