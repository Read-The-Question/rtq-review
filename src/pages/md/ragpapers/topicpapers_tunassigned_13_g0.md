Find $35 \%$ of $160$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 35 \% \ \text{of} \ 160  \\\\
&= \dfrac {35} {100} \times 160 \\\\
&= \dfrac {35 \times 160} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5600} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {56 \times 100} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {56 \times \cancel{100}} {\cancel{100}} \\\\
&= {56} \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
