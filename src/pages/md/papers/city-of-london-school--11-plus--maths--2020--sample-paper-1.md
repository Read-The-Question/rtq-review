<div class='question question'>

For this question you can do rough working on the left hand side of the page if required. 

Put answers only in the spaces on the right.

$2.74 \times 10,000 = \ldots $ 

$13^2 = \ldots $

The square root of $49$ is $ \ldots $

The cube root of $125$ is $ \ldots $

$4^3 = \ldots $ 

$\dfrac{240}{300}$ in its simplest form is $ \ldots $

$25\%$ of $68 = \ldots $ 

$43 \times 38 = \ldots $ 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
2.74 \times 10000           &= 27400 \\\\
13^2                        &= 13 \times 13 \\\\
                            &= 169 \\\\
\text{Square root of} \ 49  &= \sqrt {49} \\\\
                            &= \sqrt {7 \times 7} \\\\
                            &= 7 \\\\
\text{Cube root of} \ 125   &= \sqrt [3] {125} \\\\
                            &= \sqrt [3] {5 \times 5 \times 5} \\\\
                            &= 5 \\\\
4^3                         &= 4 \times 4 \times 4 \\\\
                            &= 64 \\\\
\dfrac {240} {300}      &= \dfrac {24 \cancel {0} } {30\cancel {0} } \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {24} {3 \times 10} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {8 \times \cancel {3} } {\cancel {3} \times 10} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {8} {10} \\\\
                        &= 0.8 \\\\
25\% \ \text{of} \ 68   &= \dfrac {25} {100} \times 68 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {25} {4 \times 25} \times 68 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel{25}} {4 \times \cancel{25}} \times 68 \\\\
                        &= 4 \times 68 \\\\
                        &= 272 \\\\
43 \times 38            &= 1634
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

I buy $7$ drinks at $72\text{p}$ each, and $9$ sandwiches at $\pounds 1.21$ each. 

How much change do I get from a $\pounds 20$ note? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required.


$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Money spent}         &= 7 \times 72 \text{p} +  9 \times \pounds 1.21 \\\\
                            &= 7 \times \pounds 0.72 + 9 \times \pounds 1.21 \\\\
                            &= 5.04 + 10.89 \\\\
                            &= \pounds 15.93 \\\\
\text {Change received}     &= 20 - 15.93 \\\\
                            &= \pounds 4.07
\end {aligned}
$

</div>

Martin is given $\pounds 240$. He gives $\dfrac{1}{8}$ to his sister and $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of what remains to his little brother. How much does he keep himself?

                     &= 30 + 42 \\\\
                     &= \pounds 72 \\\\
\text {Amount left}  &= \text {Total amount} - \text {Amount spent} \\\\
