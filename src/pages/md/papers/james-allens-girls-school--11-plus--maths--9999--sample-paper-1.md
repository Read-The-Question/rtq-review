Pauline has $90 \ \text{p}$ that she wants to share between $7$ girls and $4$ boys so that the girls each have twice as much as the boys. 
How much does each girl and each boy receive?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let each boy gets (in pence) $= a$

Each girl gets (in pence) $= 2a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
7 \times 2a + 4a        &= 90 \\\\
14a + 4a                &= 90 \\\\
18a                     &= 90 \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {90} {18} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {18 \times 5} {18} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {\cancel {18} \times 5} {\cancel {18}} \\\\
a                       &= 5
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Each boy gets}  &= 5\ \text{p} \\\\
\text{Each girl gets} &= 2 \times 5 \\\\
                      &= 10\ \text{p}
\end{aligned}
$
