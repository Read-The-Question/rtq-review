<div class='question question'>

Work out the sum of: $9, 99, 999$ and $9999$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
          &     &     &     &  9 \\
          &     &     &  9  &  9 \\
          &     &  9  &  9  &  9 \\
{}+       &  9  &  9  &  9  &  9 \\
\hline
       1  &  1  &  1  &  0  &  6
\end{array}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

 Alternative Solution 

$
\begin{aligned}
9 + 99 + 999 + 9999     &= 10 - 1 + 100 - 1 + 1000 - 1 + 10000 - 1 \\\\
                        &= 10 + 100 + 1000 + 10000 - 4 \\\\
                        &= 11110 - 4 \\\\
                        &= 11106 
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$11106$

</div>
