<div class='question question'>

After a card game, four gamblers had $\pounds 234$ between them. Harry had $\pounds 20$ more than Dana, 
Charlotte had $\pounds 53$ more than Dana and Sid had $\pounds 71$ more than Dana.

How much did each of them have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money Dana had (in $\pounds$) $= a$

Money Harry had $= a + 20$

Money Charlotte had $= a + 53$

Money Sid had $= a + 71$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + 20 + a + 53 + a + 71  &= \pounds 234 \\\\
4a + 144                      &= 234 \\\\
4a                            &= 234 - 144 \\\\
4a                            &= 90 \\\\
a                             &= \dfrac {90} {4} \\\\
a                             &= \pounds 22.50
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Money Dana had}        &= \pounds 22.50 \\\\
\text {Money Harry had}       &= 22.50 + 20 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 42.50 \\\\
\text {Money Charlotte had}   &= 22.50 + 53 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 75.50 \\\\
\text {Money Sid had}         &= 22.50 + 71 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 93.50
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Dana $=$ $\pounds 22.50$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Harry $=$ $\pounds 42.50$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Charlotte $=$ $\pounds 75.50$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Sid $=$ $\pounds 93.50$

</div>
