<div class='question question'>

A girl is $5\ \text{years}$ older than her brother. If the sum of their ages is $17$, how old are they?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let her brother's age $= a$

Girl's age $= a + 5$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + a + 5   &= 17 \\\\
2a + 5      &= 17 \\\\
2a          &= 17 - 5 \\\\
2a          &= 12 \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {12} {2} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 6} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a           &= 6 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Brother's age}   &= a \\\\
                        &= 6 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Girl's age}      &= a + 5 \\\\
                        &= 6 + 5 \\\\
                        &= 11 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6 \ \text {years}$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

$11 \ \text {years}$

</div>
