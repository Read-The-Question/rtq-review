As per the question

$
\begin{aligned}
4a   &= 70 + 30 + 70 + 30 \\\\
4a   &= 200 \\\\
a    &= \dfrac {200} {4} \\\\
a    &= \dfrac {4 \times 50} {4} \\\\
a    &= \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 50} { \cancel {4} } \\\\
a    &= 50 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Now lets find the area of two 

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area of rectangle}  &= 70 \times 30 \\\\
                          &= 2100 \text{ cm} \\\\

\text{Area of square}     &= a \times a \\\\
                          &= 50 \times 50 \\\\
                          &= 2500 \text{ cm}^2. 
\end{aligned}
$

The difference between areas

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2500 - 2100 \\\\
&= 400 \text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$
