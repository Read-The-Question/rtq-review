<div class='question question'>

If $750 \ \text{grams}$ of pasta cost $90 \ \text{p}$ find the cost of $1 \ \text{kilogram}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required.


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of 1 gram pasta}         &= \dfrac {90} {750} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {9 \cancel 0} {75 \cancel 0} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {9} {75} \ \text{p} \\\\
\text{Cost of 1000 gram pasta}      &= \dfrac {9} {75} \times 1000 \\\\
\text{Cost of 1 kilogram pasta}     &= \dfrac {9 \times 1000} {75} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {3 \times 3 \times 25 \times 40} {25 \times 3} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel 3 \times \cancel {25} \times 40} {\cancel {25} \times \cancel 3} \\\\
                                    &= 3 \times 40 \\\\
                                    &= 120 \ \text{p} \\\\
                                    &= \pounds 1.20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Let price of pasta $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
750 \ \text{grams} : 90 \ \text{pence}          &= 1000 \ \text{grams} : a \ \text{pence} \\\\
\dfrac{750} {90}                                &= \dfrac {1000} {a} \\\\
750 \times a                                    &= 90 \times 1000 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {90 \times 1000} {750} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {3 \times 30 \times 250 \times 4} {3 \times 250} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 30 \times \cancel {250} \times 4} {\cancel 3 \times \cancel {250}} \\\\
a                                               &= 30 \times 4 \\\\
a                                               &= 120 \ \text{pence} \\\\
a                                               &= \pounds 1.20
\end{aligned}
$

Price of $1 \ \text{kilogram}$ of pasta $= 120 \ \text{p}$

</div>

\end{aligned}
