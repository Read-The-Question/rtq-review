<div class='question subquestion'>

These rules show how to get from one number to the next in these sequences.

Use the rules to write the next **two** numbers in each sequence.

**Rule: Add $8$**

$
4 \quad
12 \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
$

**Rule: Multiply by $3$**

$
4 \quad
12 \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
$

**Rule: Divide by $4$ then add $11$**

$
4 \quad
12 \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
\text{\textunderscore \textunderscore \textunderscore} \quad
$


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question, the sequence would be:

$
\begin{matrix}
&   \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(+8)}}
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(\times 3)}}
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(\div 4)}}
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(+ 11)}}  \\
4,  &&    12,  &&  \ldots,  &&   \ldots  && \ldots
\end{matrix}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{First missing number}   &= 12 \times 3 \\\\
                              &= 36 \\\\
\text {Second missing number} &= 36 \div 4 + 11 \\\\
                              &= 9 + 11 \\\\
                              &= 20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$36$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

$20$

</div>
