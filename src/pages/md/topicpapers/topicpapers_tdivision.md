<div class='question question'>

What is $6 \div 0.3$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 6 \div 0.3 \\\\
&= 6 \div \dfrac {3} {10} \\\\
&= 6 \times \dfrac {10} {3} \\\\
&= 6 \times \dfrac {10} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {6 \times 10} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 2 \times 10} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 2 \times 10} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
&= 20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$20$

Divide $3$ by $0.5$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3 \div 0.5 \\\\
&= 3 \div \dfrac {5} {10} \\\\
&= 3 \times \dfrac {10} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 10} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 2 \times 5} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 2 \times \cancel {5}} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 2 \\\\
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
