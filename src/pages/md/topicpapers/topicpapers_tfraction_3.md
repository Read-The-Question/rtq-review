Which fraction is bigger, four fifths or eight ninths?

&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8} {9} \\\\

\end{aligned}

Calculate two thirds of five and a quarter.

</div>

&= \dfrac{7} {2} \\\\
&= 3 \dfrac {1} {2}
\end{aligned}
$

Four cakes are divided equally amongst seven people. One fifth of a cake is left over. What fraction of a cake does each person get?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Each person share}    &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( 4 - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{4 \times 5}{5}  - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{20}{5}  - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{20 - 1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times  \dfrac{19}{5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{19}{7 \times 5}  \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{19}{35}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac{19}{35}$

</div>
