**Question**

The mean (average) of a set of six numbers is $10$. The number $25$ is removed from the set.

Calculate the mean of the remaining numbers. 

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">
<strong style="display: block; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Question Raw</strong>
</div>
<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex">The mean (average) of a set of six numbers is $10$. The number $25$ is removed from the set.
<emptyline>
Calculate the mean of the remaining numbers. 
</code></pre>
</div>
<div class="workings">
<div class="tips placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

%empty%
</div>
</div>
<div class="formulas" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Formulas**

<div class="formula">
- ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Sum of 6 numbers}               &= 10 \times 6 \\\\
                                      &= 60 \\\\
\text{Mean of the remaining numbers}  &= \dfrac{60 - 25}{5} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac{35}{5} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac{7 \times 5}{5} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac{7 \times \cancel{5}}{\cancel{5}} \\\\
                                      &= 7
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$7$

</div>

**Question**

The mean of four numbers $x, 4, 5$ and $y$  is $5$ and the range of the numbers is $7$.  Find $x$ and  $y$.

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">

**Working**

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{x + 4 + 5 + y}{4}        &= 5 \\\\
\dfrac{9 + x + y}{4}            &= 5 \\\\
\dfrac{9 + x + y}{4}            &= 5 \\\\
9 + x + y                       &= 5 \times 4 \\\\
9 + x + y                       &= 20 \\\\
x + y                           &= 20 -  9 \\\\
x + y                           &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

Lets find out the value of $x$ and $y$ which satisfies the condition:

- The range is $7$
- $x + y = 11$

| x |       y      |         Range      |
|:-:|:------------:|:------------------:|
|$1$|  $11-1 = 10$ |       10 - 1 = 9   |
|$2$|  $11-2 = 9$  |       9 - 2 = 7    |
|$3$|  $11-3 = 8$  |       8 - 3 = 5    |
|$4$|  $11-4 = 7$  |       7 - 4 = 3    |
|$5$|  $11-5 = 6$  |       6 - 4 = 2    |
|$6$|  $11-6 = 5$  |       6 - 4 = 2    |

A quick check shows that $2$ and $9$ meets the criteria above.

$x = 2$

$y = 9$
</div>
