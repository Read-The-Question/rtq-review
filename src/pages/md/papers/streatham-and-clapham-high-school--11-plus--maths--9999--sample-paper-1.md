<div class='working'>

 Let's find the difference between the consecutive terms of the sequence.

The difference between the terms is decreasing by $7$. Hence, the sequence would be

$
\begin{matrix}
&   \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}} 
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}}
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}}  
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}}   
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}}   
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC
{(-4)}}  \\

\end{matrix}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{First number }   &= -3 + -4 \\\\
