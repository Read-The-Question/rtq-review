**Question**

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {2} {3} \ \text {of} \ 1 \ \text {bottle last for}    &= 1 \ \text {day} \\\\

                                                             &= \dfrac {2 \times 3 \times 3} {2} \\\\
                                                             &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 3 \times 3} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
                                                             &= 3 \times 3 \\\\
                                                             &= 9 \ \text {days}
\end{aligned}
$

$6$ bottles will last for $= 9$ days.

7y - 3y                                         &= 72 \\\\

y                                               &= \dfrac {72} {4} \\\\
y                                               &= \dfrac {4 \times 18} {4} \\\\
y                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 18} {\cancel 4} \\\\
y                                               &= 18
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of y to find x.

$
\begin{aligned}
x           &= 24 + y \\\\
            &= 24 + 18 \\\\
            &= 42
\end{aligned}
$
