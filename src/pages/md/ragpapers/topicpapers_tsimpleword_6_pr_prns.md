<div class='question question'>

If $9$ bananas cost $\pounds 1.23$ find the cost of $12$ bananas.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{cost}   &=   12 \times \dfrac{\pounds \ 1.23}{9} \\\\       
&=   12 \times \dfrac{123 \ \text{p}}{9} \\\\   
&=   \dfrac{3 \times 4 \times 3 \times 41}{3 \times 3}  \\\\   
&=   \dfrac{\cancel 3 \times 4 \times \cancel 3 \times 41}{\cancel 3 \times \cancel 3}  \\\\   
&= 164 \ \text{p}       \\\\
&= \pounds \ 1.64
\end{aligned}
$
