<div class='question question'>

Estimate the angle drawn below and then also measure it accurately with your proctractor.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Angles around a point add up to} = 360 \degree$


Let the angle $= a$

The reflex angle is approximately $255 \degree$.

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 255     &= 360 \\\\
a           &= 360 - 255 \\\\
a           &= 105 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Estimate angle $= 105 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$105 \degree$
