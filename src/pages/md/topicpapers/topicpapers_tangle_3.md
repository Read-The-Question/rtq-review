<div class='question question'>

One angle of an isosceles triangle is $80 \degree$. 

What are the other angles? 

There are two possible solutions to this question.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


This can be solved two ways.

**OPTION 1**

Let the two equal angles of the isosceles triangle $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a  + a + 80                             &= 180 \\\\
2a + 80                                 &= 180 \\\\
2a                                      &= 180 - 80 \\\\
2a                                      &= 100 \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {100} {2} \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {50 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {50 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a                                       &= 50 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Second angle $= 50 \degree$

Third angle $= 50 \degree$

**OPTION 2**

Let the third angle $= b$

Let the two equal angles of the isosceles triangle $= 80 \degree$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
b  + 80 + 80                            &= 180 \\\\
b + 160                                 &= 180 \\\\
b                                       &= 180 - 160 \\\\
b                                       &= 20 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Second angle $= 20 \degree$

Third angle $= 80 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$50 \degree, 50 \degree$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

$20 \degree, 80 \degree$

</div>
