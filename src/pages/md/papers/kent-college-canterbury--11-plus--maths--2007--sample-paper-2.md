$7\times b - 3 = 11$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
7 \times b - 3  &= 11 \\\\
7b              &= 11 + 3 \\\\
7b              &= 14 \\\\
b               &= \dfrac {14} {7} \\\\
b               &= \dfrac {2 \times 7} {7} \\\\
b               &= \dfrac {2 \times \cancel {7}} {\cancel {7}} \\\\
b               &= 2
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
