<div class='question subquestion'>

The sum of two consecutive whole numbers is $91$. 

What is the larger of these two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Second number $= a + 1$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + 1               &= 91 \\\\
2 a + 1                 &= 91 \\\\
2 a                     &= 91 - 1 \\\\
2 a                     &= 90 \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {90} {2} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {45 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {45 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a                       &= 45 \\\\
\text {Larger number}   &= a + 1 \\\\
                        &= 45 + 1 \\\\
                        &= 46
\end{aligned}
$ 

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$46$
