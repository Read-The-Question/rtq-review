If it is $-26.5 \degree \text{C}$ in Canada and $34.5 \degree \text{C}$ in Australia, what is the difference in temperature?

                                 &= 61 \degree \text{C}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

$61 \degree \text{C}$

<div class='question question'>

On January $1^\text{st}$, the temperature in Helsinki was -$4 \degree \text{C}$ and the temperature in Madrid was $12 \degree \text{C}$. The temperature in London was exactly half way between Helsinki and Madrid. What was the temperature in London?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Temperature (in London)}                      &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times (\text{Temperature (Helsinki + Madrid)}) \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {-4 \degree \text{C} + 12 \degree \text{C}} {2} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {8} {2} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 4} {2} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 4} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
                                                    &= 4 \degree \text{C}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4 \degree \text{C}$

</div>

                                                                    &= 19 \degree \text{C}
\end{aligned}
$

Rome is warmer than Moscow by $= 19 \degree \text{C}$

</div>
