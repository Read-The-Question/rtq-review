<div class='question question'>

Calculate $4000 \div 100$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 4000 \div 100 \\\\
&= \dfrac{4000}{100} \\\\
&= \dfrac{40 \cancel {00}} {1 \cancel {00}}  \\\\
&= 40
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$
