What time is it in London when it is $1.15 \ \text{p.m.}$ in Sri Lanka?

</div>

<div class='working'>

\begin{aligned}
\text{Time in London}        &= 1.15 - 4 \dfrac {1} {2} \ \text {hours} \\\\
                             &= 1.15 - \dfrac {9} {2} \ \text {hours} \\\\
                             &= 1.15 - 4 \ \text {hour} \ 30 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                             &= 8.45 \ \text {a.m}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
