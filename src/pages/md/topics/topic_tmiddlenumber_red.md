Find the number that is exactly halfway between $6.7$ and $9.1$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {6.7 + 9.1} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {15.8} {2} \\\\
&= 7.9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='answer'>
