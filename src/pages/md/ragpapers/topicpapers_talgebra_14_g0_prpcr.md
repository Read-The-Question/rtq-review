<div class='working'>

Let the input number $= a$
Let the output number $= b$

$
\begin{aligned}
(a + 5) \times 2 &= 10 \\\\
a + 5            &= \dfrac {10} {2} \\\\
a + 5            &= \dfrac {5 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a + 5            &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a + 5            &= 5 \\\\
a                &= 5 - 5 \\\\
a                &= 0
\end{aligned}
$


$
\begin{aligned}
b &= (10 - 8) \div 2 \\\\
b &= 2 \div 2 \\\\
b &= 1
\end{aligned}
$

Input number $=0$

Onput number $=1$
