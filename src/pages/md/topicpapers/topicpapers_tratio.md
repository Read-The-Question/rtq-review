$48$ pencils cost $\pounds 11.04$.  What is the cost of each pencil?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's solve this using ratios. 

$
\begin{aligned}
48 \ \text{pencils}                 &: \pounds 11.04 \\\\
\dfrac {48} {48} \ \text{pencils}   &: \pounds \dfrac {11.04} {48} \\\\
1 \ \text{pencil}                   &: \pounds 0.23
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

$\pounds 0.23$
