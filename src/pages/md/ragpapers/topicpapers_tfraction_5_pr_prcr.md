<div class='question question'>

Work out $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of $72$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{3}{8} \times 72 \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 72}{4} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 9 \times 8} {8} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 9 \times \cancel{8}} {\cancel{8}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 9 \\\\
&= 27
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$27$
