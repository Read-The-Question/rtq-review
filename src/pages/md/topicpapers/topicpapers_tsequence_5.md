<div class='question subquestion'>

In a number sequence, the $2 \text{nd}$ term is $6$ and the $5 \text{th}$ term is $18$, as shown below.

What is the **$7 \text{th}$ term** in the sequence?

$\ldots, \quad 6, \quad \ldots, \quad \ldots, \quad 18, \quad \ldots, \quad \ldots$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the difference $= d$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
6 + 3d                        &= 18 \\\\
3d                            &= 18 - 6 \\\\
3d                            &= 12 \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{12}{3} \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{3 \times 4}{3} \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{\cancel{3} \times 4}{\cancel{3}} \\\\
d                             &= 4 \\\\
7 ^ \text{th} \text{ term}    &= 18 + 2d \\\\
                              &= 18 + 2 \times 4 \\\\
                              &= 18 + 8 \\\\
                              &= 26
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$26$

</div>
</div>

<div class='working'>

ABBR:  Let's find the relation between the consecutive terms of the sequence.


The number of hexagons is calculated by adding $6$ to the previous number of hexagons.

This is an arithmetic sequence too and the difference between the consecutive patterns of the sequence is constant.

The next pattern is made by adding multiple of $6$ hexagons to the first pattern and the number of times it is added is based on the position in the sequence.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of hexagons}     &= 1 + (3 - 1) \times 6 \\\\
                              &= 1 + 2 \times 6 \\\\
                              &= 1 + 12 \\\\
                              &= 13
\end{aligned}
