<div class='question question'>

In the diagrams below, the square and rectangle have the same perimeter. 

The square has an area of $64 \ \text{cm}^2$.

The length of the rectangle is three times the width of the rectangle.

Work out the length of the rectangle.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of square} = \text{side} \times \text{side}$


ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of rectangle} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$


ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of square} = 4 \times \text{side}$


Let the side of the square $= s$

Let the width of the rectangle $= w$

Length of the rectangle $= 3w$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
s \times s              &= 64 \\\\
s^2                     &= 64 \\\\
s                       &= \sqrt {64} \\\\
s                       &= 8 \ \text {cm} \\\\
2 \times (3w + w)       &= 4 \times s \\\\
2 \times 4w             &= 4 \times 8 \\\\
8w                      &= 4 \times 8 \\\\
w                       &= \dfrac {4 \times 8} {8} \\\\
w                       &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel {8}} {\cancel {8}} \\\\
w                       &= 4 \ \text {cm} \\\\
\text {Length}          &= 3 \times w \\\\
                        &= 3 \times 4 \\\\
                        &= 12 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$12 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

The shape below is made from two overlapping rectangles.

One rectangle measures $5 \ \text{cm}$ by $4 \ \text{cm}$.

The other measures $8 \ \text{cm}$ by $6 \ \text{cm}$.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


Given that the area of the section shaded black is $15 \ \text{cm}^2$, work out the area of the section shaded grey.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Area of grey section}            &= \text {Area of larger rectangle} - (\text {Area of smaller rectangle} - \text {Area of black section}) \\\\
                                        &= 6 \times 8 - (4 \times 5 - 15) \\\\
                                        &= 48 - (20 - 15) \\\\
                                        &= 48 - 5 \\\\
                                        &= 43 \ \text {cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$43 \ \text {cm}^2$

</div>
