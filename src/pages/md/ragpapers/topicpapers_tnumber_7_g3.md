What is $(999 - 99 + 9) \div 9$?

- $99$
- $100$
- $101$
- $109$ 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= (999 - 99 + 9) \div 9 \\\\
&= (900 + 9) \div 9 \\\\
&= \dfrac {909} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9 \times 101} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{9} \times 101} {\cancel{9}} \\\\
&= 101
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
