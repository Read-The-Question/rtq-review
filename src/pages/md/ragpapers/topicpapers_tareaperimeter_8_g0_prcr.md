In the picture below is the net of a cube. What would be the total surface area of the outside of the cube when the net is made into the cube?

![the-manchester-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2019--arithmetic-a/section-1-question-15-00.png](/assets/the-manchester-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2019--arithmetic-a/section-1-question-15-00.png "the-manchester-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2019--arithmetic-a/section-1-question-15-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Surface area of cube} = 6 \times \text{edge} \times \text{edge}$


Let the side of the cube (in $\text{cm}$) $= a$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
3a      &= 15 \\\\
a       &= \dfrac {15} {3} \\\\
a       &= 5 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Surface area}  &= 6 \times a \times a \\\\
                     &= 6 \times 5 \times 5 \\\\
                     &= 150 \ \text{cm}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>
