numbers. So to find the **BACT** of $20$ and $30$ which is written as $B(20,30)$ we would work out:

Factors of $20$ are $\bold {\underline{1, 2}}, 4, \bold {\underline{5, 10}}, 20$

and Factors of $30$ are $\bold {\underline{1, 2}}, 3, \bold {\underline{5}}, 6, \bold {\underline{10}}, 15, 30$

the Common Factors, underlined in bold are $\bold {\underline{1, 2, 5, 10}}$

and then the **BACT** of $20$ and $30$ is $B(20,30) = 1 + 2 + 5 + 10 = 18$
