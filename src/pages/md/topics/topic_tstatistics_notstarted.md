the **mode** is the number which occurs the most often;

the **median** is the number which is in the middle of the list when the numbers have been put in order;

mode = $1$, median = $2$, mean = $\dfrac{15}{5} = 3$.

$
\begin{aligned}

&= 1.27 \text{metres} \\
