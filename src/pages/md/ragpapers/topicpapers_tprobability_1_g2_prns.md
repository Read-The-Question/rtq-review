<div class='question question'>

If the probability of it raining tomorrow is $0.08$, what is the probability of it not raining tomorrow?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Probabilities ALWAYS add up to $1$.


$
\begin {aligned} 
\text {P(Not raining)}    &= 1 - \text {P(Raining tomorrow)} \\\\
                                      &= 1 - 0.08 \\\\
                                      &= 0.92
\end {aligned}
$

I am going to take a counter out of the bag at random.

Complete the table below.

|  Colours of counters | Number of counters  | Probability  | 
|:------------: |:------------: |:------------: |
|      Red      |     $6$       |               |      
|      Blue      |              | $\dfrac{1} {5}$ |
|     Green     |     $6$       |               |

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


Let the number of blue counters = b

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{b}{6 + 6 + b}    &= \dfrac {1} {5} \\\\
\dfrac{b}{12 + b}       &= \dfrac {1} {5} \\\\
5b                      &= 12 + b \\\\
5b - b                  &= 12 \\\\
4b                      &= 12 \\\\ 
b                       &= \dfrac {12} {4} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {4 \times 3} {4} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 3} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
                        &= 3 \\\\
\text{P(red)}           &= \dfrac {6} {6 + 3 + 6} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {6} {15} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 3} {3 \times 5} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3} \times 5} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {5} \\\\
\text{P(green)}         &= \dfrac {6} {6 + 3 + 6} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {6} {15} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 3} {3 \times 5} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3} \times 5} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {5}
\end{aligned}
$

| Counter       |  Number      | Probability      |
|:------------: |:------------:|:----------------:|
|      Red      |     $6$      | $\dfrac{2} {5}$  |
|      Blue     |     $3$      | $\dfrac{1} {5}$  |
