<div class='question question'>

What is the sum of the first $40$ whole numbers ?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{First number}                             &= 0 \\\\
\text{Last number}                              &= 39 \\\\
\text{Total numbers}                            &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find the sum of  first 40 whole numbers.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {\text{Total numbers}} {2} \times (\text{First term + Last term}) \\\\
&= \dfrac {40} {2} \times (0 + 39) \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 20} {2} \times 39 \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 20} {\cancel{2}} \times 39 \\\\
&= 20 \times 39 \\\\
&= 780
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$780$
