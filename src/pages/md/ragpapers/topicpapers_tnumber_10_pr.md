<div class='question question'>

Which  of these is the largest number? (You must show your working)

(a) $2 + 0 \times 1 + 9$

(b) $2 \times 0 + 1 + 9$

(c) $2 + 0 \times 1 \times 9$

(d) $2 \times 0 + 1 + 9$

(e) $2 \times 0 \times 1 \times 9$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2 + 0 \times 1 + 9\\\\
&= 2 + 0 + 9 \\\\
&= 2 + 9 \\\\
&= 11
\end{aligned}
$



</div>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2 \times 0 + 1 + 9\\\\
&= 0 + 1 + 9 \\\\
&= 1 + 9 \\\\
&= 10
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2 + 0\times 1 \times 9\\\\
&= 2 + 0\times 9 \\\\
&= 2 + 0 \\\\
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2 \times 0 + 1 + 9\\\\
&= 0 + 1 + 9 \\\\
&= 1 + 9 \\\\
&= 10
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 2 \times 0\times 1\times 9\\\\
&= 0\times 1\times 9 \\\\
&= 0\times 9 \\\\
&= 0
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

 $\text{Hence the largest number is}11$

</div>
</div>
