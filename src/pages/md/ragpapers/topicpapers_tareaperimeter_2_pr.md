If they have equal perimeters, what is the difference in their area? 

</div>

<div class='working'>

4a   &= 70 + 30 + 70 + 30 \\\\

\end{aligned}
$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area of rectangle}            &= 70 \times 30 \\\\
                                    &= 2100 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\text{Area of square}               &= a \times a \\\\
                                    &= 50 \times 50 \\\\
                                    &= 2500 \ \text{cm}^2 \\\\
\text{The difference between areas} &= 2500 - 2100 \\\\
                                    &= 400 \ \text{cm}^2
\end{aligned}

                                                             &= \dfrac {30 \times \cancel {50} \times 2} {\cancel {50}} \\\\
                                                             &= 60                                                        
\end{aligned}
