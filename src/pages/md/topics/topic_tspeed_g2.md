**Question**

 A man travels $200 \ \text{miles}$ at an average speed of $60 \ \text{miles per hour}$.
 How long does the journey take in hours and minutes?

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">

</div>
<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex"> A man travels $200 \ \text{miles}$ at an average speed of $60 \ \text{miles per hour}$.
 How long does the journey take in hours and minutes?
</code></pre>
</div>
<div class="workings">

**Formulas**

<div class="formula">
- ABBR: $\text {Time} = \dfrac {\text{Distance}} {\text{Speed}}$
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Time taken}  &= \dfrac {200} {60} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {20 \times 10} {20 \times 3} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {20} \times 10} {\cancel {20} \times 3} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {10} {3} \\\\
                    &= 3 \dfrac {1} {3} \ \text {hours} \\\\
                    &= 3 \ \text {hours} + 60 \times \dfrac {1} {3} \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                    &= 3 \ \text {hours} \ 20 \ \text {minutes}
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$3 \ \text {hours} \ 20 \ \text {minutes}$

</div>

\end{aligned}
