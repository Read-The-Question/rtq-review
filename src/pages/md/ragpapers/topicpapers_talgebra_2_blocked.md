<div class='question subquestion'>

Find the **input** if the output number is $36$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Input} \times 5 + 1  &= 36 \\\\
\text {Input}               &= \dfrac {36 - 1} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {35} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {7 \times 5} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {7 \times \cancel {5}} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
                            &= 7
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$7$

</div>
