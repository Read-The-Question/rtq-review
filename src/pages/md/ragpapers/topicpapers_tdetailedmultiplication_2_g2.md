<div class='question question'>

$25 \times \ldots = 300$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
25 \times a               &= 300 \\\\
a                         &= \dfrac {300} {25} \\\\
a                         &= \dfrac {12 \times 25} {25} \\\\
a                         &= \dfrac {12 \times \cancel {25}} {\cancel {25}} \\\\
a                         &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$12$

</div>
</div>
