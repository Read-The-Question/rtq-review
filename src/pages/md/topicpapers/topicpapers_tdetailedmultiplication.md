<div class='question question'>

Work out $0.03 \times 3200$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 0.03 \times 3200 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {100} \times 3200 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 3200} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 32\cancel{00}} {1\cancel{00}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 32 \\\\
&= 96
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$96$
