A rectangle is $6 \ \text{cm}$ longer than it is wide. Its perimeter is $32 \ \text{cm}$. Find its area.

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


Let the width (in cm) $= a$

Length $= 6 + a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \times (a + (6 + a))         &= 32 \\\\
2 \times (2a + 6)              &= 32 \\\\
4a + 12                        &= 32 \\\\
4a                             &= 32 - 12 \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {20} {4} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {5 \times 4} {4} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel {4} } {\cancel {4} } \\\\
a                              &= 5 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find the area of the rectangle.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area}  &= 5 \times (6 + 5) \\\\
             &= 5 \times 11 \\\\
             &= 55 \ \text{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
