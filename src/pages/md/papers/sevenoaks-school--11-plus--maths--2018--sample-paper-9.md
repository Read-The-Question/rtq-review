How much water should be mixed with $30 \ \text{ml}$ of cordial?

Let the water mixed $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
1 \ \text{ml} : 4 \ \text{ml}                   &= 30 \ \text{ml} : a \ \text{ml} \\\\
\dfrac {1} {4}                                  &= \dfrac {30} {a} \\\\
1 \times a                                      &= 30 \times 4 \\\\
a                                               &= 120 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

Amount of water $= 120 \ \text{ml}$

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{cordial}                          &:& \text{water} \\\\
= & 1 \ \text{ml}                           &:& 4 \ \text{ml} \\\\
= & 1 \times 30                             &:& 4 \times 30 \\\\
= & 30 \ \text{ml}                          &:& 120 \ \text{ml}
\end{array}

Archie thinks of a number. Five sixths of the number is $60$. What is three quarters of the number?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number Archie thinks of $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\dfrac{5}{6} \times a  &= 60 \\\\
\dfrac{5 \times a}{6}  &= 60 \\\\
\dfrac{5a}{6}          &= 60 \\\\
5a                     &= 60 \times 6 \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {60 \times 6} {5} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {5 \times 12 \times 6} {5} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {\cancel {5} \times 12 \times 6} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
a                      &= 12 \times 6 \\\\
a                      &= 72
\end {aligned}
$

Let's find $\dfrac {3} {4}$ of the number.

$
\begin {aligned} 
&= \dfrac {3} {4} \ \text {of} \ 72 \\\\
&= \dfrac{3} {4} \times 72 \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 72} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 18 \times 4} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 18 \times \cancel{4}} {\cancel{4}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 18 \\\\
&= 54
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$54$
