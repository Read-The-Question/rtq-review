<div class='question question'>

In November $2006$ Becky is two and a half times Anna’s age and Mary is twice Anna’s age. 

In November $2009$ Anna is half of Becky’s age. Mary is six years older than Anna. 

How old are the three girls in November $2009$?  

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Anna’s age in $2006$ $= a$

Becky’s age $= 2 \dfrac {1} {2} \times a$

Mary's age $= 2a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
2 \times (a + 3)                                        &= 2 \dfrac {1} {2} \times a + 3 \\\\
2a + 6                                                  &= \dfrac {5a} {2} + 3 \\\\
\dfrac {5a} {2} + 3                                     &= 2a + 6 \\\\
\dfrac {5a} {2} - 2a                                    &= 6 - 3 \\\\
\dfrac {5a} {2} - \dfrac {2a \times 2} {2}              &= 3 \\\\
\dfrac {5a} {2} - \dfrac {4a} {2}                       &= 3 \\\\
\dfrac {5a - 4a} {2}                                    &= 3 \\\\
\dfrac {a} {2}                                          &= 3 \\\\
a                                                       &= 3 \times 2 \\\\
a                                                       &= 6 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

Let's find the age of the three girls in $2009$.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Anna’s age}  &= a + 3 \\\\
                    &= 6 + 3 \\\\
                    &= 9 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Mary's age}  &= 2a + 3 \\\\
                    &= 2 \times 6 + 3 \\\\
                    &= 12 + 3 \\\\
                    &= 15 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Becky's age} &= \dfrac {5a} {2} + 3 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times 6} {2} + 3 \\\\
                    &= 5 \times 3 + 3 \\\\
                    &= 15 + 3 \\\\
                    &= 18 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$
