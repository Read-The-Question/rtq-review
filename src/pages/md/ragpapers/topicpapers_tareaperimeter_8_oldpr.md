<div class='question question'>

A rectangle measures $4.0\,cm$ by $7.1\,cm$. What is its area?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area}             &= \text{length} \times \text{width} \\\\
                        &= 4.0 \times 7.1 \\\\
                        &= 28.4 \ \text{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$28.4 \ \text{cm}^2$

</div>
