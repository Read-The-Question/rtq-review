$3 ^ 4$ is bigger than $4 ^ 3$.

<div class='question question'>

Exactly one of these statements is correct. Which one?

**A** $43^2+ 56^2 = 4981$        

**B** $55^2 + 66^2= 7387$

**C** $77^2+ 22^2 = 6415$   

**D** $44^2+ 63^2 = 5905$   

**E** $34^2 + 51^2 = 3756$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

**A**

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 43^2+ 56^2 \\\\
&= 1849 + 3136 \\\\
&= 4985 \ \cancel {=} \ 4981
\end{aligned}
$

**B**

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 55^2 + 66^2 \\\\
&= 3025 + 4356 \\\\
&= 7381 \ \cancel {=} \ 7387
\end{aligned}
$

**C**

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 77^2+ 22^2 \\\\
&= 5929 + 484 \\\\
&= 6413 \ \cancel {=} \ 6415
\end{aligned}
$

**D**

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 44^2+ 63^2 \\\\
&= 1936 + 3969 \\\\
&= 5905
\end{aligned}
$

Statement D is correct.

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

D
