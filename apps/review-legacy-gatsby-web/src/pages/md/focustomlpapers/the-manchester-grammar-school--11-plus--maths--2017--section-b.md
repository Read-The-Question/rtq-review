\end{aligned}
$

Height of Nigel $= 1.34 \ \text{m}$

$
\begin{aligned}

w                   &= \dfrac {\cancel 7 \times 6 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel 5}{\cancel 2 \times \cancel 7 \times \cancel 5} \\\\
w                   &= 6 \ \text{cm} 
\end{aligned}

                &= 2.5 \times 6 \\\\
                &= 15 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Length is $=15 \ \text{cm}$
