<div class='question question'>

How many minutes are there from $11{:}11$ until $23{:}23$ on the same day?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 23{:}23 - 11{:}11 \\\\
&= 12 \ \text{hours} \ 12 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 12 \times 60 \ \text{minutes} + 12 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 720 + 12 \\\\
&= 732 \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$732 \ \text{minutes}$

</div>
</div>
