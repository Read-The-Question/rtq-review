a                 &= \dfrac {9 \times 12} {9} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {\cancel {9} \times 12} {\cancel {9}} \\\\
a                 &= 12
\end{aligned}

The number $= 12$

x           &= \dfrac{4 \times 12} {12} \\\\
x           &= \dfrac{4 \times \cancel {12} } {\cancel {12} } \\\\
x           &= 4
\end{aligned}
