$36 \ \text{cm}$

A piece of wire $36 \ \text{cm}$ long is bent into the shape of a rectangle. If the rectangle is twice as long as it is wide, what is its area (in square centimetres) ?

![oundle-school--11-plus--maths--2008--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-18-01.png](/assets/oundle-school--11-plus--maths--2008--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-18-01.png "oundle-school--11-plus--maths--2008--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-18-01.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of rectangle} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$


ABBR: $\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


$
\begin{aligned}
2 \times (l + b)         &= 36 \\\\
l + b                    &= 18 \\\\
2b + b                   &= 18 \\\\
3b                       &= 18 \\\\
b                        &= 6 \\\\
l                        &= 2b \\\\
l                        &= 2 \times 6 \\\\
l                        &= 12 \\\\
\text{Area}              &= l \times b \\\\
\text{Area}              &= 12 \times 6 \\\\
\text{Area}              &= 72
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$72$

</div>

a                                   &= 49 \\\\
b                                   &= 53 \\\\
