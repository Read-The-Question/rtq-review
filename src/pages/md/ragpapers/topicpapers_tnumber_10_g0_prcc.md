<div class='question question'>

Using exactly four fours, any of the operations $+ - \times \div$  make an expression whose value is $5$.

Example: 

$0 = 4 \div 4 \times 4 - 4$  

</div>

$((4 \times 4) + 4) \div 4 = 5$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= ((4 \times 4) + 4) \div 4 \\\\
&= (16 + 4) \div 4 \\\\
&= 20 \div 4 \\\\
&= 5
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
