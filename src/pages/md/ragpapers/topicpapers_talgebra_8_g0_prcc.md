I think of a number, double it and take away $5$. The answer is $13$. What was my number?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number that I think of $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2a - 5                                                       &= 13 \\\\
2a                                                           &= 13 + 5 \\\\
2a                                                           &= 18 \\\\
a                                                            &= \dfrac {18} {2} \\\\
a                                                            &= \dfrac {9 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a                                                            &= \dfrac {9 \times \cancel{2}} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
a                                                            &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

The number I thought of $= 9$

</div>
