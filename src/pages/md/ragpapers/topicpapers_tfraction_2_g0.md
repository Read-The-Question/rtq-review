What fraction is half-way between $2$ and $2.5$?

-  $\dfrac{7}{4}$
-  $\dfrac{9}{4}$
-  $\dfrac{11}{4}$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {2 + 2.5} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4.5} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {45} {2 \times 10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9 \times 5} {2 \times 2 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9 \times \cancel{5} } {4 \times \cancel{5} } \\\\
&= \dfrac {9} {4}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>C</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {9} {4}$
