How many moos are there in $3$ quacks and $1$ oink?

</div>

<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \text{Moos in 3 quacks} + \text{Moos in 1 oink} \\\\
&= 3 \times 3 + 1 \times 13 \ \text{quacks} \\\\
&= 9 + 1 \times 13 \times 3 \\\\
&= 9 + 39 \\\\
&= 48 \ \text{Moos}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

&= 4 \times 9 + \dfrac {3 \times 9 \times \cancel {7}} {\cancel {7}} \\\\
&= 4 \times 9 + 3 \times 9 \\\\
&= 36 + 27 \\\\
&= 63
\end{aligned}
