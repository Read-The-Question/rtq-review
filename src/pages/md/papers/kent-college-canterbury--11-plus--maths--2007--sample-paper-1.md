\begin {aligned}
(a + 5) \times 8    &= 72 \\\\
8a + 40             &= 72 \\\\
8a                  &= 72 - 40 \\\\
8a                  &= 32 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {32} {8} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {4 \times 8} {8} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel {8}} {\cancel {8}} \\\\
a                   &= 4
\end {aligned}
$

The number $= 4$
