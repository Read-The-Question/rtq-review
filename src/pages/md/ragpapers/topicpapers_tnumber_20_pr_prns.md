$
\begin{aligned}
             15 &= x + y \\
             5  &= x - y  \\
         15 + 5 &= x + y + (x - y) \\
             20 &= x + y + x -y \\
                &= x + x + y -y \\
                &= 2x \\
\dfrac {20}{10} &= x \\
              x &= 10 \\
              y &= 10 - 5 = 5 \\
\end{aligned}

**ROW 6**

$6 \times 2 = 12$

$6 + 2 = 12 \not 21$

$6 \times 3 = 18$

$18 \div 3 = 6$

$18 + 3 = 21$

The numbers are $18$ and $3$

<div class='question question'>

What is the average of $\frac {1}{2}$ and $\frac {1}{3}$ ?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{ \left ( \dfrac{1} {2} + \dfrac {1} {3} \right )} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{ \left ( \dfrac{3} {2 \times 3} + \dfrac {2} {3 \times 2} \right )} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{ \left ( \dfrac{3} {6} + \dfrac {2} {6} \right )} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{\dfrac{5} {6}} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
