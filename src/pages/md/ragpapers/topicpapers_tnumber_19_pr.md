<div class='question subquestion'>

There are two possible function machines that will give the results shown below.

Work out the rules for the two possible machines.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

**Rule 1**

Let first label $= + a$

Let second label $= \times b$

$
\begin{aligned}
(5 + a) \times b &= 4 \\\\
5b + ab &= 4 \\\\
(8 + a) \times b &= 10 \\\\
8b + ab &= 10 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for \'b\'

$
\begin{aligned}
8b + ab &= 10 \\\\
8b + (4 - 5b) &= 10 \\\\
8b - 5b &= 10 - 4 \\\\
3b &= 6 \\\\
b &= \dfrac{6}{3} \\\\
b &= \dfrac{2 \times 3}{3} \\\\
b &= \dfrac{2 \times \cancel 3}{\cancel 3} \\\\
b &= 2
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for \'a\'

$
\begin{aligned}
5b + ab &= 4 \\\\
5 \times 2 + 2 \times a &= 4 \\\\
10 + 2a &= 4 \\\\
2a &= 4 - 10 \\\\
2a &= -6 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{-6}{2} \\\\
a &= -3 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore,

The rule $= - 3 \ \text{then}\ \times 2$

**Rule 2**

Let first label $= \times a$

Let second label $= + b$

$
\begin{aligned}
5 \times a + b &= 4 \\\\
5a + b &= 4 \\\\
8 \times a + b &= 10 \\\\
8a + b &= 10 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for \'a\'

$
\begin{aligned}
8a + b &= 10 \\\\
8a + (4 - 5a) &= 10 \\\\
8a - 5a &= 10 - 4 \\\\
3a &= 6 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{6}{3} \\\\
a &= 2 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for \'b\'

$
\begin{aligned}
5a + b &= 4 \\\\
5 \times 2 + b &= 4 \\\\
10 + b &= 4 \\\\
b &= 4 - 10 \\\\
b &= -6
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore,

The rule $= \times 2 \ \text{then}\ - 6$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$-3 \ \text{then}\ \times 2 \\\\ \text{Or,}\ \times 2 \ \text{then}\ - 6$
