How much change does he receive from a $ \pounds 10?$

|      Drinks Menu     |                   |
|:--------------------:|:-----------------:|
|          Tea         |  $\pounds 1.40$   |
|         Coffee       |  $\pounds 1.60$   |       
|        Cappuccino    |  $\pounds 1.90$   |  
|     Hot Chocolate    |  $\pounds 1.50$   |
|     Fruit Juice      |  $\pounds 1.20$   |

\text {Number of coins}     &= \dfrac {\pounds 45} {5 \ \text {p}} \\\\

                            &= \dfrac {900 \times 5} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {900 \times \cancel {5}} {\cancel {5}} \\\\

A drink and a box of popcorn together cost $90 \ \text{p}$. Two drinks and a box of popcorn together cost $\pounds 1.45$. What does a box of popcorn cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Cost of a box of popcorn}    &= 2 \times \ \text {Cost of a drink and a box of popcorn} - \ \text {Cost of two drinks and a box of popcorn} \\\\
                                    &= 2 \times 90 \ \text {p} - \pounds 1.45 \\\\
                                    &= 180 \ \text {p} - \pounds 1.45 \times 100 \ \text {p} \\\\
                                    &= 180 \ \text {p} - 145 \ \text {p} \\\\
                                    &= 35 \ \text {p}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
