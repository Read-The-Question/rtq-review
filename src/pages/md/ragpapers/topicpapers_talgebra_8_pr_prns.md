<div class='question question'>

John picked strawberries every day for $7 \ \text{days}$. Each day after the first day, he picked $5$ more strawberries than the day before. In total he picked $175$ strawberries. How many strawberries did John pick on the last day? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


Let the number of strawberries picked on firstday $= x$

$
\begin{aligned}
x + x + 5 + x + 10 + x + 15 + ....                                      &= 175 \\\\
(x + x + x + x + x + x + x) + (5 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 25 + 30)              &= 175 \\\\
7x + 105                                                                &= 175 \\\\
7x                                                                      &= 175 - 105 \\\\
7x                                                                      &= 70 \\\\
x                                                                       &= 10 \\\\
x + 30                                                                  &= 10 + 30 \\\\
                                                                        &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$

</div>
</div>
