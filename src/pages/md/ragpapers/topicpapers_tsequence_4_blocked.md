$ a = 5 , n = 4 , d = 6 $

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The arthemetic progression} \\\\
a_5 &= a + (n-1)d \\\\
&= 4 + (5-1) \times 6 \\\\
&= 4 + 4 \times 6 \\\\
&= 4 + 24 \\\\
&= 28
\end{aligned}
$
