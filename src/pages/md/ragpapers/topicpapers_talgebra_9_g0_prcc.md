Find the value of $x$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

In a regular pentagon, all sides have the same length.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 5                                              &= 17 \\\\
3x                                                  &= 17 - 5 \\\\
3x                                                  &= 12 \\\\
x                                                   &= \dfrac {12} {3} \\\\
x                                                   &= \dfrac {3 \times 4} {3} \\\\
x                                                   &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 4} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
x                                                   &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
