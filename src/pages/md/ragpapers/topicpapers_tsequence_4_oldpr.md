</div>

<div class='working'>

As per the question,

Each number in the sequence is double the previous number. Hence the sequence would be :

$
\begin{matrix}
&   \footnotesize {\maroonC
{(\times 2)}}
&&  \footnotesize {\maroonC
{(\times 2)}}
&&  \footnotesize {\maroonC
{(\times 2)}}
&&  \footnotesize {\maroonC
{(\times 2)}}

                                    &= \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\

                                    &= 96
\end{aligned}
