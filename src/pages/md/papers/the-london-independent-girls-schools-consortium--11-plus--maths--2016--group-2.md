Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}

\dfrac{2} {100}                                 &= \dfrac {7} {a} \\\\
2 \times a                                      &= 7 \times 100 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {7 \times 100} {2} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {7 \times 50 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {7 \times 50 \times \cancel 2} {\cancel 2} \\\\
a                                               &= 7 \times 50 \\\\
a                                               &= 350 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

Mass of salmon pasta $= 350 \ \text{g}$

12a + 14b                                           &= 38 \\\\

\end{aligned}
