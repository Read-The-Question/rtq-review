<div class='question question'>

I think of a number, multiply it by $2$ and subtract $5$. The answer is $11$. What is my number? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $=a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 2 - 5    &= 11 \\\\
2a                &= 11 + 5 \\\\
2a                &= 16 \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {16} {2} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {2 \times 8} {2} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 8} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a                 &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

The number $= 8$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

Three apples and a banana cost $32 \text{p}$. Six apples and a banana cost $53 \text{p}$. How much does one banana cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of an apple $= a$

Cost of a banana $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
3a + b  &= 32 \text {p} \\\\
b       &= 32 - 3a
\end {aligned}
$

Using the above calcuation we get,

$
\begin{aligned}
6a + b  &= 53 \text {p} \\\\
6a + 32 - 3a    &= 53 \\\\
3a              &= 53 - 32 \\\\
3a              &= 21 \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {21} {3} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {3 \times 7} {3} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 7} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
a               &= 7 \text {p}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Cost of an apple}    &= 7 \text {p} \\\\
\text {Cost of one banana}  &= 32 - 3a \\\\
                            &= 32 - 3 \times 7 \\\\
                            &= 32 - 21 \\\\
                            &= 11 \text {p}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$11 \text{p}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

A shop sells apples and oranges.

Kate buys  $1$ apple and $2$ oranges. She paid $48 \text{p}$.

Jade buys  $1$ apple and $1$ orange. She paid $31 \text{p}$.

How much does $1$ orange cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of an apple $= a$

Let the cost of an orange $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 2b  &= 48 \text {p} \\\\
a + b   &= 31 \text {p}
\end{aligned}
$

Subtracting the above two equations we get,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 2b - a - b  &= 48 - 31 \\\\
b               &= 17 \text {p}
\end {aligned}
$

Cost of $1$ orange $= 17 \text {p}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$17 \text {p}$
