How much would it have sold for before the sale?

</div>

As per the question,

\begin{aligned}

\dfrac {1} {3} \times a             &= 114 \\\\
a                                   &= 114 \times 3 \\\\
a                                   &= \pounds 342
\end{aligned}
$

Original price $= \pounds 342$

</div>
