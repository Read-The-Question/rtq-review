<div class='question question'>

A bottle contains $150 \ \text{ml}$ of juice. 
Alex drinks $50\%$ more than Jane and these two friends finish the bottle between them. Calculate how much Alex drinks.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the amount of Juice that Jane drinks $= a$

Juice drunk by Alex $= a + 50 \% \ \text{of} \ x$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + 50 \% \ \text{of} \ a                          &= 150 \ \text{ml} \\\\
2a + \dfrac {50a} {100}                                &= 150 \\\\
2a + \dfrac {50a} {50 \times 2}                        &= 150 \\\\
2a + \dfrac { \cancel{50} a} { \cancel{50} \times 2}   &= 150 \\\\
2a + \dfrac {a} {2}                                    &= 150 \\\\
\dfrac{2a \times 2}{1 \times 2} + \dfrac{a}{2}         &= 150 \\\\
\dfrac{4a}{2} + \dfrac{a}{2}                           &= 150 \\\\
\dfrac  {4a + a} {2}                                   &= 150 \\\\
\dfrac  {5a} {2}                                       &= 150 \\\\
a                                                      &= \dfrac {150 \times 2} {5} \\\\
a                                                      &= \dfrac { 5 \times 30 \times 2} {5} \\\\
a                                                      &= \dfrac { \cancel 5 \times 30 \times 2} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                                                      &=  30 \times 2 \\\\
a                                                      &= 60 \ \text {ml} 
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Juice drunk by Jane}                             &= 60 \ \text {ml} \\\\
\text{Juice drunk by Alex}                             &= 60 + 50 \% \times 60 \\\\
                                                       &= 60 + \dfrac {50} {100} \times 60 \\\\
                                                       &= 60 + \dfrac {50 \times 30 \times 2} {50 \times 2} \\\\
                                                       &= 60 + \dfrac {\cancel {50} \times 30 \times \cancel 2} {\cancel {50} \times \cancel 2}  \\\\
                                                       &= 60 + 30 \\\\
                                                       &= 90 \ \text {ml}
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$90 \ \text {ml}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Amrit, Brenda and Clare share $13$ sweets. Amrit gets three times as many as Brenda, and Brenda get three times as many as Clare. How many do they each get?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of sweets Clare get $= a$

Number of sweets Brenda get $= 3a$

Number of sweets Amrit get $= 9a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 3a + 9a     &= 13 \\\\
4a + 9a         &= 13 \\\\
13a             &= 13 \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {13} {13} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {\cancel {13}} {\cancel {13}} \\\\
a               &= 1
\end{aligned}
$

Number of sweets Clare get $= 1$

Number of sweets Brenda get $= 3 \times 1 = 3$

Number of sweets Amrit get $= 9 \times 1 = 9$


</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

Amrit $=$ $9$

</div>
<div class='answer'>
