$4 \times \boxed{\phantom{555}} + 20 = 180$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing value $= a$.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times a + 20     &= 180  \\\\
4a                  &= 180 - 20 \\\\
4a                  &= 160 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {160} {4} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {40 \times 4} {4} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {40 \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
a                   &= 40 
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$
