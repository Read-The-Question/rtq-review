</div>

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}

a                               &= \dfrac {32} {10} \times \dfrac {100} {20} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {32 \times 100} {10 \times 20} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {16 \times 2 \times 20 \times 5} {2 \times 5 \times 20} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {16 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel {20} \times \cancel 5} {\cancel 2 \times \cancel 5 \times \cancel {20}} \\\\
a                               &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

My flask holds $480 \ \text{ml}$ when it is one quarter empty.

How much does it hold when it is one quarter full?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Capacity when one quarter full}          &= \dfrac{\text{Capacity when three quarter full}} {3} \\\\
                                               &= \dfrac {480} {3} \ \text{ml} \\\\
                                               &= \dfrac {3 \times 160} {3} \\\\
                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 160} {\cancel 3} \\\\
                                               &= 160 \ \text {ml}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$160 \ \text{ml}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='workingscodeblock'>
<div class='workingcodeblock'>
