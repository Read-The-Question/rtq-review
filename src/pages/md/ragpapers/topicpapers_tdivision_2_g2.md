$800 \div \ldots = 20$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
800 \div a               &= 20 \\\\
\dfrac {800} {a}         &= 20 \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {800} {20} \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {40 \times 20} {20} \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {40 \times \cancel {20}} {\cancel{20}} \\\\
a                        &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$

</div>
</div>
