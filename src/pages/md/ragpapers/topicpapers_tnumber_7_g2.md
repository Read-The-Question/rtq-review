<div class='question question'>

How much is three-sevenths of $196$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {3} {7} \ \text {of} \ 196 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {7} \times {196} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 196} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 28 \times 7} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 28 \times \cancel {7}} {\cancel{7}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 28 \\\\
&= 84
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
