<div class='question subquestion'>

How much flour is needed to make $12$ fairy cakes?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Flour required to make 12 fairy cakes} &= \dfrac {230} {2} \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {115 \times 2} {2} \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {115 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
                                             &= 115 \ \text{grams}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{fairy cakes}                                  &:& \text{flour} \\\\
= & 24                                                  &:& 230 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & \dfrac {24} {2}                                     &:& \dfrac{230} {2} \\\\
= & \dfrac {2 \times 12} {2}                            &:& \dfrac{2 \times 115} {2} \\\\
= & \dfrac {\cancel 2 \times 12} {\cancel 2}            &:& \dfrac{\cancel 2 \times 115} {\cancel 2} \\\\
= & 12                                                  &:& 115 \ \text{g} \\\\
\end{array}
$
