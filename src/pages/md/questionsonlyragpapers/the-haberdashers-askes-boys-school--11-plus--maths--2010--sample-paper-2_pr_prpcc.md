hotels in Orlando Florida during October this year. The prices are per person
and a reduction is offered for **children aged $2$ to $16 \ \text{years}$ old**. The
prices include the cost of the flight and accommodation. **Infants under $2 \ \text{years}$ old travel free**. There is an option to buy tickets to the theme
parks. Infants have free entry.

For example, if a family consisting of two adults and one child (aged $9$) 
stay for a week at the Little Rodent Hotel the total cost of the holiday,
