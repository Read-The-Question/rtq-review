<div class='question question'>

The day before yesterday my daughter was $10$ years old. Next year she will be $13$ years old. What is the date today?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Date of birth of daughter}       &= 31 \ \text{December} \\\\
\text{Today's date}                    &= 1 \ \text{January}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$1$ January.

<div class='working'>

Aa per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The difference} &= \text{ present year} - \text{battle year}  \\\\
&= 2022 - 1645 \\\\
&= 377
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

$377$
