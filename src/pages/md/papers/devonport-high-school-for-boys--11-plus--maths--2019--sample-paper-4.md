There is $5.5 \ \text{g}$ of sugar in every $10 \ \text{g}$ of jam. How much sugar is in $600 \ \text{g}$ of jam?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the amount of sugar $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
5.5 \ \text{g} : 10 \ \text{g}                  &= a \ \text{g} : 600 \ \text{g} \\\\
\dfrac{5.5} {10}                                &= \dfrac {a} {600} \\\\
a \times 10                                     &=  5.5 \times 600 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5.5 \times 600} {10} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5.5 \times 10 \times 60} {10} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5.5 \times 1 \cancel {0} \times 60} {1 \cancel {0}} \\\\
a                                               &= 5.5 \times 60 \\\\
a                                               &= 330 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

Amount of sugar $= 330 \ \text{g}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{jam}                    &:& \text{sugar} \\\\
= & 10 \ \text{g}                 &:& 5.5 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & 10 \times 60                  &:& 5.5 \times 60 \\\\
= & 600 \ \text{g}                &:& 330 \ \text{g}
\end{array}
$

Amount of sugar $= 330 \ \text{g}$
