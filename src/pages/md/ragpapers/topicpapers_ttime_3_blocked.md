For how long was I watching television? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


$
\begin{aligned}
&= 9{:}35 \ \text{am} - 6{:}55 \ \text{am} \\\\
&= 2 \ \text{hour} \ 30 \ \text{minutes}   \\\\
&= 2 \times 60 + 30  \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 120 + 30  \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 150  \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$
