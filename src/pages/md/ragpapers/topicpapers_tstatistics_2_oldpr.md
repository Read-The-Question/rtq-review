<div class='question question'>

The number of goals scored by St Francis’ netball team in their last $10$ games are as follows.

$7\:\:\: 2\:\:\: 12\:\:\: 10\:\:\: 5\:\:\: 3\:\:\:  7\:\:\:  11\:\:\:  6\:\:\:  4$

Find the mean 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Mean} &= \dfrac {\text {Total goals scored}} {\text {Number of games}} \\\\
             &= \dfrac {7 + 2 + 12 + 10 + 5 + 3 + 7 + 11 + 6 + 4} {10} \\\\
             &= \dfrac {67} {10} \\\\
             &= 6.7
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6.7$

</div>
</div>
