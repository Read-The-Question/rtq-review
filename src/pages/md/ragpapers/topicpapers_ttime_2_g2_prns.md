</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time taken}     &= 17{:}36 - 16{:}15 \\\\
                      &= 1 \ \text{hour} \ 21 \ \text{minutes}   \\\\
                      &= 1 \times 60 \ \text{minutes} + 21 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                      &= 60 + 21 \\\\
                      &= 81  \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$81  \ \text{minutes}$
