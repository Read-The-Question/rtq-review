<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's first multiply the numbers ignoring the decimal points.

$
\begin {array}{cccccccccccccc}
         &     2     &     3     &     5 \\
\times   &           &     1     &     8 \\
\hline 
   1     &     8     &     8     &     0 \\
   2     &     3     &     5     &     0 \\
\hline 
   4     &     2     &     3     &     0
\end {array}
$
