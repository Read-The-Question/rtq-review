<div class='question subquestion'>

A pattern has $45$ squares in total. How many of them are white?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the pattern number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 5 + (a - 1) \times 2    &=  45 \\\\
a + 5 + 2a - 2              &=  45 \\\\
a + 2a + 5 - 2              &=  45 \\\\
3a + 3                      &=  45 \\\\
3a                          &=  45 - 3 \\\\
3a                          &=  42 \\\\
a                           &=  \dfrac{42}{3} \\\\
