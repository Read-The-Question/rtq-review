<div class='question question'>

The total of the ages of a father, his daughter and his two sons is $96\ \text{years}$. 

The daughter is half the father's age and double the age of her two brothers. 

How old is the father?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the father's age $= a$

Daughter's age $= \dfrac {a} {2}$

Brothers' ages $= \dfrac {a} {4}$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + \dfrac {a} {2} + \dfrac {a} {4} + \dfrac {a} {4}                                                     &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {a \times 4} {4} + \dfrac {a \times 2} {2 \times 2} + \dfrac{a} {4} + \dfrac{a} {4}               &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {4a} {4} + \dfrac {2a} {4} + \dfrac {a} {4} + \dfrac {a} {4}                             		 &= 96 \\\\ 
\dfrac {4a + 2a + a + a} {4}                                                                    		 &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {8a} {4}                                                                                 		 &= 96 \\\\
8a                                                                                              		 &= 96 \times 4 \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= \dfrac {96 \times 4} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= \dfrac {96 \times 4} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= \dfrac {8 \times 12 \times 4} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= \dfrac {\cancel 8 \times 12 \times 4} {\cancel 8} \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= 12 \times 4 \\\\
a                                                                                               		 &= 48 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

Father's age $= 48 \ \text {years}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$48 \ \text {years}$

</div>
</div>
