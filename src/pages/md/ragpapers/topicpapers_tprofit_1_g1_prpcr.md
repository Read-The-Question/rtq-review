The rate of $\text{VAT}$ (value-added tax) in this country is $20\%$. In the shop CoCost, the price of a TV excluding $\text{VAT}$ is 
$\pounds 550$. Work out the cost of the TV after the $\text{VAT}$ is added on.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

If the price is increased by $20 \%$, then the total cost will be $100 + 20 = 120 \%$ of the original price. Using that, we get

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total cost}  &= 120 \% \ \text {of} \ 550 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {120} {100} \times 550 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {120 \times 550} {100} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {12 \cancel {0} \times 55 \cancel {0}} {1 \cancel {0} \cancel {0}} \\\\
                    &= 12 \times 55 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 660
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total cost}  &= 550 + 20 \% \ \text {of} \ 550 \\\\
                    &= 550 + \dfrac {20} {100} \times 550 \\\\
                    &= 550 + \dfrac {20 \times 550} {100} \\\\
                    &= 550 + \dfrac {2 \cancel {0} \times 55 \cancel {0}} {1 \cancel {0} \cancel {0}} \\\\
                    &= 550 + 2 \times 55 \\\\
                    &= 550 + 110 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 660
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 660$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

The price of a diamond ring, including $\text{VAT}$ is $\pounds 1200$. Work out the cost of the ring before the $\text{VAT}$ was added on. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Cost of the ring excluding VAT}  &= 1200 - 20 \% \ \text {of} \ 1200 \\\\
                                        &= 1200 - \dfrac {20} {100} \times 1200 \\\\
                                        &= 1200 - \dfrac {20 \times 1200} {100} \\\\
                                        &= 1200 - \dfrac {20 \times 12 \cancel {00}} {1 \cancel {00}} \\\\
                                        &= 1200 - 20 \times 12 \\\\
                                        &= 1200 - 240 \\\\
                                        &= \pounds 960
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
