Find the highest common factor (HCF) of $66$ and $30$.

</div>

&=6
\end{aligned}

Find the lowest common multiple (LCM) of $12$ and $8$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The prime factors for 12, 8 are:}\\\\
12&=2\times2\times3\\\\
8&=2\times 2\times2\\\\
\text{Multiply each factor the greater number of times}\\
\text{it occurs in steps i) or ii) above to find the LCM:}\\\\
&=2\times2\times2\times 3\\\\
&=24
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

When three consecutive odd numbers are multiplied together, the result is $9177$.

What is the sum of the numbers? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number be a, so consecutive odd numbers will be a + 2 and a + 4

$
\begin{aligned}

a                                                           &= \sqrt[3]{9177} \\\\
a                                                           &= 21 \, \text{approx.}
\end{aligned}
$

|    Numbers     |  Product  |
|----------------|-----------|
| $17, 19, 21$   |  $6783$   |
| $19, 21, 23$   |  $9177$   |
| $21, 23, 25$   |  $12075$  |

So the numbers are $= 19 , 21 , 23$

Sum of these numbers are $= 19 + 21 + 23 = 63$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$63$
