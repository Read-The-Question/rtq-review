Find the mean average of $8, 11,  15, 26$.

</div>

<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {8 + 11 + 15 + 26} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {60} {4} \\\\
&= 15
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>
