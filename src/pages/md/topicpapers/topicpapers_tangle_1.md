![aldenham-school--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png](/assets/aldenham-school--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png "aldenham-school--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


Let the missing angle $= a$

As the triangle is an isosceles triangle, the third angle $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a  + a + 96                             &= 180   \\\\
2a + 96                                 &= 180  \\\\
2a                                      &= 180 - 96  \\\\
2a                                      &= 84  \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {84} {2}  \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {42 \times 2} {2}  \\\\
a                                       &= \dfrac {42 \times \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2}}   \\\\
a                                       &= 42  \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Missing angle $= 42  \degree$
