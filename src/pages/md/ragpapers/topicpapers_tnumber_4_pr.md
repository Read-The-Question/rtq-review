</div>

&= 154 \text{ lb} \\\\
&= 154 \times \dfrac{1}{2.2} \text{ kg} \\\\

&= \dfrac{7 \times \cancel{22} \times 10}{\cancel{22}} \text{ kg} \\\\
&= 7 \times 10 \text{ kg} \\\\
&= 70 \text{ kg}
\end{aligned} 
$
