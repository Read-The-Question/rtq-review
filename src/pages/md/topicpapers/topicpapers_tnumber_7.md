Which of these gives the answer $15$?

- $24 \div 7 + 3$
- $3 + 7 \div 24$
- $7 + 3 \div 24$
- $3 \div 7 + 24$
- $24 \div 3 + 7$

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using trial and error.

$
\begin {aligned}
&= 24 \div 3 + 7 \\\\
&= \dfrac {24} {3} + 7 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 8} {3} + 7 \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 8} {\cancel {3}} + 7 \\\\
&= 8 + 7 \\\\
&= 15
\end {aligned}
$
