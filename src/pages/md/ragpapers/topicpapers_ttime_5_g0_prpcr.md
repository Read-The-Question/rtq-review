<div class='question question'>

Belinda has a digital alarm clock. 

The numbers on the clock are displayed as:

![the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-22-00.png](/assets/the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-22-00.png "the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-22-00.png")

Belinda's digital clock shows the time $01{:}10$

She puts it upside down on her bedside table.

The same time is shown.

At what times between $01{:}10$ and $12{:}00$ does the clock 
show the same time when it is upside down as when it is the 
right way up?

</div>
