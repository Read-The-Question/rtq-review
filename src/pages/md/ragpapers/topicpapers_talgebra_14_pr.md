<div class='question question'>

Josie and Holly share $\pounds 42$ in between them. Josie 
has three times as much as Holly. How much has Holly?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the share of Holly $= a$

Share of Josie $= 3a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 3a &= 42 \\\\
4a     &= 42 \\\\
a      &= \dfrac{42}{4} \\\\
a      &= \dfrac{2 \times 21}{2 \times 2} \\\\
a      &= \dfrac{\cancel 2 \times 21}{\cancel 2 \times 2} \\\\
a      &= \dfrac{21}{2} \\\\
a      &= 10.5
\end{aligned}
$

Holly has $=10.5$

</div>
</div>

What does $1$ apple cost?

$
\begin{aligned}
3a + o  &= 50 \text{p} \\\\

\end{aligned}

We have two variables so, will use Elimination method. First multiply first equation with $3$.

$
\begin{aligned}
3\times (3a + o  &= 50) \\\\
9a + 3o          &= 150  \\\\
\end{aligned}
$ 

Subtract second equation from above equation

$
\begin{aligned}
9a + 3o - a - 3o  &= 150 - 50  \\\\
8a                &= 100 \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac{100}{8}  \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac{25 \times 4}{2 \times 4}  \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac{25 \times \cancel 4}{2 \times \cancel 4} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac{25}{2} \\\\
a                 &= 12.5 \text{p}
\end{aligned}
$

Cost of one apple $= 12.5 \text{p}$

a total mass of $2.5 \ \text{kg}$.

Inside the shopping bag are the following items:

$2$ bags of carrots, each with a mass of $250 \ \text{grams}$

$1$ cabbage with a mass of $\dfrac{3}{4} \ \text{kilogram}$

$1$ bag of potatoes

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


Use this information to work out the mass of the bag of potatoes.

Give your answer in grams.

</div>

$
\begin{aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Jennie and Freddie have each thought of a number.

The difference between their numbers is $8$.

The sum of their numbers is $30$.

What are the two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number Jennie thought $= a$.

Let the number Freddie thought $= b$.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a - b      &= 8 \\\\
a + b      &= 30
\end{aligned}
$

We have got $2$ variables and we have $2$ equations to solve them. Let's eliminate to reduce it to just one variable. 

Lets add above two equation

$
\begin{aligned}
a - b + a + b   &= 8 + 30 \\\\
2a              &= 38 \\\\
a               &= \dfrac{38}{2} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac{2 \times 19}{2} \\\\
a               &= \dfrac{\cancel 2 \times 19}{\cancel 2} \\\\
a               &= 19
\end{aligned}
$

Jennie thought $= 19$

Freddie thought $= 30 - 19 = 11$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

Magda and her friends go to the cinema.

They buy $1$ large tub of popcorn, $4$ fizzy drinks, and $10$ packets of crisps.

The bill comes to $\pounds 10.89$

Lena and her friends go to the cinema.

They buy $1$ large tub of popcorn, $3$ fizzy drinks, and $7$ packets of crisps.

The bill comes to $\pounds 8.64$

How much would it cost to buy $1$ large tub of popcorn, $1$ fizzy drink and $1$ packet of crisps?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of popcorn $= p$

Let the cost of fizzy drinks $= d$

Let the cost of crisps $= c$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
p + 4d + 10c   &= \pounds 10.89 \\\\
p + 3d + 7c    &= \pounds 9.64 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

We will use elimination method to find the value of 1 large tub of popcorn, 1 fizzy drink and 1 packet of crisps.

First Subtract second equation from first

$
\begin{aligned}
p + 4d + 10c - (p + 3d + 7c)  &= 10.89 - 8.64 \\\\
p + 4d + 10c - p - 3d - 7c    &= 2.25 \\\\
d + 3c                        &= 2.25 \\\\                     
\end{aligned}
$

Now multiply the above equation by 3 and then subtract it from the first equation.

$
\begin{aligned}
3 \times (d + 3c)        &= 3\times 2.25 \\\\ 
3d + 9c                  &= 6.75 \\\\
p + 4d + 10c - (3d + 9c) &= 10.89 - 6.75 \\\\
p + d + c                &= 4.14
\end{aligned}
$

Cost of 1 large tub of popcorn, 11 fizzy drink and 11 packet of crisps $= 4.14$

</div>
</div>
