<div class='question question'>

Find the sizes of angles $x$ and $y$.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


$\text{Angles on a straight line add up to} = 180 \degree$.


As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
x + 104     &= 180   \\\\
x           &= 180  - 104  \\\\
x           &= 76 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $x$ to find $y$.

$
\begin{aligned}
x  + y + y              &= 180 \\\\
76  + 2y                &= 180 \\\\
2y                      &= 180  - 76 \\\\
2y                      &= 104  \\\\
y                       &= \dfrac {104 } {2}   \\\\
y                       &= \dfrac {52 \times 2} {2}   \\\\
y                       &= \dfrac {52 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
y                       &= 52 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$x$ $=$ $76 \degree$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

$y$ $=$ $52 \degree$

</div>
