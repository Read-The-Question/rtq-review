<div class='question question'>

Katie’s mum gives her $\pounds 10$.

She spends $\pounds 1.90$ on magazines, $\pounds 2.45$ on a birthday card and $75 \text{p}$ on chocolate. How much money does she have left? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

Let's convert all the expenses into pounds.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Money spent}     &= \pounds 1.90 +  \pounds 2.45 + 75 \text {p} \\\\
                        &= \pounds 1.90 +  \pounds 2.45 + \pounds 0.75 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 5.10 \\\\
\text {Money left}      &= 10 - 5.10 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 4.90
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 4.90$

</div>
</div>

How much $\bold{\underline{change}}$ do I get from a $\pounds 20$ note?

                            &= 7 \times \pounds 0.72 + 9 \times \pounds 1.21 \\\\
                            &= 5.04 + 10.89 \\\\
                            &= \pounds 15.93 \\\\
\text {Change received}     &= 20 - 15.93 \\\\
                            &= \pounds 4.07
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

                                &= \dfrac {12.40} {62} \times 100 \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {12.40 \times 100} {62} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {1240} {62} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {62 \times 20} {62} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel{62} \times 20} {\cancel{62}} \\\\
                                &= 20 \%
\end{aligned}
$
