- $7\text{p}$

- $17\text{p}$

\begin{aligned}
7\text{p}  &= 5 \text{p} \times 2 + 8\text{p} -11\text{p}\\\\

19\text{p}   &= 11 \text{p} + 8 \text{p} \\\\
