</div>

<div class='working'>

$

&= \dfrac {8} {10} + \dfrac{7} {10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8 + 7} {10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {15} {10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 3} {5 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{5} \times 3} {\cancel{5} \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
&= 1 \dfrac {1} {2}
\end{aligned}
$

&= \dfrac {1} {2}
\end {aligned}

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \text{four fifths or eight ninths} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4 \times 9} {5 \times 9} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8 \times 5} {9 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ < \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ < \ \dfrac {8} {9}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {8} {9}$
