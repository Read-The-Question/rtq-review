<div class='question subquestion'>

The temperature of Flossie’s surroundings is $18 \degree \text{C}$.

Work out how many times Flossie chirps in $1 \ \text{minute}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Flossie chirps (in $1 \ \text{minute}$) $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
18                          &= (a - 40) \div 7 + 10 \\\\
18                          &= \dfrac {a - 40} {7} + 10 \\\\
\dfrac {a - 40} {7} + 10    &= 18 \\\\
\dfrac {a - 40} {7}         &= 18 - 10 \\\\
\dfrac {a - 40} {7}         &= 8 \\\\
a                           &= 8 \times 7 + 40 \\\\
a                           &= 56 + 40 \\\\
a                           &= 96
\end{aligned}
$

Flossie chirps (in $1 \ \text{minute}$) $= 96$

</div>
</div>
