-  $5 \ \text{hrs} \ 38 \ \text{mins}$

&= 16{:}06 - 10{:}28 \\\\
&= 5 \ \text{hrs} \ 38 \ \text{mins}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

A coach left London Victoria $5 \ \text{minutes}$ late, at $13{:}45$, 
and arrived in Banbury $14 \ \text{minutes}$ early, at $17{:}05$. 
How long should the journey have taken if the coach had left and arrived on time?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {time had he not been late} &= 13{:}45 - 5 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                                  &= 13{:}40 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
\text {time if arrived on time}   &= 17{:}05 + 14 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                                  &= 17{:}19 \\\\
\text {duration of journey}       &= 17{:}19 - 13{:}40 \\\\
                                  &= 3 \ \text {hours} \ 39 \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3 \ \text {hours} \ 39 \ \text{minutes}$

</div>
</div>

</div>

<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

Chris runs round a track at a speed of $6 \ \text{km/hour}$.

Dave runs round the same track at a speed of $9 \ \text{km/hour}$.

When Chris has run $18$ laps, how many laps has Dave run?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {ratio of Dave's and Chris speed} &= \dfrac {9} {6} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 3} {3 \times 2} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 3} {\cancel{3} \times 2} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
\text {laps ran by Dave}                &= \dfrac {3} {2} \ \text {of} 18 \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3} {2} \times 18 \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 18} {2} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 9 \times 2} {2} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 9 \times \cancel{2}} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
                                        &= 3 \times 9 \\\\
                                        &= 27
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$27$

</div>
