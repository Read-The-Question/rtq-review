I have $30$ tiles to make a pattern on a grid.

The pattern is a series of squares.

I have used some of the $30$ tiles to make my pattern.

Do I have enough tiles left to make the **next square**, of side length $4$?

Show working to explain your answer.

![kent-college-canterbury--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-4-00.png](/assets/kent-college-canterbury--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-4-00.png "kent-college-canterbury--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-4-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Tiles required}   &= 4 \times 4 \\\\
                        &= 16 \\\\
\text{Tiles used}       &= 1 + 2 \times 2 + 3 \times 3 \\\\
                        &= 1 + 4 + 9 \\\\
                        &= 14 \\\\
\text{Tiles remaining}  &= 30 - 14 \\\\
                        &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

There are enough tiles left to make the next square.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Yes
