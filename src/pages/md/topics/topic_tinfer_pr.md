**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
T \times E \times E &= 20 \\\\
T \times 2E         &= 20 \\\\
T \times E          &= \dfrac{20} {2} \\\\
T \times E          &= \dfrac{10 \times 2} {2} \\\\
T \times E          &= \dfrac{10 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
T \times E          &= 10 \\\\
T \times E          &= 5 \times 2
\end{aligned}
$

So,

T = $5$

E = $2$

Lets verify,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= T \times E \times E \\\\
&= 5 \times 2 \times 2 \\\\
&= \green{20} = 20
\end{aligned}
$
