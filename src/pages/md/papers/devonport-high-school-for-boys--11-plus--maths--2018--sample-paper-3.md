<div class='question question'>

$\boxed{\phantom{5}} + (5 \times \boxed{\phantom{5}}) = 42$

What number does $\boxed{\phantom{5}}$ stand for?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + (5 \times a)    &= 42 \\\\
a + 5a              &= 42 \\\\
6a                  &= 42 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {42} {6} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {7 \times 6} {6} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {7 \times \cancel {6} } {\cancel{6}} \\\\
a                   &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$7$

</div>
