$\dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{\boxed{\phantom{3}}}{5} = \dfrac{9}{10}$

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {3} {5} + \dfrac {a} {10}                      &= \dfrac {9} {10} \\\\
\dfrac {3 \times 2} {5 \times 2} + \dfrac {a} {10}    &= \dfrac {9} {10} \\\\
\dfrac {6} {10} + \dfrac {a} {10}                     &= \dfrac {9} {10} \\\\
\dfrac {6 + a} {10}                                   &= \dfrac {9} {10} \\\\
6 + a                                                 &= 9 \\\\
a                                                     &= 9 - 6 \\\\
a                                                     &= 3
\end{aligned}
$
