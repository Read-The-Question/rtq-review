<div class='working'>

Let the rule $= \text{Multiply by}\ a \ \text{then add}\ b$

Input $= 2$

Output $= 17$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 2 + b &= 17 \\\\
2a + b &= 17
\end{aligned}
$

Input $= 7$

Output $= 52$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 7 + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + b &= 52
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $a$

$
\begin{aligned}
7a + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + (17 - 2a) &= 52 \\\\
7a - 2a &= 52 - 17 \\\\
5a &= 35 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{35}{5} \\\\
a &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $b$

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 17 \\\\
2 \times 7 + b &= 17 \\\\
14 + b &= 17 \\\\
b &= 17 - 14 \\\\

\end{aligned}

$
\begin{aligned}

&= 63 + 3 \\\\
&= \green {66} = 66 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}

&= \green {73} = 73 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the rule $= \text{Multipy by}\ 7 \ \text{then add}\ 3$

\end{aligned}
