<div class='question question'>

An equilateral triangle has three sides the same length. What is the size of one of the angles? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

We know, in an equilateral triangle, the three angles opposite to the equal sides are equal and the sum is $180 \degree$.

Let's take  an angle $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + a              &= 180 \degree \\\\
3a                     &= 180 \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {180} {3} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {3 \times 60} {3} \\\\
a                      &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 60} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
a                      &= 60 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

The size of one of the angles is $= 60 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$60 \degree$

</div>
</div>
