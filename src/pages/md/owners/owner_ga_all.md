As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}

a               &= 118 \degree
\end{aligned}

As per the diagram,

\end{aligned}

\end{aligned}

Let the missing angle $= f$

                  &= (\text {Length} \times \text {Width}) - (\text {Side} \times \text {Side}) \\\\
                  &= (8 \text {m} \times 6 \text {m}) - (2 \text {m} \times 2 \text {m}) \\\\
