Work out $15 \%$ of $\pounds 5$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 15 \% \ \text {of} \ \pounds 5 \\\\
&= \dfrac {15} {100} \times 500 \text {p} \\\\
&= \dfrac {15 \times 500} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {15 \times 5 \cancel{00}} {1 \cancel{00}} \\\\
&= 15 \times 5 \\\\
&= 75 \text{p}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$75 \text{p}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

My garden centre sells Summer bedding plants at $85 \text{p}$ each. I have $\pounds 30$ to spend. How many plants can I buy? Show your working.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of plants}     &= \dfrac {\text{Total money}} {\text{Cost of a plant}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\pounds 30} {85 \text {p}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3000 \text {p}} {85 \text {p}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {5 \times 600} {5 \times 17} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {5} \times 600} {\cancel {5} \times 17} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {600} {17} \\\\
                            &= 35 \ \text {remainder} \ 5 \\\\
                            &\approx 35
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$35$

</div>
</div>

She bought two cans of cola costing $53 \text{p}$$ each, three packets of crisps costing $27 \text{p}$$ each and a large bar of chocolate that costs $$\pounds 1.24$.

How much change did she get from $$\pounds 5$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Money spent}     &= 2 \times 53 \text {p} + 3 \times 27 \text {p} + \pounds 1.24 \\\\
                        &= 2 \times \pounds 0.53 + 3 \times \pounds 0.27 + \pounds 1.24 \\\\
                        &= 1.06 + 0.81 + 1.24 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 3.11 \\\\
\text {Change received} &= 5 - 3.11 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 1.89
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 1.89$

Jack buys **four** apples.

He pays with a $\pounds 2$ coin.

He gets $\pounds 1.20$ change.


![woldingham-school--11-plus--maths--2014--sample-paper-1/section-2-question-1-subquestion-1-00.png](/assets/woldingham-school--11-plus--maths--2014--sample-paper-1/section-2-question-1-subquestion-1-00.png "woldingham-school--11-plus--maths--2014--sample-paper-1/section-2-question-1-subquestion-1-00.png")

How much does **one** apple cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Cost of an apple}    &= \dfrac {2 - 1.20} {4} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {0.80} {4} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {0.20 \times 4} {4} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {0.20 \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
                            &= \pounds 0.20 \\\\
                            &= 20 \text {p}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$20 \text {p}$

</div>
