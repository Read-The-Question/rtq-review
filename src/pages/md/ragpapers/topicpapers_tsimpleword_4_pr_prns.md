<div class='question subquestion'>

John and Daisy share a pizza. John eats $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of it and Daisy eats $\dfrac{7}{12}$ of it. What fraction remains?                                          

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{remaining} &= 1 - (\dfrac {1}{5} + \dfrac{7}{12} ) \\\\
&= 1 - (\dfrac {1 \times 12} {5 \times 12} + \dfrac{7 \times 5}{12 \times 5}) \\\\
&= 1 - (\dfrac {12}{60} + \dfrac {35}{60}) \\\\
&= 1 - \dfrac {47} {60} \\\\
&= \dfrac {60 - 47} {60} \\\\
&= \dfrac {13} {60}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>

$\dfrac {13} {60}$

</div>

<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subsubquestion'>
<div class='question subsubquestion'>

If there are $14$ small bottles, how many large bottles will be needed?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Let bottles} &= a \\\\
14 \times \dfrac{5}{4}  &= a \times \dfrac{25}{7} \\\\
a  &= \dfrac{7}{5 \times 5} \times \dfrac{5 \times 7 \times 2}{4} \\\\
a  &= \dfrac{7}{5 \times \cancel 5} \times \dfrac{\cancel 5 \times 7 \times \cancel 2}{2 \times \cancel 2} \\\\
a &= \dfrac{49}{10} \\\\
a &= 4\dfrac{9}{10} 
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4\dfrac{9}{10}$

</div>
