</div>

$
\begin{aligned}

                   &= \pounds 4.93 + \pounds \dfrac {24} {100} + \pounds \dfrac {58} {100} \\\\
                   &= \pounds 4.93 + \pounds 0.24 + \pounds 0.58 \\\\
                   &= \pounds 5.75
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

$\pounds 5.75$
