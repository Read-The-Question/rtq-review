was dropped. It then bounces again to a height that is $75\%$ of the previous height and

Adam, Ben and Carl are weighing themselves.

What is their total weight in kg?

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
A + B                               &= 102 \\\\

-A + C                              &= -5 \\\\
C                                   &= -5+A \\\\
A + C                               &= 93 \\\\
A - 5 + A                           &= 93 \\\\
2A                                  &= 93 + 5 \\\\
A                                   & = 98 \div 2 \\\\
A                                   &= 49 \\\\
B                                   &= 53 \\\\
C                                   &= 45 \\\\
Total Weight                        &= A + B + C \\\\
                                    &= 49 + 53 + 45 \\\\
                                    &= 147
\end{aligned}
$
