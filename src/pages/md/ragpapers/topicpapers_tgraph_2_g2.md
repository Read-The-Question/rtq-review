%format% The pie chart has an angle of $90 \degree$ for the Tour de France.

How many people does this represent?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of people}     &= \dfrac {90} {360} \times 80 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {90 \times 80} {360} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {90 \times 4 \times 20} {90 \times 4} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {90} \times \cancel 4 \times 20} {\cancel {90} \times \cancel 4} \\\\
                            &= 20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
