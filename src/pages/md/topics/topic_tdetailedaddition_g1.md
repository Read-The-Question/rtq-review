**Working**

Let the missing digits = $a , b$

As per the question,

$a34 + b97 = 631$

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
      &   a       &   3   &   4 \\
{}+   &   b       &   9   &   7 \\
   \hline
      & (a+b+1)   &   3   &   1
\end{array}
$

Comparing the digits, $a + b + 1 = 6$

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b    &= 6 - 1 \\\\
a + b    &= 5
\end{aligned}
$

The set of digits $a ,b$ can be = $ (1, 4), (2, 3), (3, 2), (4,1)$
</div>
