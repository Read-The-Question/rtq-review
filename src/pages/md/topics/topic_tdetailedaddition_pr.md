<div class='question question'>

A number of shapes have been made with matches.

For this pattern:

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)

How many matches will be in the $4 \text{th}$ shape?

- $26$
- $18$  
- $24$   
- $20$  
- $22$  

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

The given pattern follow an arthemetic progression 

$6$ matches increase $1$ on next one

$ a = 4 , n = 4 , d = 6 $

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{The arthemetic progression} \\\\
a_4 &= a + (n-1)d \\\\
&= 4 + (4-1) \times 6 \\\\
&= 4 + 3 \times 6 \\\\
&= 4 + 18 \\\\
&= 22
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>Q</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$22$
