Work out $17 \dfrac{1}{2} \div 5$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 17 \dfrac{1}{2} \div 5 \\\\
&= \dfrac {35} {2} \div 5 \\\\
&= \dfrac {35} {2} \times \dfrac {1} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {35} {2 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7 \times 5} {2 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7 \times \cancel{5}} {2 \times \cancel{5}} \\\\
&= \dfrac {7} {2} \\\\
&= 3 \dfrac {1} {2}
\end{aligned}
$
