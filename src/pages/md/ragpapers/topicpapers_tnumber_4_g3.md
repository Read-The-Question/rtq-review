Find the sum of the smallest multiple of $11$ greater than $100$ and the largest multiple of $11$ less than $300$. 

</div>

<div class='working'>

\begin{aligned}
\text{Smallest multiple} > 100   &= 110 \\\\
\text{Largest multiple} < 300    &= 297 \\\\

                                  &= 407
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$407$
