How many times does two hundred go into one million?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1000000} {200} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5000 \times 200} {200} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5000 \times \cancel{200} } {\cancel{200} } \\\\
&= 5000
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$5000$
