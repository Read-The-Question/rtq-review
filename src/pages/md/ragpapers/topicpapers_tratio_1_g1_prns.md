How much will $46$ lollipops cost if they cost $\pounds 1.25$ each?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Price of 46 lollipops}    &= 46 \times \text{Price of 1 lollipop} \\\\
                                &= 46 \times 1.25 \\\\
                                &= \pounds 57.5
\end{aligned}
$

\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}

= & 1                                           &:& \pounds 1.25 \\\\
= & 1 \times 46                                 &:& 1.25 \times 46 \\\\
= & 46                                          &:& \pounds 57.5
\end{array}
$

<div class='question question'>

Jeff has $8$ pizzas to share between himself and his friends.

If each person is to get $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a pizza how many friends can he feed?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of persons}    &= \dfrac {\text{Total pizzas}} {\text{Pizza per person}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {8} { \dfrac {2} {3}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {8 \times 3} { 2} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {4 \times 2 \times 3} {2} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel{2} \times 3} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
                            &= 4 \times 3 \\\\
                            &= 12 \\\\
\text{Number of friends}    &= 12 - 1 \\\\
                            &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of persons $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{2}{3} \text{ pizzas} : 1 \text{ people}  &= 8 \text{ pizzas} : a \text{ persons} \\\\
\dfrac{2}{3 \times 1}                           &= \dfrac {8} {a} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {8 \times 3} { 2} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {4 \times 2 \times 3} {2} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel{2} \times 3} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
a                                               &= 4 \times 3 \\\\
a                                               &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of persons}    &= 12 \\\\
\text{Number of friends}    &= 12 - 1 \\\\
                            &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{pizzas}                                                           &:& \text{persons} \\\\
= & \dfrac{2}{3}                                                            &:& 1 \\\\
= & \dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{3}{2}                                        &:& 1 \times \dfrac{3}{2} \\\\
= & 1                                                                       &:& \dfrac{3}{2} \\\\
= & 1 \times 8                                                              &:& \dfrac{3 \times 8}{2} \\\\
= & 8                                                                       &:& \dfrac{3 \times 4 \times 2}{2} \\\\
= & 8                                                                       &:& \dfrac{3 \times 4 \times \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2}} \\\\
= & 8                                                                       &:& 12
\end{array}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of persons}    &= 12 \\\\
\text{Number of friends}    &= 12 - 1 \\\\
                            &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$11$

-  $1:1$
-  $1:2$
-  $1:3$ 
-  $1:4$ 
-  $1:5$

</div>

\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}

= & \dfrac{1} {4}                               &:& \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \dfrac {3 \times 4} {4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \dfrac {3 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& 3
\end{array}
$

</div>

<p>C</p>

$1:3$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let length of the model $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
50 \ \text{m} : 1 \ \text{m}                    &= 120 \ \text{m} : a \ \text{m} \\\\
\dfrac {50} {1}                                 &= \dfrac {120} {a} \\\\
50 \times a                                     &= 120 \times 1 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {120} {50} \ \text{m} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {120 \times 100} {50} \ \text{cm} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {120 \times 50 \times 2} {50} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {120 \times \cancel {50} \times 2} {\cancel {50}} \\\\
a                                               &= 120 \times 2 \\\\
a                                               &= 240 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Length of the model $= 240 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{tower}                                &:& \text{model} \\\\
= & 50 \ \text{m}                               &:& 1 \ \text{m} \\\\
= & 50 \ \text{m}                               &:& 1 \times 100 \ \text{cm} \\\\
= & 50                                          &:& 100 \\\\
= & 1                                           &:& \dfrac {100} {50} \\\\
= & 1 \times 120                                &:& \dfrac {100 \times 120} {50} \\\\
= & 120                                         &:& \dfrac {50 \times 2 \times 120} {50} \\\\
= & 120                                         &:& \dfrac {\cancel {50} \times 2 \times 120} {\cancel {50}} \\\\
= & 120                                         &:& 2 \times 120 \\\\
= & 120 \ \text{m}                              &:& 240 \ \text{cm}
\end{array}
$

Length of the model $= 240 \ \text{cm}$

$

\dfrac{2} {6}                                   &= \dfrac {9} {a} \\\\

a                                               &= \dfrac {9 \times 6} {2} \\\\

a                                               &= \dfrac {9 \times \cancel 2 \times 3} {\cancel 2} \\\\
a                                               &= 9 \times 3 \\\\
a                                               &= 27
\end{aligned}
$

Number of maltesers $= 27$

</div>

= & 1 \times 9                        &:& \dfrac {6 \times 9} {2} \\\\
