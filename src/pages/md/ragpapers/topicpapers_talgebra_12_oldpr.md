<div class='question question'>

Write down two numbers that add to $14$ and multiply to $48$. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number be $a$ and second be $b$.

As per question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b      &= 14 \\
a \times b &= 48
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find $a$ and $b$.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= x^2 + 14x + 48 \\\\
&= x^2 + (6 + 8)x + (6 \times 8) \\\\
&= (x + 6) (x+ 8)
\end{aligned}
$

$x = 6$ and $x = 8$

the two numbers are $6$ and $8$

checking: 

$
\begin{aligned}
&- a + b   \\   
&= 6 + 8 \\
&= 14 \\\\
&= a \times b \\
&= 6 \times 8
&= 48 
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6$ and $8$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

If two numbers multiply to give $36$ and their sum is $15$, what are the two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the two numbers $= a$ and $b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times b          &= 36 \\\\
a + b               &= 15
\end{aligned}
$

We can easily infer the values by using trial and error.


Factors of $36 = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36$

| Numbers   |  Multiplication  |  Sum  |
|:-------:  |:---------------: |:----: |
| $1, 36$   | $36$             | $37$  |
| $2, 18$   | $36$             | $20$  |
| $3, 12$   | $36$             | $15$  |

A quick check shows that $3$ and $12$ meets all the criteria above.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
