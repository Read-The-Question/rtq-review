I start watching a film at $7:45\,pm$ and turn the television off at $9:35\,pm$. For how long was I watching television?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Watching Time} &= 9{:}35 - 7{:}45\\\\
                     &= 1 \ \text{hour} \ 50 \ \text{minutes}   \\\\
                     &= 1 \times 60 \ \text{minutes} + 50  \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                     &= 60 + 50 \\\\
                     &= 110  \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$110  \ \text{minutes}$

<div class='question subquestion'>

If he is paid $\pounds 6.00$ for each hour worked, how much will he be paid for all the work?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required.


$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Total amount} &= 4 \ \text{hours} \ 15 \ \text{ minutes} \times 6 \\\\
                     &= (4 \ \text{hours} +   15 \ \text{ minutes} ) \times 6 \\\\
                     &= (4 \ \text{hours} +   \dfrac {15} {60} \ \text{hours} ) \times 6 \\\\
                     &= 4 \times 6 + \dfrac {15 \times 6} {60} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {5 \times 3 \times 6} {5 \times 2 \times 6} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 3 \times \cancel 6} {\cancel 5 \times 2 \times \cancel 6} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
                     &= 24 + 1.5 \\\\ 
                     &= \pounds 25.5
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 25.5$

</div>
