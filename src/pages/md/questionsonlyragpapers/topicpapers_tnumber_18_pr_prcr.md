Factors are numbers that divide into another number exactly.
For example, the factors of $28$ are $1, 2, 4, 7, 14$ and $28$.


$28$ is called a **perfect number** because the 
factors of $28$, not including the number $28$ 
itself, add up to $28$.

So, $1 + 2 + 4 + 7 + 14 = 28$.

</div>
