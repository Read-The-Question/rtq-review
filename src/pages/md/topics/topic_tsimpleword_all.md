<div class='question question'>

If a basketball weighs $300 \ \text{g}$ plus half its own weight, how much does it weigh?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}

                                 &= 300 + \dfrac {300} {2} \\\\
                                 &= 300 + \dfrac {150 \times 2} {2} \\\\
                                 &= 300 + \dfrac {150 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
                                 &= 300 + 150 \\\\
                                 &= 450 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$450 \ \text{g}$
