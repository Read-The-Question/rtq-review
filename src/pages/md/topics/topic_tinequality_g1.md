$(7 \times 5) - 4  \ \square \  7 \times (5 - 4)$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's calculate the first value.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= (7 \times 5) - 4 \\\\
&= 35 - 4 \\\\
&= 31
\end{aligned}
$

Let's calculate the second value.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 7 \times (5 - 4) \\\\
&= 7 \times 1 \\\\
&= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Using the calculation above,

$(7 \times 5) - 4 \ \quad \boxed{\green{>}}  \quad  7 \times (5 - 4)$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
