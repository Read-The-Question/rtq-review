<div class='question subquestion'>

If there were more adult tickets than child tickets, how many of 
each did I buy?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of adult tickets}    &= 6 \\\\
\text{Number of child tickets}    &= 3
\end{aligned}
$

Lets verify.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 7 \times 6 + 3 \times 3 \\\\
&= 42 + 9 \\\\
&= \pounds 51
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

</div>
