Amran wrote down the calculation:

$\dfrac{6000 \times 20} {30} = 40$

Her teacher looked at the answer and said it could not be correct.

Explain how her teacher knew the answer must be wrong.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$\dfrac{6000 \times 20} {30} = 40$

Lets verify.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{6000 \times 20} {30} \\\\ 
&= \dfrac{30 \times 200 \times 20} {30} \\\\
&= \dfrac{\cancel{30} \times 200 \times 20} {\cancel{30}} \\\\
&= 200 \times 20 \\\\
&= 4000
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4000$
