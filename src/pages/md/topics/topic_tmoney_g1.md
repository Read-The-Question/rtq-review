                     &= \dfrac {85 \times 45000} {100}  \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {85 \times 450 \cancel {00} } {1 \cancel {00} }  \\\\
                     &= 85 \times 450 \\\\
                     &= \pounds 38250
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 38250$
