![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)

<div class='working'>


Total volume of the solid $= 56 \text {cm}^3$ 

volume of one cube $=8 \text{cm}^3 $

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \sqrt{8} \\\\
&= 2 \\\\
&= 2 \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
