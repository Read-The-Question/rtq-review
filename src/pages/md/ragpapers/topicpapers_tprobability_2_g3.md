<div class='question question'>

I have $6$ blue counters and $4$ red counters in a bag. What is the probability of choosing a red counter when I take a counter out of the bag? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(red)}       &= \dfrac {4} {6 + 4} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {4} {10} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 2} {2 \times 5} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 2} {\cancel{2} \times 5} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {2} {5}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {2} {5}$

</div>
</div>

What is the probablility that the sweet you choose will be an orange sweet?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(orange)}        &= \dfrac {20} {15 + 20 + 12 + 13} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {20} {60} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {20} {3\times 20} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel {20}} {3\times \cancel {20}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>A</p>
</div>

$\dfrac{1}{3}$
