A pyramid has a rectangular base. The formula for working out the volume of a pyramid is:

$V = \dfrac{1}{3} \times L \times W \times H $

$L \ \text{metres}$ and $W \ \text{metres}$ are the length and width of the rectangular base.  

$H \ \text{metres}$ is the height of the pyramid. 

$V \ \text{cubic metres}$ is the volume of the pyramid. 

For example, if the base is $6 \ \text{metres}$ long and $3 \ \text{metres}$ wide, and 
the height is $2 \ \text{metres}$, then the volume is $12 \ \text{cubic metres}$, because; 

$V = \dfrac{1}{3} \times 6 \times 3 \times 2 = 12$

</div>
