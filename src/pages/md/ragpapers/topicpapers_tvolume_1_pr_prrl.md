<div class='question question'>

A $7 \times 7 \times 7$ cube is painted, and then cut into $1 \times 1 \times 1$ cubes. 

How many of these cubes are painted on exactly two sides?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of cubes painted two sides}                &= (7 \times 7 - 6 \times 6) - 4 + (7 \times 7 - 6 \times 6) - 4 + 5 \times 4 \\\\
                                                        &= 2(49 - 36) - 8 + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 26 - 8 + 20 \\\\
                                                        &= 38
\end{aligned}
$
