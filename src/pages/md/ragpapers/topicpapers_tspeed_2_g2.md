<div class='question question'>

Adam ran $3 \ \text{kilometres}$ in $13 \ \text{minutes}$. Jackie started $30 \ \text{seconds}$ after him and finished $30 \  \text{seconds}$ before him. What was Jackie's speed in kilometres per hour?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Speed} = \dfrac {\text{Distance}} {\text{Time}}$


ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time taken by Jackie}   &= 13 \ \text{minutes} - 30 \ \text{seconds} - 30 \ \text{seconds} \\\\
                              &= 13 \ \text{minutes} - 60 \ \text{seconds} \\\\
                              &= 13 \ \text{minutes} - 1 \ \text{minute} \\\\
                              &= 12 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
\text{Speed}                  &= \dfrac {3 \text{ kilometres}} {12 \ \text{minutes}} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {3 \times 60 \text{ kilometres}} {12 \ \text{hours}} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {3 \times 60} {12} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {3 \times 12 \times 5} {12} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel{12} \times 5} {\cancel{12}} \\\\
                              &= 3 \times 5 \\\\
                              &= 15 \text{ kilometres per hour}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$15 \text{ kilometres per hour}$

</div>
</div>

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Neil drives his car for $20 \ \text{minutes}$ at $45 \ \text{kilometres per hour}$.

Stephen does the same journey at a speed of $30 \ \text{kilometres per hour}$. How long did it take him?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$


ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Distance}                 &= 45 \text{ kilometres} \times 20 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                                &= 45 \text{ kilometres} \times \dfrac{20}{60} \ \text{hours} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {45 \times 20} {60}  \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {15 \times 3 \times 20} {20 \times 3}  \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {15 \times \cancel{3} \times \cancel{20}} {\cancel{20} \times \cancel{3}}  \\\\
                                &= 15 \text{ kilometres}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time taken by Stephen}    &= \dfrac {15}{30} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {15}{15 \times 2} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel{15}}{\cancel{15} \times 2} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {1}{2} \ \text{hours} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {60}{2} \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                                &= 30 \text{ minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$30 \ \text{minutes}$

</div>
</div>

</div>

<div class='question question'>

In $3\, hours$ a train travels $156\, km$. What is it speed in $km/h$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Time} = \dfrac {\text{Distance}} {\text{Speed}}$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Speed}   &=   \dfrac{156}{3} \\\\
               &=   \dfrac{52 \times 3}{3} \\\\
               &=   \dfrac{52 \times \cancel{3}}{\cancel{3}} \\\\
               &=   52 \text{ km/h}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$52 \text{ km/h}$

</div>
