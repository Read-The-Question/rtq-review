What fraction of this flag has been shaded? Give the fraction as simply as possible. 

![reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-22-00.png](/assets/reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-22-00.png "reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-22-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {\text{Shaded portion}} {\text{Count of portions}} &= \dfrac {9} {24} \\\\
                                                          &= \dfrac {3 \times 3} {8 \times 3} \\\\
                                                          &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel{3}} {8 \times \cancel{3}} \\\\
                                                          &= \dfrac {3} {8}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {3} {8}$

</div>

How many students favourite colour is Green? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Students who prefer green}    &= \dfrac {1} {4} \times 32 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {32} {4} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {4 \times 8} {4} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel{4} \times 8} {\cancel{4}} \\\\
                                    &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

How many students favourite colour is NOT Blue?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Note: **NOT** selecting blue is same as selecting any of the other colours instead.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Not blue}     &= \bigg(\dfrac {1} {4} + \dfrac {1} {4} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \bigg(\dfrac {1 \times 2} {4 \times 2} + \dfrac {1 \times 2} {4 \times 2} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \bigg(\dfrac {2} {8} + \dfrac {2} {8} + \dfrac {1} {8} \bigg) \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5} {8} \times 32 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times 32} {8} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times 8 \times 4} {8} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel{8} \times 4} {\cancel{8}} \\\\
                    &= 5 \times 4 \\\\
                    &= 20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$20$

</div>
</div>
