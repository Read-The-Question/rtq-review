<div class='question question'>

$4$ girls have an average height of $1.4 \ \text{m}$. A fifth girl of height $1.2 \ \text{m}$ joins them.
What is the average height of all five girls?

-  $2.6 \ \text{m}$
-  $1.3 \ \text{m}$
-  none of these
-  $1.36 \ \text{m}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total height of 4 girls}     &= 4 \times 1.4 \\\\
                                    &= 5.6 \ \text {m} \\\\
\text {Average of 5 girls}          &= \dfrac {5.6 + 1.2} {5} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {6.8} {5} \\\\
                                    &= 1.36 \ \text {m}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$1.36 \ \text {m}$

</div>

is minus two. What is the other number?

a                       &= 12 + 2 \\\\

\end{aligned}
