$
\begin{aligned}

&= \dfrac{111492} {76 \times 100} \\\\
&= \dfrac{1467 \times 76}{76 \times 100} \\\\
&= \dfrac{1467 \times \cancel{76}} {\cancel{76} \times 100} \\\\
&= \dfrac{1467} {100} \\\\
&= 14.67 
\end{aligned}

If $13! = 13 \times n!$ what number does $'n'$ stand for?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
n!      &= \dfrac {13!} {13} \\\\
n!      &= \dfrac {13 \times 12!} {13} \\\\
n!      &= \dfrac {\cancel {13} \times 12!} {\cancel {13}} \\\\
n!      &= 12! \\\\
n       &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$12$
