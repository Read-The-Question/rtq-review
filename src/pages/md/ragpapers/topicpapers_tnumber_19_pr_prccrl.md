![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


Write suitable labels on the diagram below.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the input $= x$

Let the rule $= a \times x + b$

From Row 1, where $x = -1$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times -1 + b &= 1 \\\\
b - a &= 1
\end{aligned}
$

From Row 2, where $x = 0$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 0 + b &= 1.5 \\\\
b &= 1.5
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for \'a\',

$
\begin{aligned}
b - a &= 1 \\\\
1.5 - a &= 1 \\\\
a &= 0.5
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the rule $= 0.5 \times x + 1.5$

Lets verify,

From Row 3, where $x = 2$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 0.5 \times 2 + 1.5 \\\\
&= 1 + 1.5 \\\\
&= 2.5 = 2.5
\end{aligned}
$

From Row 4, where $x = 5$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 0.5 \times 5 + 1.5 \\\\
&= 2.5 + 1.5 \\\\
&= 4 = 4
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the labels are **$\times 0.5$** , **$+ 1.5$**

</div>
