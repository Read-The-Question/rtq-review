Billy is $2 \ \text{metres}$ and $2 \ \text{centimetres}$ tall. Write his height as a decimal, in metres.


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Height}   &= 2 \ \text{metres } 2 \ \text{centimetres} \\\\
                &= 2 \ \text{metres} + \dfrac{2}{100} \ \text{metres} \\\\
                &= 2 \ \text{metres} + 0.02 \ \text{metres} \\\\
                &= 2.02 \ \text{metres}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$2.02 \ \text{metres}$

<div class='question subquestion'>

$\boxed{\phantom{5555}} \times 24 = 12$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 24 &= 12 \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {12} {24} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {12} {12 \times 2} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {\cancel {12}} {\cancel {12} \times 2} \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {1} {2}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {2}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

$72 \div 9 = \boxed{\phantom{5555}} \div 3$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
72 \div 9 &= a \div 3 \\\\
\dfrac {72} {9}  &= \dfrac {a} {3} \\\\
a                &= \dfrac {72 \times 3} {9} \\\\
a                &= \dfrac {8 \times {9} \times 3} {{9}} \\\\
a                &= \dfrac {8 \times \cancel{9} \times 3} {\cancel{9}} \\\\
a                &= 24
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$24$

</div>
