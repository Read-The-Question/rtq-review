A piece of string is $12.6 \ \text{cm}$ long. If a piece $3.75 \ \text{cm}$ is cut from it, 
how long is the piece of string that remains? 

</div>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{String (Remains)} &= \text{Sting (Total)} - \text{String (Cut)} \\\\
&=  12.6 - 3.75 \\\\
&=   8.85 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
