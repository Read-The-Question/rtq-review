- A score of less than $2$ is more likely than a score of $2$ or more

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(3)}       &= \dfrac {2} {8} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {2} {4 \times 2} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {\cancel {2}} {4 \times \cancel {2}} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {1} {4}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(4)}       &= \dfrac {2} {8} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {2} {4 \times 2} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {\cancel {2}} {4 \times \cancel {2}} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {1} {4}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(1)}       &= \dfrac {3} {8}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{P(Odd)}       &= \dfrac {5} {8}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
