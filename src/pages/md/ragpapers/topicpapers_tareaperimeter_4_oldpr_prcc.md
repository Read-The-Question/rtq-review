If the shape to the right is a square, work out the 
value of $x$.

![reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-7/section-1-question-13-subquestion-1-00.png](/assets/reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-7/section-1-question-13-subquestion-1-00.png "reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2020--sample-paper-7/section-1-question-13-subquestion-1-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2x + 5                                       &= 21 \\\\
2x                                           &= 21 - 5 \\\\
2x                                           &= 16 \\\\
x                                            &= \dfrac {16} {2} \\\\
x                                            &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 8} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
x                                            &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8$

</div>
