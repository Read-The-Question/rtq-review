<div class='question subquestion'>

Complete the table.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let total student in class $6$A be A and in class $6B$ be $B. Total boys in $6$A be BA and $6$B be BB, and total girls in $6$A
be GA and $6$B GB.

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total student}     &= 48 \\\\
A + B                    &= 48 \\\\
A + 18                   &= 48 \\\\
A                        &= 48 -18 \\\\
                         &= 30
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total girls in 6A} + \text{total boys in 6A}    &= 30 \\\\
BA + GA                                               &= 30 \\\\
14 + GA                                               &= 30 \\\\
GA                                                    &= 30 - 14 \\\\
                                                      &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total girls in 6B} + \text{total boys in 6B}    &= 18 \\\\
BB + GB                                               &= 18 \\\\
BB + 6                                               &= 18 \\\\
BB                                                    &= 18 - 6 \\\\
                                                      &= 12 
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total boys in both classes}    &= BA + BB \\\\
                                     &= 14 + 12 \\\\
                                     &= 26
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total boys in both classes}    &= GA + GB \\\\
                                     &= 16 + 6 \\\\
                                     &= 22
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$16, 30, 12, 26, 22$

</div>
