<div class='question subquestion'>

What is the area of the triangle formed by these three points?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Base of the triangle}     &= \sqrt {(-1 - 2) ^ 2 + (1 - 1) ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {(-3) ^ 2 + 0 ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {9 + 0} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {9} \\\\
                                &= 3 \\\\
\text{Height of the triangle}   &= 2 - 1 \\\\
                                &= 1 \\\\
\text{Area of the square}       &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \text{(Base of the triangle)} \times \text{(Height of the triangle)} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 3 \times 1 \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
                                &= 1.5
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

What is the area of the rectangle?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Length of rectangle}      &= \sqrt {(4 - 4) ^ 2 + (5 - 1) ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {0 ^ 2 + 4 ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {0 + 16} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {16} \\\\
                                &= 4 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\text{Breadth of rectangle}     &= \sqrt {(-1 - 4) ^ 2 + (1 - 1) ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {(-5)) ^ 2 + 0 ^ 2} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {25 + 0} \\\\
                                &= \sqrt {25} \\\\
                                &= 5 \ \text{cm} \\\\
\text{Area of rectangle}        &= \text{Length of rectangle} \times \text{Breadth of rectangle} \\\\
                                &= 4 \times 5 \\\\
                                &= 20 \ {\text{cm}} ^ 2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
