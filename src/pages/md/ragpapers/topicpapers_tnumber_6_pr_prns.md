With which number must 4 be *clotted* with to give $42$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$42$ backwards = $24$

let the number = a

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times \text a &= 24 \\\\
\text a          &= \dfrac{24}{4} \\\\
\text a          &= \dfrac{6 \times 4}{4} \\\\
\text a          &= \dfrac{6 \times \cancel 4}{\cancel 4} \\\\
\text a          &= 6
\end{aligned}
$
