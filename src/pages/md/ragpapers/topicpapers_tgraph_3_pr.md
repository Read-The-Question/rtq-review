<div class='question question'>

$40$ children predicted who would win the boys' Call of Duty competition. 

The pie chart below shows their predictions.

![sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2011--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-11-00.png](/assets/sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2011--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-11-00.png "sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2011--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-11-00.png")

How many children predicted that Stefan would win?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Angle of Stefan sector}     &= 90 - 5 \% \text{ of } 360 \\\\
                                  &= 90 - \dfrac {5} {100} \times 360 \\\\
                                  &= 90 - \dfrac {5 \times 360} {100} \\\\
                                  &= 90 - \dfrac {5 \times 20 \times 18} {5 \times 20} \\\\
                                  &= 90 - \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times \cancel {20} \times 18} {\cancel 5 \times \cancel {20}} \\\\
                                  &= 90 - 18 \\\\
                                  &= 72 \degree \\\\
\text{Number of children}         &= \dfrac {72} {360} \times 40 \\\\
                                  &= \dfrac {72 \times 40} {360} \\\\
                                  &= \dfrac {8 \times 9 \times 40} {9 \times 40} \\\\
                                  &= \dfrac {8 \times \cancel 9 \times \cancel {40}} {\cancel 9 \times \cancel {40}} \\\\
                                  &= 8
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8$

</div>
</div>

If, instead, the vertical line had passed through $20$ on the $x$ axis and you reflected $ABCD$ in the new line, what would the co-ordinates of all four corners of your new shape be?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

We have to reflect through a vertical line therefore y-coordinates will remain the same,

and x-coordinates of reflected points will be at the same distance as the original points but on the other side

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Distance of point with abscissa 3}    &= 20 - 3 \\\\
                                              &= 17 \\\\
\text{Corresponding reflected abscissa}       &= 20 + 17 \\\\
                                              &= 37 \\\\
\text{Distance of point with abscissa 5}    &= 20 - 5 \\\\
                                              &= 15 \\\\
\text{Corresponding reflected abscissa}       &= 20 + 15 \\\\
                                              &= 35
\end{aligned}
$

$(35, 3), (37, 3), (35, 5), (37, 5)$
