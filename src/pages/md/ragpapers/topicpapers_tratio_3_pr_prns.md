<div class='question question'>

If $750 \ \text{grams}$ of pasta cost $90 \text{p}$ find the cost of $1 \ \text{kilogram}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let price of pasta $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
750 \ \text{grams} : 90 \ \text{pence}          &= 1000 \ \text{grams} : a \ \text{pence} \\\\
\dfrac{750} {90}                                &= \dfrac {1000} {a} \\\\
750 \times a                                    &= 90 \times 1000 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {90 \times 1000} {750} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {3 \times 30 \times 250 \times 4} {3 \times 250} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 30 \times \cancel {250} \times 4} {\cancel 3 \times \cancel {250}} \\\\
a                                               &= 30 \times 4 \\\\
a                                               &= 120 \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

Price of $1 \ \text{kilogram}$ of pasta $= 120 \ \text{pence}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{pasta}                                  &:& \text{cost} \\\\
= & 750 \ \text{g}                                &:& 90 \text{p} \\\\
= & 1                                             &:& \dfrac {90} {750} \\\\
= & 1000 \ \text{g}                               &:& \dfrac {90 \times 1000} {750} \\\\
= & \dfrac {1000} {1000} \ \text{kg}              &:& \dfrac {3 \times 30 \times 250 \times 4} {3 \times 250} \\\\
= & \dfrac {1 \cancel {000}} {1 \cancel {000}}    &:& \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 30 \times \cancel {250} \times 4} {\cancel 3 \times \cancel {250}} \\\\
= & 1                                             &:& 30 \times 4 \\\\
= & 1 \ \text{kg}                                 &:& 120 \text{p}
\end{array}
$

Price of $1 \ \text{kilogram}$ of pasta $= 120 \ \text{pence}$

</div>
</div>

If $7$ tennis lessons cost $\pounds 167.65$ what is the cost of $1$ lesson?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of 1 lesson} &= \dfrac {167.65} {7} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {16765} {7 \times 100} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2395 \times 7} {7 \times 100} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2395 \times \cancel {7} } {\cancel {7} \times 100} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2395} {100} \\\\
                        &= \pounds 23.95
\end{aligned}
$

Tom and Jerry have a competition.

When Jerry has run $100 \ \text{metres}$, Tom sets off in pursuit.

How many metres will Tom run before he catches Jerry?

<div class='working'>

Let the distance travelled by Tom $= a$

Distance travelled by Jerry $= a + 100$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}

100 \times (a + 100)                            &= a \times 60 \\\\
100a + 10000                                    &= 60a \\\\
100a - 60a                                      &= 10000 \\\\
40a                                             &= 10000 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {10000} {40} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {40 \times 250} {40} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel {40} \times 250} {\cancel {40}} \\\\
a                                               &= 250 \ \text{m}
\end{aligned}
$
