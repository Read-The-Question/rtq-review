<div class='question question'>

A calculator costs $\pounds 1.75$. In 1979 the same calculator cost 24 times as much. How much did the calculator cost in 1979? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost in 1979} &= 24 \times 1.75 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 42
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 42$

</div>
