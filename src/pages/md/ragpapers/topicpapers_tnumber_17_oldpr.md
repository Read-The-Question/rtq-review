$5! \div 3!$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 5! \div 3! \\\\
&= \dfrac {5!} {3!} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 4 \times 3!} {3!} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 4 \times \cancel {3!}} {\cancel {3!}} \\\\
&= 5 \times 4 \\\\
&= 20
\end{aligned}
$
