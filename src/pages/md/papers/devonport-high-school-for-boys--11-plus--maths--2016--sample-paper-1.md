There is $6 \ \text{g}$ of sugar in every $10 \ \text{g}$ of jam. How much sugar is in $500 \ \text{g}$ of jam?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the amount of sugar $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
10 \ \text{grams} : 6 \ \text{grams}            &= 500 \ \text{grams} : a \ \text{grams} \\\\
\dfrac {10} {6}                                 &= \dfrac {500} {a} \\\\
10 \times a                                     &= 500 \times 6 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {500 \times 6} {10} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {50 \cancel 0 \times 6} {1 \cancel 0} \\\\
a                                               &= 50 \times 6 \\\\
a                                               &= 300 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

Sugar $= 300 \ \text{g}$

</div>

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{jam}                              &:& \text{sugar} \\\\
= & 10 \ \text{g}                           &:& 6 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & 10 \times 50                            &:& 6 \times 50 \\\\
= & 500 \ \text{g}                          &:& 300 \ \text{g}
\end{array}
$

Sugar $= 300 \ \text{g}$
