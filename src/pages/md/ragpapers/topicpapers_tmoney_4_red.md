What percentage of the coins are $20 \ \text{pence}$ coins?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Percentage}  &= \dfrac {2} {10} \times 100 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 100} {10} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 10 \cancel {0}} {1 \cancel {0}} \\\\
                    &= 2 \times 10 \\\\
                    &= 20 \%
\end{aligned}
$
