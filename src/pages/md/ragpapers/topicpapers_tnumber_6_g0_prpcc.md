<div class='question question'>

Here are $2$ number machines:

![eltham-college--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-20-00.png](/assets/eltham-college--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-20-00.png "eltham-college--11-plus--maths--2019--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-20-00.png")

Both machines have the same input.
Work out the input if both machines also have the same output.

- $2$
- $-2$
- $6$
- $-6$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Output of machine A       $= (n \times 4) + 4$

Output of machine B       $= (n \times 5) - 2$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
(n \times 4) + 4                                 &= (n \times 5) - 2 \\\\
4n + 4                                           &= 5n -2 \\\\
4 + 2                                            &= 5n - 4n \\\\
n                                                &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
