<div class='question question'>

If the probability of it raining tomorrow is $0.08$, what is the probability of it not raining tomorrow?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Probabilities ALWAYS add up to $1$.


$
\begin {aligned} 
\text {P(Not raining)}    &= 1 - \text {P(Raining tomorrow)} \\\\
                                      &= 1 - 0.08 \\\\
                                      &= 0.92
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$0.92$

<div class='question question'>

I flip a fair coin that has the number $3$ on one side and the number $7$ on the other side.
At the same time I roll a fair four sided dice that has the numbers $1$ to $4$ on it.
Then I add the two numbers. If I do this $50$ times how many times would you expect me to get a total of $7?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Possible arrangements for 7}      &= (3, 4) \\\\
\text{Total events}                     &= 2 \times 4 \\\\
\text{P(7)}                             &= \dfrac {1} {2 \times 4} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
50 \times \text{P(7)}                   &= 50 \times \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {50} {8} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 25} {2 \times 4} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 25} {\cancel{2} \times 4} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {25} {4} \\\\
                                        &= 6.25 \\\\
                                        &\approx 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6$

</div>
</div>

and one ball is chosen at random.

Circle which of the following is more likely:

| The ball shows a multiple of $3$ | The ball shows an even number |
|:------------------------------:|:-----------------------------:|

Explain your answer clearly.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{even number}          &= 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 \\\\
\text{P(even number)}       &= \dfrac {6} {12} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {6} {2 \times 6} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {6}} {2 \times \cancel {6}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
\text{multiples of 3}       &= 3, 6, 9, 12 \\\\
\text{P(multiples of 3)}    &= \dfrac {4} {12} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {4} {3 \times 4} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {4}} {3 \times \cancel {4}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end{aligned}
$

More likely: The ball shows an even number.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

The ball shows an even number.

</div>
</div>
