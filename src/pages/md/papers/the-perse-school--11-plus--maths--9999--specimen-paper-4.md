Fill in the missing digits to complete the following multiplication:
$2005 = \boxed{5}\:\:\:\times \:\:\:\Box\Box\Box$

a       &= \dfrac {\cancel {5} \times 401} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
a       &= 401
\end{aligned}
$

$401$
