Find three consecutive numbers with a sum of $36$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Second consecutive number $= a + 1$

Third consecutive number $= a + 2$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + (a+1) + (a+2)  &= 36 \\\\
3a + 3             &= 36 \\\\
3a                 &= 36 - 3 \\\\
3a                 &= 33 \\\\
a                  &= \dfrac {33} {3} \\\\
a                  &= \dfrac {3 \times 11} {3} \\\\
a                  &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 11} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
a                  &= 11
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {First number} 				 &= 11 \\\\
\text {Second consecutive number is} &= 11 + 1 \\\\
									 &= 12 \\\\
\text {Third consecutive number is}  &= 12 + 1 \\\\
									 &= 13
\end{aligned}
$

The three consecutive numbers are $11, 12, 13$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$11, 12, 13$

                    &= \dfrac {65 \times 600} {100} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {65 \times 6 \cancel {00}} {1 \cancel {00}} \\\\
                    &= 65 \times 6 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 390
\end{aligned}
$

                    &= 600 - \dfrac {35} {100} \times 600 \\\\
                    &= 600 - \dfrac {35 \times 600} {100} \\\\
                    &= 600 - \dfrac {35 \times 6 \cancel {00}} {1 \cancel {00}} \\\\
                    &= 600 - 35 \times 6 \\\\
                    &= 600 - 210 \\\\
