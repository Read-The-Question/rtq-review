<div class='question question'>

A swimming pool has a length that is four times of its width.

The length and width are both multiples of $5$.

The perimeter is less than $150 \ \text{m}$.

How many different widths could it have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the length $= l$

Let the width $= w$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
l                &= 4b \\\\
2 \times (l + b) &< 150 \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac{150} {2} \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac {75 \times 2} {2} \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac {75 \times  \cancel 2} {\cancel 2} \\\\
l + b            &< 75 \\\\
4b + b           &< 75 \\\\
5b               &< 75 \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {75} {5} \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {5 \times 15} {5} \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 15} {\cancel 5} \\\\
b                &< 15
\end{aligned}
$

Length and width are multiples of $5$

Possible values of b $= 5, 10$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

<div class='question question'>

A cube has six faces.

Theo builds a bigger cube out of $27$ little cubes.

He paints the outside of the big cube and lets the paint dry.

He then breaks it up into the original $27$ cubes.

What is the total number of **unpainted** faces on the little cubes? Show clearly how you get your answer. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total number of faces}            &= 27 \times 6 \\\\
                                        &= 162 \\\\
\text{Total number of faces painted}    &= (3 \times 3) \times 6 \\\\
                                        &= 9 \times 6 \\\\
                                        &= 54 
\end{aligned}
$

\begin{aligned}
\text{Total number of faces not painted}    &= \text{Total number of faces} - \text{Total number of faces painted} \\\\
                                            &= 162 - 54 \\\\
                                            &= 108 
\end{aligned}
$
