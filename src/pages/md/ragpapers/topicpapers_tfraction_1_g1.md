Write (as a **decimal**) the difference between $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{7}{10}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{7}{10} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 5}{4 \times 5} - \dfrac{7 \times 2}{10 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{15}{20} - \dfrac{14}{20} \\\\
&= \dfrac{15-14}{20} \\\\
&= \dfrac{1}{20} \\\\
&= 0.05
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$0.05$

$A + B$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
F     &= \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
B     &= \dfrac {1} {2} - \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {4} {2 \times 4} - \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {4} {8} - \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {4 - 1} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {3} {8} \\\\
A + B &= \dfrac {1} {4} + \dfrac {3} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {2} {4 \times 2} + \dfrac {3} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {2} {8} + \dfrac {3} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {2 + 3} {8} \\\\
      &= \dfrac {5} {8} 
\end{aligned}
$
