<div class='question subquestion'>

How many sticks are needed to make the pattern with eight boxes?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR:  Let's find the relation between the consecutive terms of the sequence.


The number of sticks is calculated by adding $5$ to the previous number of sticks.

This is an arithmetic sequence too and the difference between the consecutive patterns of the sequence is constant.

The next pattern is made by adding multiple of $5$ sticks to the first pattern and the number of times it is added is based on the position in the sequence.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of sticks} &= 6 + (8 - 1) \times 5 \\\\
                        &= 6 + 7 \times 5 \\\\
                        &= 6 + 35 \\\\
                        &= 41
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$41$

What is the rule of the above sequence?

- $4n - 17$
- $17 - 4n$
- $-7n + 13$
- $5 - 3n$
- $9n + 4$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

This is an arithmetic sequence and the difference between the consecutive patterns of the sequence is constant.

The next terms is calculated by adding multiple of $-4$ to the first term and the number of times it is added is based on the position in the sequence.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 13 + (-4) \times (n-1)\\\\
&= 13 - 4n + 4 \\\\
&= 13 + 4 - 4n \\\\
&= 17 - 4n
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>

</div>
