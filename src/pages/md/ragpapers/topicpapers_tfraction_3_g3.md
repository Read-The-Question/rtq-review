<div class='question question'>

What is three-quarters of seven eighths? 

Write your answer in words. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac{3} {4} \times \dfrac {7} {8} \\\\
&= \dfrac{3 \times 7}{4 \times 8} \\\\
&= \dfrac{21}{32} \\\\
&= \text{twenty one over thirty two}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

twenty-one over thirty-two
