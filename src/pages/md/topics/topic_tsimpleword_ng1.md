**Question**

The water cooler in the staff room contains $5 \ \text{litres}$ of water.

How many $200 \ \text{ml}$ glasses of water can it fill before it needs replacing?

**Working**

The capacity of cooler is given in litres and capacity of glasses is given ml. Let's convert capacity of cooler in ml.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total water}       &= 5 \ \text{litres} \\\\
                         &= 5 \times 1000 \ \text{ml} \\\\
                         &= 5000 \ \text{ml}
\end{aligned}
$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of glasses} &= \dfrac {\text{Total Water in cooler}} {\text{Water in a glass}} \\\\
                         &= \dfrac {5000} {200} \\\\
                         &= \dfrac {200 \times 25} {200} \\\\
                         &= \dfrac {\cancel {200} \times 25} {\cancel {200}} \\\\
                         &= 25
\end{aligned}
$
</div>

<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

<div class="question" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Question**

George plants a tree in his garden which is initially $180 \ \text{cm}$ high.

After the first year it has increased in height by $30\%$. During the following year its height increased by $25\%$.

How tall is the tree at the end of the second year?

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">
<strong style="display: block; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Question Raw</strong>
</div>
<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex">George plants a tree in his garden which is initially $180 \ \text{cm}$ high.
<emptyline>
After the first year it has increased in height by $30\%$. During the following year its height increased by $25\%$.
<emptyline>
How tall is the tree at the end of the second year?
</code></pre>
</div>
<div class="workings">
<div class="tips placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Tips**

<div class="tip">
%empty%
</div>
</div>
<div class="formulas placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Formulas**

<div class="formula">
%empty%
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

If the height increased by $30 \%$, then the new height will be $100 + 30 = 130 \%$ of the previous height. Using that, we get

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Tree height after first year}  &= \dfrac {130} {100} \times 180 \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac {130 \times 180} {100} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac {13\cancel{0} \times 18\cancel{0}} {1\cancel{0}\cancel{0}} \\\\
                                      &= 13 \times 18 \\\\
                                      &= 234 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

If the height increased by $25 \%$, then the new height will be $100 + 25 = 125 \%$ of the previous height. Using that, we get

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Tree height after second year} &= \dfrac {125} {100} \times 234 \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac {125 \times 234} {100} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac {29250} {100} \\\\
                                      &= 292.5 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Tree height at the end of the second year $= 292.5$ cm.
</div>
<div class="tips" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Tips**

<div class="tip">

<div class="formulas placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

<div class="formula">
%empty%
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
