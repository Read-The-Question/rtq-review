<div class='question subquestion'>

Ann and Ben share $28$ sweets in the ratio 4 : 3

(Ann has $4$ sweets for every $3$ sweets that Ben has). 

How many sweets do they each receive?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let number of sweets received by Ann $= a$

Number of sweets received by Ben $= 28 - a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \ \text{Ann} :3 \ \text{Ben}                  &= a \ \text{Ann} : (28 - a) \ \text{Ben} \\\\
\dfrac{4} {3}                                   &= \dfrac {a} {28 - a} \\\\
4 \times (28 - a)                               &= a \times 3 \\\\
4 \times 28 - 4a                                &= 3a \\\\
112 - 4a                                        &= 3a \\\\
112                                             &= 3a + 4a \\\\
3a + 4a                                         &= 112 \\\\
7a                                              &= 112 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {112} {7} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {7 \times 16} {7} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 7 \times 16} {\cancel 7} \\\\
a                                               &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of sweets received by Ann}     &= 16 \\\\
\text{Number of sweets received by Ben}     &= 28 - 16 \\\\
                                            &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$16, 12$

</div>
</div>

A necklace is made from black and white beads in a ratio of 3 : 2, in other words for every $3$ black beads there were $2$ white beads.

● ● ● ○ ○ ● ● ● ○ ○

240 - 3a                                        &= 2a \\\\

2a + 3a                                         &= 240 \\\\
5a                                              &= 240 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {240} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5 \times 48} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 48} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                                               &= 48
\end{aligned}
$

Number of black beads $= 48$

<div class='question question'>

Jason and Patrick win a prize of $\pounds 75$ for their entry into an inventing competition. For every $2$ hours that Patrick worked on the project Jason worked $3$, so it is decided that for every $\pounds 2$ that Patrick receives Jason should receive $\pounds 3$. How much will Jason and Patrick receive? Write your 
answers in the boxes below. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money received by Jason $= a$

Money received by Patrick $= 75 - a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \ \text{Patrick} : 3 \ \text{Jason}           &= (75 - a) \ \text{Patrick} : a \ \text{Jason} \\\\
\dfrac {2} {3}                                  &= \dfrac {75 - a} {a} \\\\
2a                                              &= 3 \times (75 - a) \\\\
2a                                              &= 3 \times 75 - 3a \\\\
2a                                              &= 225 - 3a \\\\
2a + 3a                                         &= 225 \\\\
5a                                              &= 225 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {225} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {5 \times 45} {5} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 45} {\cancel 5} \\\\
a                                               &= \pounds 45
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Money received by Jason}        &= \pounds 45 \\\\
\text{Money received by Patrick}      &= 75 - 45 \\\\
                                      &= \pounds 30
\end{aligned}
$

$\pounds 45, \pounds 30$
