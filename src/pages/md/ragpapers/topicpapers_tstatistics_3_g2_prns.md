<div class='question subquestion'>

Two numbers have a mean of $12$ and a range of $6$. 

What are the two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


ABBR: $\text{Range} = \text{Highest number} - \text{Lowest number}$


Let the first number $= a$

Let the second number $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\dfrac {a + b} {2}      &= 12 \\\\
a + b                   &= 12 \times 2 \\\\
a + b                   &= 24 & (1) \\\\
b - a                   &= 6  & (2)
\end {aligned}
$

Let's use elimination to reduce it to just one variable. Add the two equations to eliminate $a$.

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b                   &= 24 \\\\
b - a                   &= 6  \\\\
a + b + b -a            &= 24 + 6 \\\\
2b                      &= 30 \\\\
b                       &= \dfrac {30} {2} \\\\
b                       &= 15
\end {aligned}
$

Let's substitute the value of $b$ in one of the initial equations to find the value of $a$.

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b                       &= 24  \\\\
a + 15                      &= 24 \\\\
a                           &= 24 - 15 \\\\
a                           &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

$9$

<div class='question question'>

On a wet week in Rusholme the mean (or average) rainfall over the five days from Monday to Friday was $12 \ \text{mm}$. 
On Monday, when the rain was heaviest there was $22 \ \text{mm}$, but on Friday there was only $5 \ \text{mm}$ of rain.  

What was the mean rainfall on the other three days?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


Let the average rainfall for other three days $= r$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {22 + 3r + 5} {5}                     &= 12 \\\\
\dfrac {27 + 3r} {5}                         &= 12 \\\\
27 + 3r                                      &= 12 \times 5 \\\\
27 + 3r                                      &= 60 \\\\
3r                                           &= 60 - 27 \\\\
3r                                           &= 33 \\\\
r                                            &= \dfrac {33} {3} \\\\
r                                            &= \dfrac {11 \times 3} {3} \\\\
r                                            &= \dfrac {11 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
r                                            &= 11 \ \text{mm}
\end{aligned}
$

Average rainfall $= 11 \ \text{mm}$
