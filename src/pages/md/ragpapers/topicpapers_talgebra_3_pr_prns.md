<div class='question question'>

A ruler costs *k* pence, and a pen costs *m* pence.

Match each statement with the correct expression for the amount in pence.

The first one is done for you.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cost of 5 rulers and 5 pens}        &= 5 \times k + 5 \times m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                &= 5 (k + m) \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Difference between cost of 5 pens and 5 rulers}       &= 5 \times m - 5 \times k \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                            &= 5m - 5k \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Change from} \ \pounds \text{5 after buying 5 pens}        &= \pounds 5 - 5 \times m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                            &= 5 \times 100 - 5m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                            &= 500 - 5m \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Total cost of $5$ pens and $5$ rulers $= 5 (k + m) \ \text{pence}$.

Difference between cost of $5$ pens from $5$ rulers $= 5m - 5k \ \text{pence}$.

Change from $\pounds 5$ after buying $5$ pens $= 500 - 5m \ \text{pence}$.

</div>

<div class='question question'>

The numbers in the following shapes must follow the rule:

To find the number in the circle, multiply the number in the triangle by $3$, multiply the number in the square by $4$ and find the difference

Example: 

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


Work out the missing values:

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Value in the circle}      &= 3 \times 6 - 4 \times 2 \\\\
                                &= 18 - 8 \\\\
                                &= 10
\end{aligned}
$

Let the value inside the square $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times a - 3 \times 7         &= 7 \\\\
4a - 21                         &= 7 \\\\
4a                              &= 21 + 7 \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {28} {4} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {4 \times 7} {4} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 7} {\cancel 4} \\\\
a                               &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Let the value inside the triangle $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
3 \times a - 4 \times 2         &= 40 \\\\
3a - 8                          &= 40 \\\\
3a                              &= 40 + 8 \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {48} {3} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {3 \times 16} {3} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 16} {\cancel 3} \\\\
a                               &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

Let the value inside the square and triangle $= a \ \text{and} \ b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times a - 3 \times b         &= 13 \\\\
4a                              &= 13 + 3b
\end{aligned}
$

|  Value of b  |  Value of ($13 + 3b$)  |
|:------------:|:----------------------:|
|     $0$      | $13 + 3 \times 0 = 13$ |
|     $1$      | $13 + 3 \times 1 = 16$ |

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Value of a}       &= \dfrac {16} {4} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {4 \times 4} {4} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
                        &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Value inside circle $= 10$

Value inside square $= 7$

Value inside triangle $= 16$

Value inside square and triangle $= 4 \ \text{and} \ 1$

</div>
