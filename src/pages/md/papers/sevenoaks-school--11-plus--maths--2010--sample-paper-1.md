A sunflower is one hundred and fifty centimetres tall.

How tall will it be if its height increases by ten per cent?

</div>

Using this we get,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{New height}  &= 110 \% \text { of} \ 150 \\\\
                   &= \dfrac {110} {100} \times 150 \\\\
                   &= \dfrac {110 \times 150} {100} \\\\
                   &= \dfrac {11 \cancel {0} \times 15 \cancel {0}} {1 \cancel {0} \cancel {0}} \\\\
                   &= 11 \times 15 \\\\
                   &= 165 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Bella makes purple paint by mixing blue paint and red paint in the ratio of $4 : 3$. How much blue paint is needed to make $21 \ \text{litres}$ of purple paint?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the volume of blue paint $= b$

Volume of red paint $= 21 - b$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
b \ \text{litres} : (21 - b) \ \text{litres}       &= 4 \ \text{litres} : 3 \ \text{litres} \\\\
\dfrac {b} {21 - b}                                &= \dfrac {4} {3} \\\\
3 \times b                                         &= 4 \times (21 - b) \\\\
3b                                                 &= 84 - 4b \\\\
3b + 4b                                            &= 84 \\\\
7b                                                 &= 84 \\\\
b                                                  &= \dfrac {84} {7} \\\\
b                                                  &= \dfrac {7 \times 12} {7} \\\\
b                                                  &= \dfrac {\cancel 7 \times 12} {\cancel 7} \\\\
b                                                  &= 12 \ \text{litres}
\end{aligned}
$

Blue paint needed $= 12 \ \text{litres}$
