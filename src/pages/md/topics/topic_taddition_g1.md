</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Money spent} &= \pounds 4.93 + 24 \text{p} + 58 \text{p} \\\\
                   &= \pounds 4.93 + \pounds \dfrac {24} {100} + \pounds \dfrac {58} {100} \\\\
                   &= \pounds 4.93 + \pounds 0.24 + \pounds 0.58 \\\\
                   &= \pounds 5.75
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
