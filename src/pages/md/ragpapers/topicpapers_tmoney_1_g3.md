<div class='question question'>

Decrease $\pounds 44$ by $10\%$.

- $\pounds 39.60$
- $\pounds 4.40$
- $\pounds 48.40$
- $\pounds 34$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

If the amount is reduced by $10 \%$, then the reduced value will be $100 - 10 = 90 \%$ of the original value. Using that, we get

$
\begin {aligned}
&= 90 \% \ \text {of} \ \text 44 \\\\
&= \dfrac {90} {100} \times 44 \\\\
&= \dfrac {90 \times 44} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3960} {100} \\\\
&= \pounds 39.60 
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= 44 - 10 \% \ \text {of} \ 44 \\\\
&= 44 - \dfrac {10} {100} \times 44 \\\\
&= 44 - \dfrac {10 \times 44} {100} \\\\
&= 44 - \dfrac {440} {100} \\\\
&= 44 - 4.40 \\\\
&= \pounds 39.60 
\end {aligned}
$

</div>

A can of lemonade from a vending machine costs $65 \ \text{p}$. 
The table below shows the coins that were collected from the machine in one day.

|      Coins      | Number of Coins |
|:---------------:|:---------------:|
| $50 \ \text{p}$   |      $22$       |
| $20 \ \text{p}$   |      $18$       |
| $10 \ \text{p}$   |      $33$       |
|  $5 \ \text{p}$   |      $41$       |

How many cans of lemonade were sold that day?

-   $\pounds 20.85$ 
-   $\pounds 19.95$
-   $31$ 
-   $30$

\text {Number of cans sold}     &= \dfrac {1995} {65} \\\\
                                &= 30 \ \text {remainder} \ 5 \\\\
                                &\approx 30
\end {aligned}
$

I buy $7$ drinks at $72\ \text{p}$ each, and $9$ sandwiches at $\pounds 1.21$ each. 

How much change do I get from a $\pounds 20$ note?

                            &= 5.04 + 10.89 \\\\
                            &= \pounds 15.93 \\\\
\text {Change received}     &= 20 - 15.93 \\\\
