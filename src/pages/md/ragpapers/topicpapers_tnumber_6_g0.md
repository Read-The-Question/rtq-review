<div class='question question'>

A hexagon is worth $6$ points.
A square is worth $4$ points.
How much is triangle worth?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


-  Can't tell
-  $1$
-  $2$
-  $4$


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the worth of triangle $= t$

Let the worth of circle $= c$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
6 + 4 + 6 + c                                  &= 6 + c + 4 + 4 + t + t \\\\
16 + c                                         &= 14 + c + 2t \\\\
16 + c - c                                     &= 14 + 2t \\\\
16                                             &= 14 + 2t \\\\
16 - 14                                        &= 2t \\\\
2t                                             &= 2 \\\\
t                                              &= \dfrac {2} {2} \\\\
t                                              &= 1
\end{aligned}
$

Worth of triangle $= 1$ point
