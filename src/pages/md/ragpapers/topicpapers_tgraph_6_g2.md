**Estimate** the amount of Brie sold by Tosco in 1 week.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text{Brie sold by Tosco}               &= \dfrac {90} {360} \times 300 \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {90 \times 300} {360} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {90 \times 4 \times 75} {90 \times 4} \\\\
                                        &= \dfrac {\cancel {90} \times \cancel 4 \times 75} {\cancel {90} \times \cancel 4} \\\\
                                        &= 75 \ \text{kg}
\end {aligned}
$

What is the average (mean) number of pizzas sold per day?

$
