<div class='question question'>

Find $5\%$ of $24$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 5 \% \text{ of } 24 \\\\
&= \dfrac {5} {100} \times 24 \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 24} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 4 \times 6} {5 \times 5 \times 4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times \cancel 4 \times 6} {\cancel 5 \times 5 \times \cancel 4} \\\\
&=\dfrac  {6}{5}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {6} {5}$

</div>
