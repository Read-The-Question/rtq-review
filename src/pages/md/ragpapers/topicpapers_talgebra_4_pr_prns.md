\begin {aligned}
(2 \ \clubsuit \ 3) \ \clubsuit \ 4 &= (2^2 + 3) \ \clubsuit \ 4 \\
&= 7 \ \clubsuit \ 4 \\
&= 7^2 + 4 \\
&= 49 + 4 \\
&= 53 
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

Rosie's plant doubles its height every day.

It is $4 \ \text{cm}$ tall on Monday. On what day does it become taller than two metres?

</div>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
4 \times 2^n &>200 \\\\
2^n &> \dfrac {200} {4} \\\\
2^n &> \dfrac {50 \times 4} {4} \\\\
2^n &> \dfrac {50 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
2^n &> 50 \\\\
n       &= 6 
\end {aligned}
$

The day it become taller than two metres $= \text{Monday} + 6 \ \text{days} = \text{Sunday}$
