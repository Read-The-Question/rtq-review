$
According to question,

1 flapjack need

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {100} {20} \\\\
&= 5
\end{aligned}
$

50 flapjacks will need, 

\begin{aligned}
&= 5 \times 50 \\\\
&= 250 \ \text {g}
\end{aligned}

To make healthier flapjacks, you can use $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the recommended amount of sugar.

&= \dfrac {3} {4} \times 200 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 4 \times 50} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel4 \times 50} {\cancel4} \\\\
&= 150 \ \text {grams}
\end{aligned}
