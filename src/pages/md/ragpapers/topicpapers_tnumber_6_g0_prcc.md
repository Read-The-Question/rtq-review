A hexagon is worth $6$ points.
A square is worth $4$ points.
How much is triangle worth?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


-  Can't tell
-  $1$
-  $2$
-  $4$


</div>

<div class='working'>

Let the worth of circle $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
6 + 4 + 6 + b                                  &= 6 + b + 4 + 4 + a + a \\\\
16 + b                                         &= 14 + b + 2a \\\\
16 + b - b                                     &= 14 + 2a \\\\
16                                             &= 14 + 2a \\\\
16 - 14                                        &= 2a \\\\
2a                                             &= 2 \\\\
a                                              &= \dfrac {2} {2} \\\\
a                                              &= 1
\end{aligned}
$
