<div class='question question'>

A coat cost $\pounds 125$ and was then reduced by $20\%$ in a sale. What is the new price?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {New price}       &= \text {Original cost} - \text {Sale Discount} \\\\
                        &= \pounds 125 - 20 \% \ \text {of} \ \pounds 125 \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20} {100} \times 125 \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20 \times 125} {100} \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {20 \times 5 \times 25} {20 \times 5} \\\\
                        &= 125 - \dfrac {\cancel {20} \times \cancel {5} \times 25} {\cancel {20} \times \cancel {5}} \\\\
                        &= 125 - 25 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 100
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 100$

</div>
</div>
