A pack of $15$ pens cost $\pounds 4.65$.
Pens are the same value if they are bought individually.

What is the correct calculation to wrk out the cost of $19$ pens?

-  $(4.65 \times 15) \div 19$
-  $(15 \div 4.65) \times 19$
-  $(4.65 \div 15) \times 19$
-  $(15 \div 4.65) \div 19$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of 15 pens}      &= \pounds 4.65 \\\\
\text{Cost of 1 pen}        &= 4.65 \div 15 \\\\
\text{Cost of 19 pens}      &= (4.65 \div 15) \times 19
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>C</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Calculators normally cost $\pounds 6$ and protractors normally cost $30 \text{p}$.

In a sale, calculators have been reduced by a quarter and protractors are half price.

David bought $8$ calculators and $20$ protractors.

How much did he have to pay? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

If the calculator cost is reduced by one quarter, then the sale's calculator cost will be 1 - 1/4 = 3/4 of the original cost.

If the protractor cost is reduced by half, then the sale's protractor cost will be 1 - 1/2 = 1/2 of the original cost.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cost}   &= 8 \times (\dfrac {3} {4} \times \pounds 6) + 20 \times (\dfrac {1} {2} \times 30 \text {p}) \\\\
                    &= 8 \times (\dfrac{3}{4} \times \pounds 6) + 20 \times (\dfrac {1} {2} \times \pounds 0.3) \\\\
                    &= (\dfrac{8 \times 3 \times 6}{4}) + (\dfrac {20 \times 1 \times 0.3}{2}) \\\\
                    &= (\dfrac{4 \times 2 \times 3 \times 6}{4}) + (\dfrac {10 \times 2 \times 1 \times 0.3}{2}) \\\\
                    &= (\dfrac{\cancel{4} \times 2 \times 3 \times 6}{\cancel{4}}) + (\dfrac {10 \times \cancel{2} \times 1 \times 0.3}{\cancel{2}}) \\\\
                    &= 2 \times 3 \times 6 + 10 \times 1 \times 0.3 \\\\
                    &= 36 + 3 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 39
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 39$

</div>
</div>
