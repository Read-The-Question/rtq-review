Write down a $\underline{fraction}$ between $0.5$ and $0.6$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Half-way between two numbers is the same as finding the average of two numbers.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {0.5 + 0.6} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1.1} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1.1 \times 10} {2 \times 10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {11} {20}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {11} {20}$

&= \dfrac {7} {4 \times 2} \\\\

\end{aligned}
