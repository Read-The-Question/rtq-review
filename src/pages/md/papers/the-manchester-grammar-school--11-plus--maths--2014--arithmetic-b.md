A second triangle is isosceles, and one of its angles is $48\degree$. What are the TWO possible pairs of values for the other two angles?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


This can be solved two ways.

**OPTION 1**

Let the two equal angles of the isosceles triangle $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + a + 48           &= 180 \\\\
2a + 48              &= 180 \\\\
2a                   &= 180 - 48 \\\\
2a                   &= 132 \\\\
a                    &= \dfrac {132} {2} \\\\
a                    &= 66 \degree
\end {aligned}
$

Other two angles $= 66 \degree , 66 \degree$


**OPTION 2**

Let the third angle $= b$

Let the two equal angles of the isosceles triangle $= 48 \degree$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
48 + 48 + b    &= 180 \\\\
96 + b         &= 180 \\\\
b              &= 180 - 96 \\\\
b              &= 84 \degree
\end {aligned}
$

Other two angles $= 48 \degree, 84 \degree$

\end{aligned}

We can easily infer the values by using trial and error. Let's find out $2$ numbers which can satisfy the equations above.

| Value of a   |   Value of b      |     5a + 3b    |

|     $1$      |         $2$       |       $11$     |

|     $3$      |         $15$      |       $60$     |         
|     $6$      |         $10$      |       $60$     |
