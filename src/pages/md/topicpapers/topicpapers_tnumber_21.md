&= (\dfrac{3} {6} + \dfrac {2} {6}) \div 2 \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6} \div {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac{5} {12}$
