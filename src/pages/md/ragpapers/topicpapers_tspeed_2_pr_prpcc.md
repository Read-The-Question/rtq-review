<div class='question question'>

Train $A$ leaves Birmingham New Street station at $1747$ travelling due North on the slow line at $90 \ \text{km/hour}$.

How many kilometres has this train travelled when the time is $1817?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$


$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Time taken}  &= 18{:}17 - 17{:}47 \\\\
                    &= 30 \text{ minutes} \\\\
\text{Distance}     &= 30 \times 90 \text{ km/hour} \\\\
                    &= 30 \times \dfrac{90}{60} \text{ km/minutes} \\\\
                    &= 30 \times \dfrac{90}{60} \\\\
                    &= 30 \times \dfrac{3 \times 3 \times 10}{3 \times 2 \times 10} \\\\
                    &= 30 \times \dfrac{3 \times \cancel{3} \times \cancel{10}}{\cancel{3} \times 2 \times \cancel{10}} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{3 \times 30}{2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{3 \times 15 \times 2}{2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{3 \times 15 \times \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2}} \\\\
                    &= 3 \times 15 \\\\
                    &= 45 \text{ km}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$45\text{ km}$

</div>
</div>

                    &= \dfrac{80 \times 6}{6} \\\\

\end {aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Alan and Brian are two runners. Alan runs at $6.6\, \text{m/s}$ and Brian runs at $8.4\, \text{m/s}$. They start from the same place on a circular $400\, \text{m}$ track and run in opposite directions round and round the track.
 

</div>

<li class='questionContainer'>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

How far has Brian run when they pass each other for the first time?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Time} = \dfrac {\text{Distance}} {\text{Speed}}$


Let the time taken (in seconds) $= t$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
6.6 \times t + 8.4 \times t &= 400 \\\\
6.6t + 8.4t                 &= 400 \\\\
15t                         &= 400 \\\\
t                           &= \dfrac{400}{15} \text{ seconds}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Distance covered by brian} &= \dfrac{400}{15} \times 8.4 \\\\
                                 &= \dfrac{40 \times 10 \times 8.4}{15} \\\\
                                 &= \dfrac{40 \times 84}{15} \\\\
                                 &= \dfrac{5 \times 8 \times 28 \times 3}{5 \times 3} \\\\
                                 &= \dfrac{\cancel{5} \times 8 \times 28 \times \cancel{3}}{\cancel{5} \times \cancel{3}} \\\\
                                 &= 8 \times 28 \\\\
                                 &= 224 \text{ metres}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$224$

</div>
</div>
