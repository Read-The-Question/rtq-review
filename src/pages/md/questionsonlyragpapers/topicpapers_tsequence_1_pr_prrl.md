To find the $n \text{th} $ triangular number, you can use this rule.

$n \text{th} $ triangular number $ = \dfrac{n}{2}(n + 1)$

Example:

$ 3 \text{rd} $ triangular number $ \begin{aligned}
    &= \dfrac{3}{2} (3 + 1) \\
    &= 6
\end{aligned}
$
