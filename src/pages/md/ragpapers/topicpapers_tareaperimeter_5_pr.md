Let the width of one cube $= w$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Total Volume}            &= 56 \\\\
7 \times w^3                    &= 56 \\\\
7w^3                            &= 56 \\\\
w^3                             &= \dfrac {56} {7} \\\\
w^3                             &= \dfrac {7 \times 8} {7} \\\\
w^3                             &= \dfrac {\cancel {7} \times 8} {\cancel {7}} \\\\
w^3                             &= 8 \\\\
w                               &= \sqrt[3] {8} \\\\
w                               &= 2 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Width of one cube is $2 \ \text {cm}$.
