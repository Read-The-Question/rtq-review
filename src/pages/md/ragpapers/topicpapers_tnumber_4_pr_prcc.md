<div class='question question'>

 ≈ means approximately equal to. Given that $1\ \text{kilogram (kg)} ≈ 2·2\ \text{pounds (lb)}$, convert $154\ \text{lb}$ to $\text{kg}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 154 \text{ lb} \\\\
&= 154 \times \dfrac{1}{2.2} \text{ kg} \\\\
&= \dfrac{7 \times 22 \times 10}{22} \text{ kg} \\\\
&= \dfrac{7 \times \cancel{22} \times 10}{\cancel{22}} \text{ kg} \\\\
&= 7 \times 10 \text{ kg} \\\\
&= 70 \text{ kg}
\end{aligned} 
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>
