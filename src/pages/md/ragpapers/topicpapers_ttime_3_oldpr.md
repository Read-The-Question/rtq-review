                     &= (4 \ \text{hours} +   \dfrac {15} {60} \ \text{hours} ) \times 6 \\\\
                     &= 4 \times 6 + \dfrac {15 \times 6} {60} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {5 \times 3 \times 6} {5 \times 2 \times 6} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 3 \times \cancel 6} {\cancel 5 \times 2 \times \cancel 6} \\\\
                     &= 24 + \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
                     &= 24 + 1.5 \\\\ 
                     &= \pounds 25.5
\end{aligned}
$
