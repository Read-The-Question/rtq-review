<div class='question subquestion'>

Calculate the quantities needed to make $16$ cupcakes.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{pancakes}   &:& \text{flour}                                                                            &:& \text{eggs}                                                                 &:& \text{butter}                                                                         &:& \text{sugar} \\\\
= & 12                &:& 180 \ \text{g}                                                                          &:& 3                                                                           &:& 150 \ \text{g}                                                                        &:& 120 \ \text{g} \\\\
= & 1                 &:& \dfrac {180} {12}                                                                       &:& \dfrac {3} {12}                                                             &:& \dfrac {150} {12}                                                                     &:& \dfrac {120} {12} \\\\
= & 16                &:& \dfrac {180 \times 16} {12}                                                             &:& \dfrac {3 \times 16} {12}                                                   &:& \dfrac {150 \times 16} {12}                                                           &:& \dfrac {120 \times 16} {12} \\\\
= & 16                &:& \dfrac {3 \times 60 \times 4 \times 4} {4 \times 3}                                     &:& \dfrac {3 \times 4 \times 4} {4 \times 3}                                   &:& \dfrac {50 \times 3 \times 4 \times 4} {4 \times 3}                                   &:& \dfrac {12 \times 10 \times 16} {12} \\\\
= & 16                &:& \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 60 \times 4 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4 \times \cancel 3}     &:& \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 4 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4 \times \cancel 3}   &:& \dfrac {50 \times \cancel 3 \times 4 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4 \times \cancel 3}   &:& \dfrac {\cancel {12} \times 10 \times 16} {\cancel {12}} \\\\
= & 16                &:& 60 \times 4                                                                             &:& 4                                                                           &:& 50 \times 4                                                                           &:& 10 \times 16 \\\\
= & 16                &:& 240 \ \text{g}                                                                          &:& 4                                                                           &:& 200 \ \text{g}                                                                        &:& 160 \ \text{g} \\\\
\end{array}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Sugar $= 160 \ \text{g}$

Eggs $= 4$

Butter $= 200 \ \text{g}$

Flour $= 240 \ \text{g}$

= & 7                 &:& \dfrac {12 \times 7} {3} \\\\

= & 7                 &:& \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 4 \times 7} {\cancel 3} \\\\
= & 7                 &:& 4 \times 7 \\\\
