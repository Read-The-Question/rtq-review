Which of these gives the answer $15$?

- $24 \div 7 + 3$
- $3 + 7 \div 24$
- $7 + 3 \div 24$
- $3 \div 7 + 24$
- $24 \div 3 + 7$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text{A}    &= 24 \div 7 + 3 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {24} {7} + 3 \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24} {7} + \dfrac {7 \times 3} {7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24} {7} + \dfrac {21} {7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{24 + 21}{7} \\\\
            &= \dfrac{45}{7} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{B}    &= 3 + 7 \div 24 \\\\
            &= 3 + \dfrac {7} {24} \\\\
            &= 3 \dfrac{7}{24} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{C}    &= 7 + 3 \div 24 \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {3} {24} \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {3} {3 \times 8} \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {\cancel {3}} {\cancel {3} \times 8} \\\\
            &= 7 + \dfrac {1} {8} \\\\
            &= 7 \dfrac{1}{8} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{D}    &= 3 \div 7 + 24 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {3} {7} + 24 \\\\
            &= 24 \dfrac {3}{7} \neq \red{15} \\\\
\text{E}    &= 24 \div 3 + 7 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {24} {3} + 7 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {3 \times 8} {3} + 7 \\\\
            &= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 8} {\cancel {3}} + 7 \\\\
            &= 8 + 7 \\\\
            &= \green{15}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>E</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$24 \div 3 + 7$

</div>
</div>
