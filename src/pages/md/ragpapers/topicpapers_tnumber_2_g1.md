<div class='question subquestion'>

What number does $m$ stand for, if $m * 6 = 41?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
m * 6            &= 41 \\\\
5 \times m + 6 &= 41 \\\\
5m               &= 41 - 6 \\\\
5m               &= 35 \\\\
m                &= \dfrac{35}{5} \\\\
m                &= \dfrac{5 \times 7}{5} \\\\
m                &= \dfrac{\cancel{5} \times 7}{\cancel{5}} \\\\
m                &= 7
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$7$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

If $n * 4 = 8 * n$, what number does $n$ stand for?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
n * 4            &= 8 * n \\\\
5 \times n + 4 &= 5 \times 8 + n \\\\
5n + 4           &= 40 + n \\\\
5n - n           &= 40 - 4 \\\\
4n               &= 36 \\\\
n                &= \dfrac{36}{4} \\\\
n                &= \dfrac{4 \times 9}{4} \\\\
n                &= \dfrac{\cancel{4} \times 9}{\cancel{4}} \\\\
n                &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$9$

</div>
