What is the average of $\dfrac {1}{2}$ and $\dfrac {1}{3}$ ?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= (\dfrac{1} {2} + \dfrac {1} {3}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac{3} {2 \times 3} + \dfrac {2} {3 \times 2}) \div 2 \\\\
&= (\dfrac{3} {6} + \dfrac {2} {6}) \div 2 \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6} \div {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {6 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{5} {12}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac{5} {12}$

</div>
</div>

I am thinking of two numbers. 

If I double the first and add the second, my answer is $35$. 

If I double the second and subtract the first, my answer is $15$.

By forming and solving two simultaneous equations, find the two numbers.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Let the second number $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 35 \\\\
2b - a &= 15
\end{aligned}
$

First, let's find a

$
\begin{aligned}
2b - a          &= 15 \\\\
2(35 - 2a) - a  &= 15 \\\\
70 - 4a - a    &= 15 \\\\
70 - 5a        &= 15 \\\\
70 -15          &= 5a \\\\
5a             &= 55 \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {55} {5} \\\\
a               &= 11
\end{aligned}
$

Now, let's find b

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 35 \\\\
2 \times 11 + b &= 35 \\\\
22 + b &= 35 \\\\
b &= 35 - 22 \\\\
b &= 13
\end{aligned}
$

\end{aligned}
