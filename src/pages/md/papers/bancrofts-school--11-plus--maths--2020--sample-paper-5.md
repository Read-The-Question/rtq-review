What is the square's area?

<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of square} = \text{side} \times \text{side}$


Let the side of the square $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times a          &= 20 \\\\
4a                  &= 20 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {20} {4} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {4 \times 5} {4} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 5} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
a                   &= 5 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find the area of the square.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Area}            &= a \times a \\\\
                        &= 5 \times 5 \\\\
                        &= 25 \ \text{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$
