**Working**

The next term is calculated by adding $0.2$ to the previous term. Hence, the sequence would be:

$
\begin{matrix}
&   \footnotesize{\maroonC{(+0.2)}} 
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC{(+0.2)}}  
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC{(+0.2)}}   
&&  \footnotesize{\maroonC{(+0.2)}}  \\
\ldots, && 20.0, &&  20.2, &&  20.4
\end{matrix}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Missing number}      &= 20.0 - 0.2 \\\\
                        &= 19.8
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$19.8$
