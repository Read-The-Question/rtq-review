</div>

<div class='working'>

3 a + 6           &= 15 \\\\
3 a               &= 15 - 6 \\\\
3 a               &= 9 \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {9} {3} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {3 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {3}} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
a                 &= 3
$

Three numbers $= 3, \ 5, \ 7$
