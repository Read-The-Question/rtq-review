<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
7000 \div 100                                &= 700 \div a \\\\
\dfrac{7000}{100}                            &= \dfrac{700}{a} \\\\
a \times \dfrac{7000}{100}                   &= 700 \\\\
a                                            &= \dfrac{700 \times 100}{7000} \\\\
a                                            &= \dfrac{7000 \times 10}{7000} \\\\
a                                            &= \dfrac{\cancel{7000} \times 10}{\cancel{7000}} \\\\
70                                           &= 10
\end{aligned}
$

| Add the mother's and father's heights. Divide by $2$. Add $7 \ \text{cm}$ to the result. The boy is likely to be this height, plus or minus $10 \ \text{cm}$. |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|

Marc's mother is $168\ \text{cm}$ tall. His father is $194\ \text{cm}$ tall.

What is the greatest height Marc is likely to be when he grows up? 

Show your working.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Max height of Marc}           &= \dfrac {1} {2} \times (168 + 194) + 7 + 10 \\\\

                                    &= \dfrac {362} {2} + 17 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 181} {2} + 17 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel 2 \times 181} {\cancel 2} + 17 \\\\
                                    &= 181 + 17 \\\\
                                    &= 198 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

I travel $1\ \text{mile}$ at $60\ \text{m.p.h.}$ and then $1\ \text{mile}$ at $30\ \text{m.p.h.}$ What is my average speed in miles per hour?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text {Speed} = \dfrac {\text{Total distance}} {\text{Total time}}$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Total distance}  &= 1 + 1 \\\\
                        &= 2 \ \text{miles} \\\\
\text {Time 1}          &= \dfrac {1} {60} \ \text{hour} \\\\
\text {Time 2}          &= \dfrac {1} {30} \ \text{hour} \\\\
\text {Total time}      &= \dfrac {1} {60} + \dfrac {1} {30} \\\\
\text {Average speed}   &= \dfrac {2} {\dfrac {1} {60} + \dfrac {1} {30}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {\dfrac {1} {60} + \dfrac {2} {30 \times 2}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {\dfrac {1} {60} + \dfrac {2} {60}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {\dfrac {3} {60}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 60} {3} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 20 \times 3} {3} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 20 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
                        &= 40 \ \text {m.p.h}
\end{aligned}
$
