How many thousands are there in one million?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text{Number of thousands}   &= \dfrac {1000000} {1000} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {1000 \times 1000} {1000} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {1000 \times \cancel{1000}} {\cancel{1000}} \\\\
                            &= 1000
\end {aligned}
$

How much is three-sevenths of $196$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {3} {7} \ \text {of} \ 196 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {7} \times {196} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 196} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 28 \times 7} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 28 \times \cancel {7}} {\cancel{7}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 28 \\\\
&= 84
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$84$
