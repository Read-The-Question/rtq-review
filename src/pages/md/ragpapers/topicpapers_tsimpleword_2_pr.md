<div class='question question'>

A minibus carries $16$ pupils.

How many buses would be needed to take $300$ pupils?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of bused required} &= \dfrac {300} {16} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {4 \times 75} {4 \times 4} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 75} {\cancel 4 \times 4} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac {75} {4} \\\\
                                &= 18 \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\
                                &= 19
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
