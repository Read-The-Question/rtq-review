Which of these fractions are greater than one half?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$ 

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$ 

- $\dfrac{8}{15}$ 

- $\dfrac{11}{20}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

A fraction will be bigger than $\dfrac{1}{2}$ if twice of its numerator is bigger than the denominator.

Using trial and error,


| Fraction  |$2 \times$ Numerator|         $> \dfrac{1}{2}$         |
|:-------:  |:------------------:|:-------------------------------: |
| $3, 5$    |  $2 \times 3 = 6$  |             Yes                  |
| $3, 7$    |  $2 \times 3 = 6$  |              No                  |
| $5, 12$   |  $2 \times 5 = 10$  |              No                  |
| $8, 15$   |  $2 \times 8 = 16$  |             Yes                  |
| $11, 20$  |  $2 \times 11 = 22$ |             Yes                  |


$ \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{8}{15}, \dfrac{11}{20} $

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$ \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{8}{15}, \dfrac{11}{20} $

To convert kilometers to miles I multiply by $5$ and then divide by $8$.

Using this method how many miles is $240 \ \text{kilometers}$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&=  240 \ \text {kilometers} \\\\
&=  (240 \times 5) \div 8 \ \text {miles} \\\\
&=  \dfrac {240 \times 5} {8} \\\\
&=  \dfrac {8 \times 30 \times 5} {8} \\\\
&=  \dfrac {\cancel {8} \times 30 \times 5} {\cancel {8}} \\\\
&=  30 \times 5 \\\\
&=  150 \ \text {miles}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$150 \ \text {miles}$

</div>

What fraction of a day is $15 \ \text{minutes}$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text {Fraction}    &= \dfrac {15 \ \text{minutes}} {1 \ \text{day}} \\\\

                    &= \dfrac {15 \ \text{minutes}} {24 \times 60 \ \text{minutes}} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {15} {24 \times 60} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {15} {24 \times 15 \times 4} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {15} } {24 \times \cancel {15} \times 4} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac {1} {24 \times 4}\\\\
                    &= \dfrac {1} {96}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {96}$
