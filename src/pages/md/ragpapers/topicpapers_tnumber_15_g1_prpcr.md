<div class='question subquestion'>

If $p * 4 = 48$ find the value of $p$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
p * 4                   &= 48  \\\\
p^2 + (3 \times {4})    &= 48 \\\\
p^2 + 12                &= 48 \\\\
p^2                     &= 48 - 12 \\\\
p^2                     &= 36 \\\\
p                       &= \sqrt{36} \\\\
p                       &= \sqrt{6 \times 6} \\\\
p                       &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$6$

</div>
</div>

If $2 * q = 25$ find the value of $q$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 * q                &= 25  \\\\
2^2 + (3 \times q)   &= 25 \\\\
4 + 3q               &=  25 \\\\
3q                   &= 25 - 4 \\\\
q                    &= \dfrac{21}{3} \\\\
q                    &= \dfrac{7 \times{3}}{3} \\\\
q                    &= \dfrac{7 \times \cancel{3}}{\cancel3} \\\\
q                    &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$7$

<div class='question subquestion'>

If $s * t = 10$ where $s$ and $t$ are positive whole numbers, what are 
the possible values of $s$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
s * t           &= 10 \\\\
s ^ 2 + 3t      &= 10
\end{aligned}
$

We can easily infer the value by using trial and error.

| $s$ 	| $t$ 	                                            | Possible 	|
|:---:	|:------------------------------------------------:	|:--------:	|
| $0$ 	| $t = \dfrac {10 - 0^2} {3} = \dfrac {10} {3}$ 	| No, not a whole number 	    |
| $1$ 	| $t = \dfrac {10 - 1^2} {3} = 3$  	                | Yes 	    |
| $2$ 	| $ \begin{aligned} 2^2 + 3t &= 10 \\\\ t      &= 2  \end{aligned} $ 	| Yes 	|
| $3$ 	| $ \begin{aligned} 3^2 + 3t &= 10 \\\\ t      &= \dfrac{1}{3}  \end{aligned} $ 	| No, not a whole number. 	|
| $4$ 	| $ \begin{aligned} 4^2 + 3t &= 10 \\\\ t      &= -2  \end{aligned} $ 	| No, negative, $s$ too big 	|

Possible values for $s = 1, 2, 3$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$1, 2, 3$

</div>
</div>
