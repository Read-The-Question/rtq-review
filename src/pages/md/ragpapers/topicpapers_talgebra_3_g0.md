<div class='question question'>

$\boxed{\phantom{3}} + (2 \times \boxed{\phantom{3}}) = 27$

What number does $\boxed{\phantom{3}}$ stand for?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + (2 \times a)    &= 27 \\\\
a + 2a              &= 27 \\\\
3a                  &= 27 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {27} {3} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {9 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {9 \times \cancel {3} } {\cancel{3}} \\\\
a                   &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

Missing number $= 9$.

</div>
</div>
