&= \dfrac {3 \times 15} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {45} {2}
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

What is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $414?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {3} \ \text {of} \ 414 \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {3} \times 414 \\\\
&= \dfrac {414} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 138} {3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 138} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
&= 138
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
