<div class='question subsubquestion'>

$x \degree$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


As the triangle is an isosceles triangle, the third angle is also $= x$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
x + 40 + x  &= 180 \\\\
2 x + 40    &= 180 \\\\
2 x         &= 180 - 40 \\\\
2 x         &= 140 \\\\
x           &= \dfrac {140} {2} \\\\
x           &= \dfrac {70 \times 2} {2} \\\\
x           &= \dfrac {70 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
x           &= 70 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$70 \degree$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

One of the angles of an isosceles triangle is $96 \degree$ . Find the sizes of the other two angles.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


Let the missing angle $= a$

As the triangle is an isosceles triangle, the third angle is also $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + a + 96      &= 180  \\\\
2a + 96         &= 180  \\\\
2a              &= 180 - 96  \\\\
2a              &= 84  \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {84} {2}  \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {42 \times 2} {2}  \\\\
a               &= \dfrac {42 \times \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2}}   \\\\
a               &= 42 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Other two angles $= 42 \degree$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$42 \degree$

</div>
</div>
