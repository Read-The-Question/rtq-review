How many times could Zoya completely fill her jug from these bottles?

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total volume Zoya has} &= 5 \times 2 \text{litre} \\\\
&= 10 \text{litre}
\end{aligned}
$

Let number of times jug can be filled $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 1.5 &= 10 \\\\
a \times \dfrac{15}{10} &= 10 \\\\
a \times \dfrac{3}{2} &= 10 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{10}{\dfrac{3}{2}} \\\\
a &= 10 \times \dfrac{2}{3} \\\\
a &= \dfrac{20}{3} \\\\
a &= 6.66
\end{aligned}
$

Since the jug had to be completely filled,

Total times $= 6$

In order to convert from the imperial units ounces and pounds to the metric unit kilograms, you should use the following conversions:

$
\begin{aligned}
16 \ \text{ounces} &= 1 \ \text{pound}\\
2.2 \ \text{pounds} &= 1 \ \text{kg}
\end{aligned}
$

Newborn tiger cubs weigh about $56 \ \text{ounces}$.

Circle the mass in kilograms which gives the best approximation of the mass of a newborn tiger cub.

$0.5 \ \text{kg} \quad 1 \ \text{kg} \quad 1.5 \ \text{kg} \quad 2 \ \text{kg} \quad 2.5 \ \text{kg} \quad 3 \ \text{kg}$

</div>

&= (3.5 \div 2.2) \ \text{kg} \\\\
&= 1.59 \ \text{kg} \\\\
&= 1.5 \ \text{kg} \ \text{(approx.)}
\end{aligned}
$
