**Working**

Convert each number to a decimal.

$
\begin{aligned}
& 0.66 \quad \dfrac{3}{4} \quad 70\% \quad \dfrac{2}{3} \quad \dfrac{6}{10} \\\\
& 0.66 \quad 0.75 \quad 0.7 \quad 0.666 \quad 0.6
\end{aligned}
$

Put them in ascending order.

$
\begin{aligned}
& 0.6 < 0.66 < 0.666 < 0.7 < 0.75 \\\\
& \dfrac{6}{10} < 0.66 < \dfrac{2}{3} < 70\% < \dfrac{3}{4}
\end{aligned}
$

Number in the middle $= \dfrac {2} {3}$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
