less than he paid. The next day Bill sold it to Jack for $\pounds 90$. What is Bill's profit on the final sale?

                                    &= 120 - 36 \\\\
                                    &= \pounds 84 \\\\
\text{Bill's profit}                &= 210 - 84 \\\\
                                    &= \pounds 126
\end{aligned}
$
