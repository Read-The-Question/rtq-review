<div class='question question'>

Dan has to walk $1\dfrac {3} {4} \ \text{km}$ to school. How far has he walked when he is halfway?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Dividing fractions is the same as multiplying by the reciprocal (inverse) of the second fraction.


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Halfway distance}                  &= 1 \dfrac {3} {4} \div 2 \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7} {4} \times \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7} {4 \times 2} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7} {8} \ \text{km} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7} {8} \times 1000 \ \text{metres} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7 \times 1000}{8} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7 \times 125 \times 8}{8} \\\\
                                         &= \dfrac {7 \times 125 \times \cancel{8}}{\cancel{8}} \\\\
                                         &= 7 \times 125 \\\\
                                         &= 875 \text{ metres}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$875 \text{ metres}$

</div>
</div>
