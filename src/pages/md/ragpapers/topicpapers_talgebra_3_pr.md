<div class='question question'>

**Cats and dogs**

A farmer decides to buy some cats and some dogs.

Two cats and three dogs would cost $\pounds 180$.

One cat and four dogs would cost $\pounds 190$.

Work out the total cost of three and seven dogs.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of one cat $= c$

Let the cost of one dog $= d$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2c + 3d             &= 180 \\\\
c + 4d              &= 190
\end{aligned}
$

Let's add both the equations.

$
\begin{aligned}
2c + 3d + c + 4d            &= 180 + 190 \\\\
3c + 7d                     &= 370
\end{aligned}
$

The cost of three cats and seven dogs $= \pounds 370$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 370$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

**Bacteria**

Some bacteria are growing in a dish in a laboratory.

Each day the number of bacteria doubles.

On February $1 \text{st}$ there are $200$ bacteria.

On what day does the number of bacteria first become more than one million?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of days $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
2 ^ a \times 200            &= 1000000 \\\\
2 ^ a                       &= \dfrac {1000000} {200} \\\\
2 ^ a                       &= \dfrac {200 \times 5000} {200} \\\\
2 ^ a                       &= \dfrac {\cancel {200} \times 5000} {\cancel {200}} \\\\
2 ^ a                       &= 5000
\end{aligned}
$

We can easily infer the value by using trial and error. Lets find out a number that satisfies the above equation.

|  Value of a  |  Value of $2 ^ a$  |
|:------------:|:------------------:|
|     $10$     |        $1024$      |
|     $11$     |        $2048$      |
|     $12$     |        $4096$      |
|     $13$     |        $8192$      |

The day when number of bacteria become more than one million $= 13 \ \text{February}$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$13 \ \text{February}$

</div>
</div>

A ruler costs *k* pence, and a pen costs *m* pence.

Match each statement with the correct expression for the amount in pence.

The first one is done for you.

<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cost of 5 rulers and 5 pens}        &= 5 \times k + 5 \times m \\\\
                                                &= 5 (k + m) \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Difference between cost of 5 pens and 5 rulers}       &= 5 \times m - 5 \times k \\\\
                                                            &= 5m - 5k \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Change from} \ \pounds \text{5 after buying 5 pens}       &= \pounds 5 - 5 \times m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                                &= 5 \times 100 \ \text{pence} - 5m \ \text{pence} \\\\
                                                                &= 500 - 5m \ \text{pence}
\end{aligned}
$
