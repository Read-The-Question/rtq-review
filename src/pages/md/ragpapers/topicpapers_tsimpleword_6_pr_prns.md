<div class='question question'>

A box of biscuits contains $36$ biscuits. $20$ biscuits have foil wrappers. $15$ are chocolate biscuits with foil wrappers. 
If $9$ are not chocolate and do not have a foil wrapper, then how many chocolate biscuits are there?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Number of chocolate biscuits without foil wrappers}  &= 36 - 20 - 9 \\\\
                                                            &= 16 - 9 \\\\
                                                            &= 7
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total chocolate biscuits}    &= 15 + 7 \\\\
                                    &= 22
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$22$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

$75$ students need $3$ pens each. Pens are sold in packs of $6$. How many whole packs need to be bought?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total packs needed}       &= \dfrac{\text{Total pens required}}{\text{Pens per pack}} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac{75 \times 3}{6} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac{75 \times 3}{2 \times 3} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac{75 \times \cancel{3}}{2 \times \cancel{3}} \\\\
                                &= \dfrac{75}{2} \\\\
                                &= 37 \ \text {remainder} \ 1 \\\\
                                &\approx 38
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$38$

</div>
</div>

Sixteen people, and no more, can sit evenly spaced around a large square table. 
Jas arranges six of these square tables in a row to make one long rectangular table. 
What is the maximum number of people that can sit evenly  spaced around this long table?

- $46$
- $76$

- $86$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Number people one side of the square table}      &= \dfrac {\text {Total people}} {\text {Number of sides}} \\\\
                                                        &= \dfrac {16} {4} \\\\
                                                        &= 4 \\\\
\text {Maximum number of people in rectangular table}   &= \text {Total square tables} \times \text {People per table} - \text {Number of seats lost} \\\\
                                                        &= 6 \times 16 - 10 \times 4 \\\\
                                                        &= 96 - 40 \\\\
                                                        &= 56
\end {aligned}
$
