<div class='question subquestion'>

What is the rule for this number machine? Write your answer inside the machine.

![the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-1/section-1-question-13-subquestion-2-00.png](/assets/the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-1/section-1-question-13-subquestion-2-00.png "the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-1/section-1-question-13-subquestion-2-00.png")

![missing table](/papers/missing_table.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the rule $= \text{Multiply by}\ a \ \text{then add}\ b$

Input $= 2$

Output $= 17$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 2 + b &= 17 \\\\
2a + b &= 17
\end{aligned}
$

Input $= 7$

Output $= 52$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 7 + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + b &= 52
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $a$

$
\begin{aligned}
7a + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + (17 - 2a) &= 52 \\\\
7a - 2a &= 52 - 17 \\\\
5a &= 35 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{35}{5} \\\\
a &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $b$

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 17 \\\\
2 \times 7 + b &= 17 \\\\
14 + b &= 17 \\\\
b &= 17 - 14 \\\\
b &= 3 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

Lets verify,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 9 \times 7 + 3 \\\\
&= 63 + 3 \\\\
&= \green {66} = 66 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 10 \times 7 + 3 \\\\
&= 70 + 3 \\\\
&= \green {73} = 73 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the rule $= \text{Multipy by}\ 7 \ \text{then add}\ 3$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\text{Multipy by}\ 7 \ \text{then add}\ 3$
