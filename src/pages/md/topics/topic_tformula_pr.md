The rule $a * b$ means $(a \times a) + (b \times b)$
 
That is, you multiply the first number by the first number, the second number by the second number and then you add your answers together.

For example, 

$
\begin{aligned}
6 * 4 &= (6 \times 6) + (4 \times 4) \\
      &= 36           + 16           \\
      &= 52     
\end{aligned}
$

$a * 8 = 208$. Work out the value of $a$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a * 8                           &= 208 \\\\
(a \times a) + (8 \times 8)     &= 208 \\\\
a^2 + 64                        &= 208 \\\\
a^2                             &= 208 - 64 \\\\
a^2                             &= 144 \\\\
a^2                             &= 12 \times 12 \\\\
a^2                             &= 12^2 \\\\
a                               &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

<div class='question subquestion'>

$b * 2 = 4b$. Work out the value of $b$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
b * 2                           &= 4b \\\\
(b \times b) + (2 \times 2)     &= 4b \\\\
b^2 + 4                         &= 4b \\\\
b^2 - 4b + 4                    &= 0 \\\\
(b-2)^2                         &= 0 \\\\
b - 2                           &= 0 \\\\
b                               &= 2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
