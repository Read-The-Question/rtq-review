$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Temperature of Freddie’s surroundings} &= (61 - 40) \div 7 + 10 \\\\
                                             &= 21 \div 7 + 10 \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {21} {7} + 10 \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {7 \times 3} {7} + 10 \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {\cancel {7} \times 3} {\cancel {7}} + 10 \\\\\
                                             &= 3 + 10 \\\\
                                             &= 13 \degree \ \text{C}
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

In Moscow, the temperature is $-11 \degree \text{C}$ and in Paris the temperature is $14 \ \text{degrees}$ warmer.


What is the temperature in Paris?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Temperature in Paris}  &= \text {Temperature in Moscow} + 14 \degree \\\\
                              &= -11 \degree \text {C} + 14 \degree \\\\
                              &= 3 \degree \text {C}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3 \degree \text {C}$

<div class='question question'>

The temperature at midday in Moscow is $-3\degree\,C$. Between midday and midnight the temperature in Moscow rises by $5\degree\,C$ and then falls by $11\degree\,C$. 

What is the temperature at midnight in Moscow?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Temperature in midday}    &= -3 \degree C \\\\
\text{Temperature at midnight}  &= -3 \degree C + 5 \degree C - 11 \degree C \\\\
                                &= -9 \degree C
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$-9 \degree C$

</div>
