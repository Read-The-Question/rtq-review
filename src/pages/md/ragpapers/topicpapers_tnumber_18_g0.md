<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$

As per question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 16  &= 36 \div 9 \\\\
a \times 16  &= \dfrac {36} {9} \\\\
a            &= \dfrac {36} {9 \times 16} \\\\
a            &= \dfrac {9 \times 4} {9 \times 4 \times 4} \\\\
a            &= \dfrac {\cancel {9} \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {9} \times 4 \times \cancel {4}} \\\\
a            &= \dfrac {1} {4}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {4}$

</div>
</div>
