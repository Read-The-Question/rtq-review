<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

How much does a raisin bar cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of a nut bar (in pence) $= a$

Cost of a raisin bar (in pence) $= a + 12$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
9 \times (a + 12) + 12 \times a     &= \pounds 5.28 \\\\
9a + 9 \times 12 + 12a              &= 528 \ \text{p} \\\\
21a + 108                           &= 528 \\\\
21a                                 &= 528 - 108 \\\\
21a                                 &= 420 \\\\
a                                   &= \dfrac {420} {21} \\\\
a                                   &= \dfrac {20 \times 21} {21} \\\\
a                                   &= \dfrac {20 \times \cancel {21}} {\cancel {21}} \\\\
a                                   &= 20 \ \text{p} \\\\
\text {Cost of raisin bar}          &= a + 12 \\\\
                                    &= 20 + 12 \\\\
                                    &= 32 \ \text{p}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$32 \ \text{p}$

</div>
</div>
