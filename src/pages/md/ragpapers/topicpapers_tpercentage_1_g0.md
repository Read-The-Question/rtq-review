Work out $56\%$ of $80$

Using previous answers

$50\% \ \text{of} \ 8 = 40$

$5\% \ \text{of} \ 8 = 4$

$1\% \ \text{of} \ 8 = \dfrac {4} {5}$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 56\% \text{ of } 80 \\\\
&= (50\% + 5\% + 1\%) \times 80 \\\\
&= 50\% \times 80 + 5\% \times 80 + 1\% \times 80 \\\\
&= 40 + 4 + \dfrac {4} {5}  \\\\
&= 44 + \dfrac {4} {5}  \\\\
&= \dfrac {44 \times 5} {1 \times 5} + \dfrac {4} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {220} {5} + \dfrac {4} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {220 + 4} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {224} {5} \\\\
&= 44 \dfrac {4} {5}
\end{aligned}
$

$44 \dfrac {4} {5}$
