<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

How many red counters does she have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Red counters} &= \dfrac {1} {3} \times 60 \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {60} {3} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {20 \times 3} {3} \\\\
                     &= \dfrac {20 \times \cancel {3} } {\cancel {3} } \\\\
                     &= 20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$20$

</div>
</div>

</div>

<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

$25\%$ of her counters are blue.

How many blue counters does she have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Blue counters} &= 25 \% \ \text {of} \ 60 \\\\
                      &= \dfrac {25 \times 60} {100} \\\\
                      &= \dfrac {25 \times 4 \times 15} {25 \times 4} \\\\
                      &= \dfrac {\cancel{25} \times \cancel{4} \times 15} {\cancel{25} \times \cancel{4}} \\\\
                      &= 15
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$15$

</div>
</div>

What is the rule for this number machine? Write your answer inside the machine.

![missing table](/papers/missing_table.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the rule $= \text{Multiply by}\ a \ \text{then add}\ b$

Input $= 2$

Output $= 17$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 2 + b &= 17 \\\\
2a + b &= 17
\end{aligned}
$

Input $= 7$

Output $= 52$

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times 7 + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + b &= 52
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $a$

$
\begin{aligned}
7a + b &= 52 \\\\
7a + (17 - 2a) &= 52 \\\\
7a - 2a &= 52 - 17 \\\\
5a &= 35 \\\\
a &= \dfrac{35}{5} \\\\
a &= 7
\end{aligned}
$

Lets solve for $b$

$
\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 17 \\\\
2 \times 7 + b &= 17 \\\\
14 + b &= 17 \\\\
b &= 17 - 14 \\\\
b &= 3 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

Lets verify,

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 9 \times 7 + 3 \\\\
&= 63 + 3 \\\\
&= \green {66} = 66 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 10 \times 7 + 3 \\\\
&= 70 + 3 \\\\
&= \green {73} = 73 \\\
\end{aligned}
$

Therefore, the rule $= \text{Multipy by}\ 7 \ \text{then add}\ 3$

All the water from these two beakers is poured into the empty beaker. 
Draw a line to show the level of the water in the new beaker.

![the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-2/section-1-question-17-00.png](/assets/the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-2/section-1-question-17-00.png "the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2009--group-2/section-1-question-17-00.png")

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Water in first beaker}  &= 300 \ \text{ml}
                              &= 300 \times \dfrac{1}{1000} \ \text{litre} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{300}{1000} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{3 \times 100}{100 \times 10} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{3 \times 100}{100 \times 10} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{3 \times \cancel{100}}{\cancel{100} \times 10} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {3}{10} \\\\
                              &= 0.3 \ \text{litre} \\\\
\text{Water in first beaker}  &= 450 \ \text{ml}
                              &= 450 \times \dfrac{1}{1000} \ \text{litre} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{450}{1000} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{45 \times 10}{100 \times 10} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{45 \times 10}{100 \times 10} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac{45 \times \cancel{10}}{\cancel{10} \times 100} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {45}{100} \\\\
                              &= 0.45 \ \text{litre}   \\\\
\text{Total amount of water}  &= 0.3 + 0.45 \\\\
                              &= 0.75 \ \text{litre}
\end{aligned}
