in the year in which
Lord Owlynose was born.
He died in $1940$, aged $80$.
How old was the oak tree
in $2011$?

A     B     C     D     E

\end {aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Richard is $12$ years old.

Adam is $\text{twice}$ as old as Richard.

Will is $\dfrac{2}{3}$  of Adam’s age.
        
What is their total age?

</div>
<div class='workings'>

\begin{aligned}
\text {Richard's age}    &= 12 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

\begin{aligned}
\text {Adam's age}       &= 2 \times \text {Richard's age} \\\\
                         &= 2 \times 12 \\\\
                         &= 24 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Will's age}       &= \dfrac {2} {3} \times \text {Adam's age} \\\\
                         &= \dfrac {2} {3} \times 24 \\\\
                         &= 16 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Total age}        &= 12 + 24 + 16 \\\\
                         &= 52 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$52 \ \text {years}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Richard is $18$ years old.

Adam is half as old as Richard.

Will is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of Adam's age.

What is their total age?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Richard's age}    &= 18 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Adam's age}       &= \dfrac {\text{Richard's age}} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {18} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 9} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 9} {\cancel{2}} \\\\
                        &= 9 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text{Will's age}       &= \dfrac {2} {3} \times \text{Adam's age} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2} {3} \times 9 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 9} {3} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 3 \times 3} {3} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {2 \times 3 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
                        &= 2 \times 3 \\\\
                        &= 6 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text{Total age}        &= \text{Richard's age + Adam's age + Will's age} \\\\
                        &= 18 + 9 + 6 \\\\
                        &= 33 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$33 \ \text {years}$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question question'>

The total of the ages of a father, his daughter and his two twin sons is $96$ years. 
The daughter’s age is half the father’s age and twice the age of each of her two brothers. 

How old is the **father**? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let father’s age $= a$

Daughter’s age $= \dfrac {a} {2}$

Brother's age $= \dfrac{a} {4}$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + \dfrac {a} {2} + \dfrac {a} {4} + \dfrac {a} {4}                                            &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {a \times 4} {4} + \dfrac {a \times 2} {2 \times 2} + \dfrac{a}{4} + \dfrac{a}{4}        &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {4a} {4} + \dfrac{2a}{4} + \dfrac{a}{4} + \dfrac{a}{4}                                   &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {4a + 2a + a + a} {4}                                                                    &= 96 \\\\
\dfrac {8a} {4}                                                                                 &= 96 \\\\
8a                                                                                              &= 96 \times 4 \\\\
a                                                                                               &= \dfrac{96 \times 4} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               &= \dfrac{96 \times 4} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               &= \dfrac{12 \times 4 \times 8} {8} \\\\
a                                                                                               &= \dfrac{12 \times 4 \times \cancel {8}} {\cancel {8}} \\\\
a                                                                                               &= 12 \times 4 \\\\
a                                                                                               &= 48 \ \text {years}
\end{aligned}
$

Father's age $= 48 \ \text {years}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$48 \ \text {years}$

</div>

<div class='question subquestion'>

What is the combined age of Alan, Ben and Carl?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Alan's age $= a$

Let Ben's age $= b$

Let Carl's age $= c$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b       &= 43 \\\\
a + c       &= 45 \\\\
b + c       &= 66
\end{aligned}
$

Let’s find the combined age of Alan, Ben and Carl

$
\begin{aligned}
(a + b) + (b + c) + (a + c) &= 43 + 66 + 45 \\\\
2a + 2b + 2c                &= 154 \\\\
2 \times (a + b + c)        &= 154 \\\\
a + b + c                   &= \dfrac {154} {2} \\\\
a + b + c                   &= \dfrac {2 \times 77} {2} \\\\
a + b + c                   &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 77} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a + b + c                   &= 77
\end{aligned}
$

The combined age of Alan, Ben and Carl is seventy seven.

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

$\text {Seventy seven}$
