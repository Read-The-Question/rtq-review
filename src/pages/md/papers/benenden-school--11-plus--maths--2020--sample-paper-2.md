| $12$ |    $4$    |         $6$          |

\text{Let total}\           &= n \\\\
\text{Median's position}\   &= \dfrac {n + 1} {2} \\\\

                            &= \dfrac {31} {2} \\\\
                            &= 15.5 \\\
