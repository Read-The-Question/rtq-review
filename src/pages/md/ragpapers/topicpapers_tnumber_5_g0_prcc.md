Calculate how much bigger is $3\dfrac{1}{4}$ is than $1\dfrac{3}{4}$ writing your answer as a **decimal**.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

&= 3 \dfrac {1} {4} - 1 \dfrac {3} {4} \\\\

&= \dfrac {13 - 7} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {6} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 2} {2 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {2}} {2 \times \cancel {2}} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {2} \\\\
&= 1 \dfrac {1} {2} \\\\
&= 1.5
\end{aligned}
$
