ABBR:  Let's find the relation between the consecutive terms of the sequence.


The next pattern is made by adding $6$ sticks to the previous pattern.

This is an arithmetic sequence too and the difference between the consecutive patterns of the sequence is constant.

The next pattern is made by adding multiple of $6$ sticks to the first pattern and the number of times it is added is based on the position in the sequence.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of sticks in pattern 4}      &= 16 + 6 \\\\
                                          &= 22 \\\\
\text{Number of sticks in pattern 7}      &= 4 + 6 \times 6 \\\\
                                          &= 4 + 36 \\\\
                                          &= 40
\end{aligned}
$

Let's calculate the pattern number where $70$ sticks were used.

Let the pattern number $= p$

$
\begin{aligned}
4 + (p-1) \times 6  &= 70 \\\\
4 + 6p-6            &= 70 \\\\
6p                  &= 70 + 6 - 4 \\\\
6p                  &= 70 + 2 \\\\
6p                  &= 72 \\\\
p                   &= \dfrac{72}{6} \\\\
p                   &= \dfrac{12 \times 6}{6} \\\\
p                   &= \dfrac{12 \times \cancel{6}}{\cancel{6}} \\\\
p                   &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

$
    
Find the missing numbers in the sequence.

</div>

<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Third number $= 3 + a$

Fourth number $= (3 + a) + 3 = 6 + a$

Fifth number in the sequence $= (6 + a) + (3 + a)$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
(6 + a) + (3 + a)       &= 19 \\\\
2 a + 9                 &= 19 \\\\
2 a                     &= 19 - 9 \\\\
2 a                     &= 10 \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {10} {2} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {2 \times 5} {2} \\\\
a                       &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 5} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a                       &= 5 \\\\
\text {First  number}   &= 5 \\\\
\text {Third  number}   &= 3 + a \\\\
                        &= 3 + 5 \\\\
                        &= 8 \\\\
\text {Fourth number}   &= 6 + a \\\\
                        &= 6 + 5 \\\\
                        &= 11
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

In a number sequence, the $2 \text{nd}$ term is $6$ and the $5 \text{th}$ term is $18$, as shown below.

What is the **$7 \text{th}$ term** in the sequence?

$\ldots, \quad 6, \quad \ldots, \quad \ldots, \quad 18, \quad \ldots, \quad \ldots$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the difference $= d$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
6 + 3d                        &= 18 \\\\
3d                            &= 18 - 6 \\\\
3d                            &= 12 \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{12}{3} \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{3 \times 4}{3} \\\\
d                             &= \dfrac{\cancel{3} \times 4}{\cancel{3}} \\\\
d                             &= 4 \\\\
7 ^ \text{th} \text{ term}    &= 18 + 2d \\\\
                              &= 18 + 2 \times 4 \\\\
                              &= 18 + 8 \\\\
                              &= 26
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$26$

</div>
</div>

</div>
