A piece of wire $36 \ \text{cm}$ long is bent into the shape of a rectangle. If the rectangle is twice as long as it is wide, what is its area (in square centimetres) ?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \times (l + b)         &= 36 \\\\
l + b                    &= 18 \\\\
2b + b                   &= 18 \\\\
3b                       &= 18 \\\\
b                        &= 6 \\\\
l                        &= 2b \\\\
l                        &= 2 \times 6 \\\\
l                        &= 12 \\\\
area                     &= l \times b \\\\
area                     &= 12 \times 6 \\\\
area                     &= 72
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$72$
