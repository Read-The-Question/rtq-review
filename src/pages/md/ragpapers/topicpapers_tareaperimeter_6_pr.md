Let the length of second edge of cuboid $= w$

Let the length of third edge cuboid $= h$

$
\begin{aligned}
l \times w                                      &= 24 \\\\
w                                               &= \dfrac {24} {l} \\\\
w \times h                                      &= 32 \\\\
\dfrac {24} {l} \times h                        &= 32 \\\\
\dfrac {24h} {l}                                &= 32 \\\\
24h                                             &= 32 \times l \\\\
h                                               &= \dfrac {32 \times l} {24} \\\\
l \times h                                      &= 48 \\\\
l \times \dfrac{32 \times l} {24}               &= 48 \\\\
\dfrac{l \times 32 \times l} {24}               &= 48 \\\\
\dfrac{32 \times l^2} {24}                      &= 48 \\\\
32 \times l^2                                   &= 48 \times 24 \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {48 \times 24} {32} \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {16 \times 3 \times 2 \times 12} {16 \times 2} \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {\cancel {16} \times 3 \times \cancel {2} \times 12} {\cancel {16} \times \cancel {2}} \\\\
l^2                                             &= 3 \times 12 \\\\
l^2                                             &= 36 \\\\
l                                               &= \sqrt {36} \\\\
l                                               &= 6 \ \text {cm} \\\\

w                                               &= \dfrac {6 \times 4} {6} \\\\

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of rectangle} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$


Let the length of the rectangle $= l$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
l \times 4          &= 24 \\\\
4l                  &= 24 \\\\
l                   &= \dfrac {24} {4} \\\\
l                   &= \dfrac {4 \times 6} {4} \\\\
l                   &= \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 6} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
l                   &= 6 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find the perimeter of the rectangle.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Perimeter}       &= 2 \times (6 + 4) \\\\
                        &= 2 \times 10 \\\\
                        &= 20 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

$20 \ \text {cm}$
