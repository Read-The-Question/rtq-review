Maria uses $6 \ \text{litres}$ of water every time she cleans her kitchen.

She cleans her kitchen every day except for Sunday.

For how many weeks does a bottle of *Kleeno* last her?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{water}                            &:& \text{kleeno} \\\\
= & 4 \ \text{litres}                       &:& 20 \ \text{ml} \\\\
= & 1                                       &:& \dfrac {20} {4} \\\\
= & 6                                       &:& \dfrac {20 \times 6} {4} \\\\
= & 6                                       &:& \dfrac {4 \times 5 \times 6} {4} \\\\
= & 6                                       &:& \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 5 \times 6} {\cancel 4} \\\\
= & 6                                       &:& 5 \times 6 \\\\
= & 6 \ \text{litres}                       &:& 30 \ \text{ml}
\end{array}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of weeks}      &= \dfrac {540} {30 \times 6} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {30 \times 6 \times 3} {30 \times 6} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {30} \times \cancel 6 \times 3} {\cancel {30} \times \cancel 6} \\\\
                            &= 3
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3$

</div>
