Here is a **right angled triangle** inside a **rectangle**. Calculate the value of angle $x$. Do not use a protractor.

![john-lyon-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-21-00.png](/assets/john-lyon-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-21-00.png "john-lyon-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-21-00.png") 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


ABBR: $\text{Angles on a straight line add up to} = 180 \degree$


Let the third angle of the triangle $=a$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
83 + a + 58  &= 180 \\\\
141 + a      &= 180 \\\\
a            &= 180 - 141 \\\\
a            &= 39 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $a$ to find $x$.

$
\begin{aligned}
x + 39 + 90  &= 180 \\\\
x + 129      &= 180 \\\\
x            &= 180 - 129 \\\\
x            &= 51 \degree 
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$51 \degree$

</div>
