What is $10\%$ of $\pounds 400?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 10\% \ \text{of} \ \pounds 400 \\\\
&= \dfrac {10} {100} \times 400 \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times 400} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times 4 \cancel{0} \cancel{0}} {1 \cancel{0} \cancel{0}} \\\\
&= 10 \times 4 \\\\
&= \pounds 40
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 40$

</div>
</div>

What percentage of the grid below is shaded? You can assume all boxes are identical in size.

![st-pauls-girls-school--11-plus--maths--2017--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-8-00.png](/assets/st-pauls-girls-school--11-plus--maths--2017--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-8-00.png "st-pauls-girls-school--11-plus--maths--2017--sample-paper-2/section-1-question-8-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total number of boxes}        &= 25 \\\\
\text{Number of shaded boxes}       &= 17 \\\\
\text{\% shaded grid}               &= \dfrac {\text{Shaded boxes}} {\text{Total boxes}} \times 100 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {17}{25} \times 100 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {17 \times 100} {25} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {17 \times 4 \times 25} {25} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {17 \times 4 \times \cancel {25}} {\cancel {25}} \\\\
                                    &= 17 \times 4 \\\\
                                    &= 68 \%
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
