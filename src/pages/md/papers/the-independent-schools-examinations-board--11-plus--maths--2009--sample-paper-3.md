<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

What is the first number in the pattern which is greater than $48$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the term number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
3 + (a - 1) \times 4     &>    48 \\\\
(a - 1) \times 4         &>    48 - 3 \\\\
(a - 1) \times 4         &>    45 \\\\
a - 1                    &>    \dfrac{45}{4} \\\\
a - 1                    &>    11.25 \\\\
a                        &>    11.25 + 1 \\\\
a                        &>    12.25 \\\\
a                        &\approx  13
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{First number greater than } 48      &= 3 + (13-1) \times 4 \\\\
                                          &= 3 + 12 \times 4 \\\\
                                          &= 3 + 48 \\\\
                                          &= 51
\end{aligned}
$

$51$ is the first number in the pattern which is greater than $48$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$51$

</div>

There is a pattern with $40$ white tiles.

Which pattern number is it?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the pattern number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
8 + (a - 1) \times 4   &= 40 \\\\
(a - 1) \times 4       &= 40 - 8 \\\\
(a - 1) \times 4       &= 32 \\\\
a - 1                  &= \dfrac{32}{4} \\\\
a - 1                  &= \dfrac{\cancel{4} \times 8}{\cancel{4}} \\\\
a - 1                  &= 8 \\\\
a                      &= 8 + 1 \\\\
a                      &= 9 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

Pattern number $= 9$

</div>
</div>

$9$
