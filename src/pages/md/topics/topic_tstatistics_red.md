<div class='question question'>

The mean of two numbers is six. One of the numbers 
is minus two. What is the other number?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= b$

As per the question

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {a + (-2)} {2} &= 6 \\\\
\dfrac {a - 2} {2}    &= 6 \\\\
a - 2                 &= 6 \times 2 \\\\
a                     &= 12 + 2 \\\\
a                     &= 14
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$14$

</div>
