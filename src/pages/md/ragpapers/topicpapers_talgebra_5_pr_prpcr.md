In the town of Mathsville there are $360$ residents.

$45 \%$ of the residents are children, the rest are adults.

How many adults are there in Mathsville?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of adults} &= 55 \% \ \text {of} \ 360 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {55} {100} \times 360 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {55 \times 360} {100} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {11 \times 5 \times 20 \times 18} {5 \times 20} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {11 \times \cancel {5} \times \cancel {20} \times 18} {\cancel {5} \times \cancel {20}} \\\\
                        &= 11 \times 18 \\\\
                        &= 198
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

The price of a bus ticket from Mathsville to Numbertown has **increased** by $50\%$ in the past year. 
A bus ticket now costs $\pounds 1.80$.

What did it cost a year ago?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of bus ticket a year ago $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of the bus ticket after a year} &= a + 50\% \ \text{of} \ a \\\\
                                           &= a + \dfrac {50} {100} \times a \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + \dfrac {50} {100} \times a &= 1.80 \\\\
a + \dfrac {50 \times a} {50 \times 2}  &= 1.80 \\\\
a + \dfrac {\cancel {50} \times a} {\cancel {50} \times 2}  &= 1.80 \\\\
a + \dfrac {a} {2}  &= 1.80 \\\\
\dfrac {a \times 2} {2} + \dfrac {a} {2}  &= 1.80 \\\\
\dfrac {2a + a} {2} &= 1.80 \\\\
\dfrac {3a} {2} &= 1.80 \\\\
3a &= 1.80 \times 2 \\\\
3a &=  3.60 \\\\
a &= \dfrac {3.60} {3} \\\\
a &= \dfrac { 3 \times 1.20} {3} \\\\
a &= \dfrac { \cancel 3 \times 1.20} { \cancel 3} \\\\
a &= \pounds 1.20
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='answer'>

<div class='question subquestion'>

Mathsville Maths Supplies is a shop selling maths equipment.

Tina bought $4$ calculators and $3$ protractors for $\pounds 16.10$.
Ahmed bought $2$ calculators and $1$ protractors for $\pounds 7.50$.

How much does a calculator cost, and how much does a protractor cost?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of a calculator (in pounds) $= a$

Let the cost of a protractor (in pounds) $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4a + 3b &= 16.10 \\\\
2a + b  &= 7.50
\end{aligned}
$

We have got 2 variables and we have 2 equations to solve them .Let's use elimination to reduce it to just one variable. 

Multiply second equation by 3.

$
\begin{aligned}
3 \times 2a + 3 \times b    &= 3 \times 7.50 \\\\
6a + 3b                     &= 22.5
\end{aligned}
$

Subtract first equation from the above equations.

$
\begin{aligned} 
6a + 3b - (4a + 3b)         &= 22.50 - 16.10 \\\\
6a + 3b - 4a - 3b           &= 6.4 \\\\
2a                          &= 6.4 \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {6.4} {2} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {3.2 \times 2} {2} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {3.2 \times \cancel 2} {\cancel 2} \\\\
a                           &= 3.2
\end{aligned}
$

Substituting the above value in second equation

$
\begin{aligned}
2 \times 3.2 + b  &= 7.50 \\\\
6.4 + b           &= 7.50 \\\\
b                 &= 7.50 - 6.4 \\\\
b                 &= 1.10
\end{aligned}
$

The cost of a calculator $= \pounds 3.20$

The cost of a protractor $= \pounds 1.10$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>
