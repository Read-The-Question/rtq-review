<div class='question question'>

In a tin of Quality Street, $12\%$ are Coffee Crèmes. If the tin has a total of 250 chocolates, how many are **not** Coffee Crèmes?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

According to the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Not Coffee Crèmes}  &= 250 - 12 \ \% \ \text{of} \ 250 \\\\
                          &= 250 - \dfrac {12 \times 250} {100} \\\\
                          &= 250 - \dfrac {30 \times 2 \times 5 \times 2 \times 5} {2 \times 5 \times 2 \times 5} \\\\
                          &= 250 - \dfrac {30 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel 5 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel 5} {\cancel 2 \times \cancel 5 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel 5} \\\\
                          &= 250 - 30 \\\\
                          &= 220
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$220$

</div>
</div>
