**Question**

A $7 \times 7 \times 7$ cube is painted, and then cut into $1 \times 1 \times 1$ cubes.

<div class="question" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Question**

What is the largest number of $15\ \text{cm}$ by $30\ \text{cm}$ by $20\ \text{cm}$ shoe boxes that can fit in a $1\ \text{m} \times 3\ \text{m} \times 2\ \text{m}$ crate?

Be careful - you can't chop up the shoe boxes!

![papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i00.png](/assets/papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i00.png "papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i00.png")       ![papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i01.png](/assets/papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i01.png "papers/city-of-london-school-for-girls/sample/sample-paper-1/s02-q05-i01.png")

<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">

<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex">What is the largest number of $15\ \text{cm}$ by $30\ \text{cm}$ by $20\ \text{cm}$ shoe boxes that can fit in a $1\ \text{m} \times 3\ \text{m} \times 2\ \text{m}$ crate?
<emptyline>
Be careful - you can't chop up the shoe boxes!
<emptyline>
%image%       %image%
</code></pre>

<div class="tip">

<div class="formula">
- ABBR: $\text{Volume of a cuboid} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of shoe boxes} &= \dfrac {\text{Volume of the crate}} {\text{Volume of the shoe boxes}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {0.9 \ \text{m} \times 3 \ \text{m} \times 2 \ \text{m}}{15 \ \text{cm} \times 30 \ \text{cm} \times 20 \ \text{cm}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {90 \ \text{cm} \times 300 \ \text{cm} \times 200 \ \text{cm}}{15 \ \text{cm} \times 30 \ \text{cm} \times 20 \ \text{cm}} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3 \times 3 \times 10 \times 30 \times 10 \times 20 \times 10}{3 \times 5 \times 30 \times 20} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel{3} \times 3 \times 2 \times \cancel{5} \times \cancel{30} \times 10 \times \cancel{20} \times 10} {\cancel{3} \times \cancel{5} \times \cancel{30} \times \cancel{20}} \\\\
                            &= 3 \times 2 \times 10 \times 10 \\\\
                            &= 600
\end{aligned}
$
</div>

<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$600$
