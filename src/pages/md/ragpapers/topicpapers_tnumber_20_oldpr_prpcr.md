Which **one** of the following statements is false?

**A.** $2 + 5 \times 4 = 22$    

**B.** $18 - 6 \times 3 = 0$    

**C.** $15-2 \times 7 = 1$

**D.** $7 + 1 \times 3 = 24$    

**E.** $8 \times 5 - 7 = 33$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{A}    &= 2 + 5 \times 4 \\\\
            &= 2 + 20 \\\\
            &= \red{22} = 22 \\\\
\text{B}    &= 18 - 6 \times 3 \\\\
            &= 18 - 18 \\\\
            &= \red{0} = 0 \\\\
\text{C}    &= 15 - 2 \times 7 \\\\
            &= 15 - 14 \\\\
            &= \red{1}  = 1 \\\\
\text{D}    &= 7 + 1 \times 3 \\\\
            &= 7 + 3 \\\\
            &= \green{10} \neq 24
\end{aligned}
$

False statement $= \text{D}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$D$

</div>

Write down two of these numbers with a difference of $8$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$4, -4 \ \text {or} \ 3, -5 \ \text {or} \ 5, -3$

Let's verify.

$
\begin{aligned}
4, -4   &= 4 - ( -4 ) \\\\
         &= 4 + 4 \\\\
         &= 8 \\\\
3, -5   &= 3 - ( -5 ) \\\\
         &= 3 + 5 \\\\
         &= 8 \\\\
5, -3   &= 5 - ( -3 ) \\\\
         &= 5 + 3 \\\\
         &= 8
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>

$4, -4 \ \text {or} \ 3, -5 \ \text {or} \ 5, -3$
