<div class='question question'>

Natalie, Sara and Antonia catch $3$ different buses to go to the same destination.

Antonia catches the $19{:}15$ bus.

Natalie’s bus journey takes three times as long as Sara’s. 
Natalie’s bus leaves $20 \ \text{minutes}$ before Antonia’s bus and arrives at $20{:}10$. 
Sara catches the $19{:}25$ bus. 

What time does Sara arrive at the destination? 

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)
 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time when Natalia catch the bus}      &= \text{Time when Antonia catch the bus} - 20 \ \text{min} \\\\
                                            &= 19{:}15 - 20 \ \text{min} \\\\
                                            &= 18{:}55
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Duration of Natalia journey}      &= 20{:}10 - 18{:}55 \\\\
                                        &= 75 \ \text{min}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Duration of Sara journey}      &= \dfrac {\text{Duration of Natalia journey}} {3} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {75} {3} \ \text{min} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {3 \times 25} {3} \\\\
                                     &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 25} {\cancel 3} \\\\
                                     &= 25 \text{min}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time when sara arrive at destination}     &= 19{:}25 + 25 \ \text{min} \\\\
                                                &= 19{:}50
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$19{:}50$

</div>
</div>
