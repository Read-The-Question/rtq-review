<div class='question question'>

Two numbers add to give $15$ and multiply to give $56$.

What is the difference between the two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
Let the first number $= a$.

Let the second number $= b$.

As per the question,

$a + b = 15$.

$ab = 56$.

As we know, $(a - b) ^ 2 = a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 - 2ab$.

and $(a + b) ^ 2 = a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 + 2ab$.

Using this we get,

$
\begin {aligned}
(a - b) ^ 2  &= a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 - 2ab \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 - (4ab - 2ab) \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= (a ^ 2 + b ^ 2 + 2ab) - 4ab \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= (a + b) ^ 2 - 4ab \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= (15) ^ 2 - 4 \times 56 \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= 225 - 224 \\\\
(a - b) ^ 2  &= 1 \\\\
a - b        &= \sqrt {1} \\\\
a - b        &= 1
\end {aligned}
$  

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$1$

</div>
</div>

$3$
