<div class='question question'>

Through what angles does the hour hand of a clock rotate between $8 \ \text{am}$ and $9{:}30 \ \text{am}$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: The hour hand of a clock moves by $30 \degree$ every hour.


ABBR: The hour hand of a clock moves by $0.5 \degree$ every minute.


$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total minutes}   &= 9{:}30 \ \text{am} -  8 \ \text{am} \\\\
                        &= 60 \ \text{minutes} + 30 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                        &= 90 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
\text {Angle moved}     &= 90 \times 0.5 \\\\
                        &= 45 \degree
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$45 \degree$

</div>
</div>
