$

\end{aligned}

Adding the above two equations, we get

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b + a - b &= 32 + 6 \\\\
2 a           &= 38 \\\\
a             &= \dfrac{38}{2} \\\\
a             &= \dfrac{2 \times 19}{2} \\\\
a             &= \dfrac{\cancel {2} \times 19} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
a             &= 19
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Larger number}   &= 19 \\\\
\text {Smaller number}  &= 19 - 6 \\\\
                        &= 13
\end {aligned}
