<div class='question question'>

Write down two numbers that add to $14$ and multiply to $48$. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number be $a$ and second be $b$.

As per question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b      &= 14 \\
a \times b &= 48
\end{aligned}
$

Let's find $a$ and $b$.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= x^2 + 14x + 48 \\\\
&= x^2 + (6 + 8)x + (6 \times 8) \\\\
&= (x + 6) (x+ 8)
\end{aligned}
$

$x = 6$ and $x = 8$

the two numbers are $6$ and $8$

checking: 

$
\begin{aligned}
&- a + b   \\   
&= 6 + 8 \\
&= 14 \\\\
&= a \times b \\
&= 6 \times 8
&= 48 
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
