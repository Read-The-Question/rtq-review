Calculate $0.35 + \dfrac{4}{5} + \dfrac{37}{100}$, leaving your answer as a decimal. 

</div>

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 0.35 + \dfrac {4} {5} + \dfrac {37} {100} \\\\
&= 0.35 + \dfrac {4 \times 2} {5 \times 2} + \dfrac {37} {100} \\\\
&= 0.35 + \dfrac {8} {10} + \dfrac {37} {100} \\\\
&= 0.35 + 0.8 + 0.37 \\\\
&= 1.52
\end{aligned}
$
