How much smaller is the area of a $60 \ \text{cm}$ by $40 \ \text{cm}$ rectangle than that of a square with the same perimeter? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}

                 &= 2 \times (100) \\\\
                 &= 200 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$

As per the question

$
\begin{aligned}
2 (a + a)  &= 200 \\\\
4a         &= 200 \\\\
a          &= \dfrac {200} {4} \\\\

a          &= \dfrac {50 \times \cancel {4} } {\cancel {4} }  \\\\

The area of square

                                 &= 2400 \text{ cm}^2 \\\\

\dfrac{b}{a}             &= \dfrac{3}{4} \\\\

\end{aligned}

a                        &= \dfrac {7 \times 3 \times 4} {7} \\\\
