- $\pounds 8.40$
- $\pounds 8.75$
- $\pounds 87.50$
- $\pounds 865.00$
- $\pounds 9.25$    

</div>

$
\begin{aligned}

                               &= 25 \times 35 \\\\
                               &= 875  \text{p} \\\\
                               &= \pounds 8.75
\end{aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Find the cost of downloading $5$ apps if each one costs $\pounds 2.98$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of download five apps}       &= \text{Cost of downloading one app} \times \text{Number of apps} \\\\
                                        &= \text{Cost of downloading one app} \times 5\\\\
                                        &= \pounds 2.98 \times 5
\end{aligned}
$

ABBR: Let's first multiply the numbers ignoring the decimal points.


$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
                 & 2     & 9     & 8     \\
{}\times         &       &       & 5     \\
\hline 
           1     & 4     & 9     & 0     \\
\hline 
\end{array}
$

Between the two numbers, the total number of digits after the decimal point is two.

Let's account for the decimal point now. 

$= \pounds 14.90$

The cost of download $5$ apps $= \pounds 14.90$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 14.90$

</div>
</div>
