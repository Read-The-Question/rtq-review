$5^2\: \: \: 3^2\: \: \: 3^3\: \: \: 2^4$

</div>

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&=5^2,\: \: \: 3^2,\: \: \: 3^3,\: \: \: 2^4 \\\\
&= 5 \times 5,\: \: \: 3 \times 3,\: \: \: 3 \times 3 \times 3,\: \: \: 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \\\\
&= 25,\: \: \: 9,\: \: \: 27,\: \: \: 16 
\end{aligned}
$

Let's put them in ascending order now.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 9,\: \: \: 16,\: \: \: 25,\: \: \: 27
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
