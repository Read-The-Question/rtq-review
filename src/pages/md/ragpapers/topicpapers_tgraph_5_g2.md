What fraction of the children in class $6A$ chose swimming?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Fraction who chose swimming}                  &= \dfrac {90} {360} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {90} {90 \times 4} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {90}} {\cancel {90} \times 4} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {1} {4}
\end{aligned}
$

What percentage of the children in class $6A$ chose athletics?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{\%  who chose athletics}                      &= \dfrac {180} {360} \times 100 \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {180 \times 100} {360} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {180 \times 50 \times 2} {180 \times 2} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel {180} \times 50 \times \cancel 2} {\cancel {180} \times \cancel 2} \\\\
                                                    &= 50 \%
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

$6$ pupils chose swimming.

How many children are there altogether in class $6A$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the total number of children in the class $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {1} {4} \times a       &= 6 \\\\
\dfrac {a} {4}                &= 6 \\\\
a                             &= 6 \times 4 \\\\
a                             &= 24
\end{aligned}
$

Total number of children in class $= 24$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$24$

How many more children chose athletics than gymnastics?

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Difference}       &= \dfrac {180} {360} \times 24 - \dfrac {45} {360} \times 24 \\\\
                        &= (\dfrac {180} {360} - \dfrac {45} {360}) \times 24 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {180 - 45} {360} \times 24 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {135} {360} \times 24 \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {135 \times 24} {360} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {45 \times 3 \times 8 \times 3} {45 \times 8} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {\cancel {45} \times 3 \times \cancel 8 \times 3} {\cancel {45} \times \cancel 8} \\\\
                        &= 3 \times 3 \\\\
                        &= 9
\end{aligned}
$
