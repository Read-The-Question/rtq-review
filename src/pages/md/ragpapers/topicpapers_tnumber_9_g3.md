<div class='question question'>

Write down a number between $0.3$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times (0.3 + \dfrac {2} {5}) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times (0.3 + 0.4) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 0.7 \\\\
&= \dfrac {0.7} {2} \\\\
&= 0.35
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$0.35$

</div>
</div>

&= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {4} \times 6} {\cancel {4}} \\\\
&= 18
\end{aligned}
