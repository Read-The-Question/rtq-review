$26712 \div 36$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 26712 \div 36 \\\\
&= \dfrac {26712}{36} \\\\
&= \dfrac {742 \times 36}{36} \\\\
&= \dfrac {742 \times \cancel{36}}{\cancel{36}} \\\\
&= 742
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$742$
