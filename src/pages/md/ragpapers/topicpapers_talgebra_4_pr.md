Barry's number $= 2a$

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \sqrt{36a} \\\\
&= 6 \sqrt{a}
\end {aligned}

<div class='question question'>

Rosie's plant doubles its height every day.

It is $4 \ \text{cm}$ tall on Monday. On what day does it become taller than two metres?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the days after it become taller than two metres $= n$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
4 \ \text{cm} \times 2^n  &> 2 \ \text{m} \\\\
4 \ \text{cm} \times 2^n  &> 2 \times 100 \ \text{cm} \\\\
4 \times 2^n              &> 200 \\\\
2^n                       &> \dfrac {200} {4} \\\\
2^n                       &> \dfrac {50 \times 4} {4} \\\\
2^n                       &> \dfrac {50 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
2^n                       &> 50 \\\\
\end {aligned}
$


We can easily infer the value by using trial and error. Lets find out a number that satisfies the above equation.

|  Value of n  |  Value of $2 ^ n$  |
|:------------:|:------------------:|
|     $3$      |        $8$         |
|     $4$      |        $16$        |
|     $5$      |        $32$        |
|     $6$      |        $64$        |


$
\begin {aligned}
\text{The day it become taller than two metres} &= \text{Monday} + 6 \ \text{days} \\\\
                                                &= \text{Sunday}
\end {aligned}
$




</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Sunday

</div>
</div>
