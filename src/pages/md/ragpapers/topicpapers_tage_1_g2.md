<div class='question question'>

If Tom is half his mum’s age and together their ages add up to $72$, how old is Tom?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Tom's age $= a$

Tom's mum’s age $=2a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a + 2a                     &= 72 \\\\
3a                         &= 72 \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {72} {3} \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {24 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                          &= \dfrac {24 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3}} \\\\
a                          &= 24 \ \text{years}
\end {aligned}
$

Tom's age $= 24 \ \text{years}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$24 \ \text{years}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Guy is half as old as Gerald, who is three times older than Gill. If the sum of all of their ages is $99$, what are their ages?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Gill's age $= a$

Gerald's age $= 3a$

Guy's age $= \dfrac {3a} {2}$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
3a + \dfrac {3a} {2} + a                                        &= 99 \\\\
4a + \dfrac {3a} {2}                                            &= 99 \\\\
\dfrac {4a \times 2} {2} + \dfrac {3a} {2}                      &= 99 \\\\
\dfrac {8a} {2} + \dfrac {3a} {2}                               &= 99 \\\\
\dfrac {11a} {2}                                                &= 99 \\\\
a                                                               &= \dfrac {99 \times 2} {11} \\\\
a                                                               &= \dfrac {11 \times 9 \times 2} {11} \\\\
a                                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel {11} \times 9 \times 2} {\cancel {11}} \\\\
a                                                               &= 18 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

Gill's age $= 18 \ \text {years}$

Let's find Gerald's and Guy's age.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Gerald's age}    &= 3a \\\\
                        &= 3 \times 18 \\\\
                        &= 54 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Guy's age}       &= \dfrac {3a} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 18} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3 \times 2 \times 9} {2} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {2} \times 9} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
                        &= 27 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Gill $=$ $18 \ \text {years}$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Gerald $=$ $54 \ \text {years}$

</div>
<div class='answer'>

Guy $=$ $27 \ \text {years}$

</div>

How much older is Anna than Callum?

<div class='working'>

Anna's age $= a - 3$

Callum's age $= a - 7$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text{Difference between Anna and Callum} &= (a - 3) - (a - 7) \\\\
                                          &= a - 3 -a + 7 \\\\
                                          &= 4 \ \text{years}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>

How old will Ryan will be in $2$ years?

</div>

$
\begin{aligned}

3a                          &= 36 \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {36} {3} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {12 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                           &= \dfrac {12 \times \cancel {3}} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
a                           &= 12 \ \text{years} 
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Ryan's age after } 2 \text{ years }    &= 12 + 2 \\\\
                                            &= 14 \ \text{years}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$14 \ \text{years}$

</div>
