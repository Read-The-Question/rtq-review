Biscuits are sold in packets of $12$.

How many packets will I need to buy if I want $148$ biscuits?

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of packets needed to buy}       &= \dfrac {\text{Total number of biscuits}} {\text{Number of biscuits in each packet}} \\\\
                                             &= \dfrac {148} {12} \\\\
                                             &= 12 \text{ remainder } 4 \\\\
                                             &\approx 13
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{Biscuits}                   &:& \text{Packets} \\\\
= & 12                                &:& 1 \\\\
= & 1                                 &:& \dfrac {1} {12} \\\\
= & 1 \times 148                      &:& \dfrac {148} {12} \\\\
= & 148                               &:& 12 \text{ remainder } 4 \\\\
= & 148                               &:& \approx 13
\end{array}
$

Number of packets needed to buy $= 13$
