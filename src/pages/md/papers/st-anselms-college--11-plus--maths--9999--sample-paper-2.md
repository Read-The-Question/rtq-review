a                 &= \dfrac {108} {9} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {9 \times 12} {9} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {\cancel {9} \times 12} {\cancel {9}} \\\\
a                 &= 12
\end{aligned}
$

The number $= 12$

\begin {aligned}

                                      &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \\\\
                                      &= \dfrac{1}{2}
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned} 
\text {P(Blue)}+\text {P(Red)}                    &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\\\
\dfrac{x}{18 \times 2} + \dfrac{14}{18 \times 2}  &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\\\
\dfrac{x + 14}{18 \times 2}                       &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\\\
      x + 14                                      &= \dfrac{18 \times \cancel {2}}{\cancel {2}} \\\\
      x + 14                                      &= 18 \\\\
      x                                           &= 18 -14 \\\\
      x                                           &= 4 
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

In November $2007$ Becky is six times Anna’s age and Mary is four times Anna’s age. 

In November $2009$ Anna is a quarter of Becky’s age. Mary is six years younger than Becky. 

How old are the three girls in November $2009$?  

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Anna’s age in $2007$ $= a$

Becky’s age $= 6a$

Mary's age $= 4a$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
4 \times (a + 2)    &= 6a + 2 \\\\
4a + 8              &= 6a + 2 \\\\
6a + 2              &= 4a + 8 \\\\
6a - 4a             &= 8 - 2 \\\\
2a                  &= 6 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {6} {2} \\\\
a                   &= 3 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

Let's find the age of the three girls in $2009$.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Anna’s age}  &= a + 2 \\\\
                    &= 3 + 2 \\\\
                    &= 5 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Becky's age} &= 6a + 2 \\\\
                    &= 6 \times 3 + 2 \\\\
                    &= 18 + 2 \\\\
                    &= 20 \ \text {years} \\\\
\text {Mary's age}  &= 4a + 2 \\\\
                    &= 4 \times 3 + 2 \\\\
                    &= 12 + 2 \\\\
                    &= 14 \ \text {years}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
