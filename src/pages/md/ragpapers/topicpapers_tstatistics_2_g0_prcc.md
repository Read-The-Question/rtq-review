<div class='question question'>

Find the mean average of $8, 11,  15, 26$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


$
\begin {aligned}
\text{Mean}     &= \dfrac {8 + 11 + 15 + 26} {4} \\\\
                &= \dfrac {60} {4} \\\\
                &= \dfrac {4 \times 15} {4} \\\\
                &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 15} {\cancel 4} \\\\
                &= 15
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$15$

</div>
</div>
