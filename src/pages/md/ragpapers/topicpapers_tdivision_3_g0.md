<div class='question question'>

Work out $44.8 \div 0.7$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {44.8} {0.7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {44.8 \times 10} {0.7 \times 10} \\\\
&= \dfrac{448} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {64 \times 7} {7} \\\\
&= \dfrac {64 \times \cancel{7}} {\cancel{7}} \\\\
&= 64
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$64$
