<div class='question question'>

Naheeda uses $10$ matchsticks to make a row of $3$ squares.
How many matchsticks would she need to make a row of $7$ squares?

![forest-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png](/assets/forest-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png "forest-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-9-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As shown in the figure,

To make a row of $n$ squares, we need $3n + 1$ matchsticks.

So,

For making a row of 7 squares

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3 \times 7 + 1 \\\\
&= 21 + 1 \\\\
&= 22
\end{aligned}
$

She need $22$ matchsticks for making a row of 7 squares.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$22$

</div>
</div>

How many terms are there until the first negative term?
