<div class='question question'>

What is the missing number in the following sum? 

$4060800 = 4000000 + \ldots + 800$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the missing number $= a$.

$
\begin {aligned}
4060800                     &= 4000000 + a + 800 \\\\
4060800 - 4000000 - 800     &= a \\\\
a                           &= 60000
\end {aligned}
$

The missing number is $= 60000$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$60000$

</div>
