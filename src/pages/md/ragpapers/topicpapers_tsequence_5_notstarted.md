$1 \text{st}$ term $= 6, \quad 2 \text{nd}$ term $= 12, \quad 3 \text{rd}$ term $= 24, \quad 4 \text{th}$ term $= 48$ 

is a $GP$ because you multiply by $2$ to go from one term to the next.
