Four of these numbers can make two pairs so that each pair adds up to $5871$. Which number is the odd one out?

**A** $1917$

**B** $2743$ 

**C** $3008$ 

**D** $3954$

**E** $2863$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Two pairs are $=1917, 3954$ and $3008, 2863$

Let's verify.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 1917 + 3954 \\\\
&= 5871 \\\\
&= 3008 + 2863 \\\\
&= 5871
\end{aligned}
$

Odd one $= 2743$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>B</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>
