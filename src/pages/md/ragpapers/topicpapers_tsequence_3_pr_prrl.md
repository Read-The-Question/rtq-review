A wheel has $3$ different colours painted on its rim, red (R), green (G) and yellow (Y).

As it turns, a pattern is left on the ground.

![north-london-collegiate-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-33-00.png](/assets/north-london-collegiate-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-33-00.png "north-london-collegiate-school--11-plus--maths--9999--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-33-00.png")

Y R G Y R ...
