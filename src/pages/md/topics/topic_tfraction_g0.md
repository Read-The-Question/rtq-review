<div class='question question'>

Calculate $\frac {1} {3} - \frac {1} {6}$ 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {3} - \dfrac {1} {6} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1 \times 2} {3 \times 2} - \dfrac {1} {6} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2} {6} - \dfrac {1} {6} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 - 1} {6} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {6}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

\end{aligned}
