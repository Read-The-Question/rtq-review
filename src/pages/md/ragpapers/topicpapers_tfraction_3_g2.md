$\dfrac{3}{8}-\dfrac{1}{24}$

&= \dfrac {9 - 1} {24} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8} {24} \\\\
&= \dfrac {8} {3 \times 8} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{8}} {3 \times \cancel{8}} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end{aligned}
$
