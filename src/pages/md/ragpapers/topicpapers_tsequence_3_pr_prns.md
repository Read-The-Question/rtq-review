How many squares will there be in total in the $19^{th}$ pattern?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR:  Let's find the relation between the consecutive terms of the sequence.


The number of squares is calculated by adding $3$ to the previous number of squares.

This is an arithmetic sequence too and the difference between the consecutive patterns of the sequence is constant.

The next pattern is made by adding multiple of $3$ squares to the first pattern and the number of times it is added is based on the position in the sequence.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of squares in } 19^\text{th} \text{ pattern}   &= 1 + (19 - 1) \times 3 \\\\
                                                            &= 1 + 18 \times 3 \\\\
                                                            &= 1 + 54 \\\\
                                                            &= 55 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question subquestion'>

Which pattern number has $31$ white squares and $33$ black squares?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the term number $= a$

$
\begin{aligned}
1 + \dfrac{(a - 1)}{2} \times 3 &= 31 \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2} \times 3     &= 31 - 1 \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2} \times 3     &= 30 \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2}              &= \dfrac{30}{3} \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2}              &= \dfrac{3 \times 10}{3} \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2}              &= \dfrac{\cancel{3} \times 10}{\cancel{3}} \\\\
\dfrac{(a - 1)}{2}              &= 10 \\\\
a - 1                           &= 10 \times 2 \\\\
a - 1                           &= 20 \\\\
a                               &= 21
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
0 + \dfrac{a}{2} \times 3    &= 33 \\\\
\dfrac{a}{2}                 &= \dfrac{33}{3} \\\\
\dfrac{a}{2}                 &= \dfrac{11 \times 3}{3} \\\\
\dfrac{a}{2}                 &= \dfrac{11 \times \cancel{3}}{\cancel{3}} \\\\
\dfrac{a}{2}                 &= 11 \\\\
a                            &= 11 \times 2 \\\\
a                            &= 22
\end{aligned}
$

$22^\text{nd}$ pattern number has $31$ white squares and $33$ black squares.
