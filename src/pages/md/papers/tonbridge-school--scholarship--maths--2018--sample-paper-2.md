<div class='question subquestion'>

If $S = 1800$ and $R = 10r$ , find $r$.

<div class='working'>

\begin{aligned}
2\,\pi^2\,\times 10 \times 3.1 + 4\pi\,r^2                              &= 1800 \\\\

611.29 + 12.56 r                                                        &= 1800 \\\\
12.56 r^2                                                               &= 1800 - 611.29 \\\\
12.56 r^2                                                               &= 1188.71 \\\\
r^2                                                                     &= \dfrac {1188.71} {12.56} \\\\
r^2                                                                     &= 94.64 \\\\
r                                                                       &= \sqrt {94.64} \\\\
r                                                                       &= 9.7
\end{aligned}
