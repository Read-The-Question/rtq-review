<div class='question question'>

Circle which of the following has a different value from the others:

\begin{matrix}
3 - 7 - 4 && (3 - 7) - 4 && 3 - (7 - 4) && ((3 - 7) - 4)
\end{matrix} 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$3 - 7 - 4 = -8$

$(3 - 7) - 4 = (-4) - 4 = -8$

$3 - (7 - 4) = 3 - (3) = 0$

$((3 - 7) - 4) = ((-4) - 4) = -8$

Therefore,

\begin{matrix}
3 - 7 - 4 && (3 - 7) - 4 && \boxed{\green
{3 - (7 - 4)}} && ((3 - 7) - 4)
\end{matrix}

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3 - (7 - 4)$

</div>
