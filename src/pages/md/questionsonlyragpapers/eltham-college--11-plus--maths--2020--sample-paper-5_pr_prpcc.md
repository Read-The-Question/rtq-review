Which digit must go in the * position?

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
                          &   4   					          &  \boxed{\phantom{15}}     \\
\times                    &   \boxed{\phantom{15}}            &  \boxed{\phantom{15}}     \\
\hline
						  &	   \boxed{\phantom{15}}  	      &   8   					   &   \boxed{\phantom{15}}    \\
                    	  &			8      					  &   \boxed{\phantom{15}}     &   0   \\
\hline
\boxed{\phantom{15}}      &    \boxed{\phantom{15}}           &   4   &   *
\end{array}
$
