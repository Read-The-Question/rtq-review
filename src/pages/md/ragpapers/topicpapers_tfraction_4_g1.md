Which of the two shapes below has a greater percentage shaded? Explain how you know.

![st-anselms-college--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-3/section-1-question-14-subquestion-3-00.png](/assets/st-anselms-college--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-3/section-1-question-14-subquestion-3-00.png "st-anselms-college--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-3/section-1-question-14-subquestion-3-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{\% area shaded for Shape A}   &= \dfrac{1}{4} \times 100 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac{100}{4} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac{\cancel{4} \times 25}{\cancel{4}} \\\\
                                    &= 25 \% \\\\
\text{Total boxes for Shape B}      &= 9 + \dfrac{1}{2} \times 8 \\\\
                                    &= 9 + \dfrac{\cancel{2} \times 4}{\cancel{2}}   \\\\
                                    &= 9 + 4    \\\\
                                    &= 13     \\\\
\text{Shaded boxes for Shape B}     &= 2 + \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \\\\
                                    &= 2 + 1    \\\\
                                    &= 3        \\\\
\text{\% area shaded for Shape B}   &= \dfrac{3}{13} \times 100 \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac{300}{13} \\\\
                                    &\approx 23 \%
\end{aligned}
$

Shape A has a greater percentage shaded.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Shape A

</div>
</div>
