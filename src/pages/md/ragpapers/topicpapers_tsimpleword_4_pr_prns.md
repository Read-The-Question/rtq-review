If a bag of $2 \ \text{pence}$ coins weighs $350 \ \text{g}$, how many coins are there?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of coins} &= \dfrac {350} {7} \\\\
                       &= \dfrac {50 \times 7} {7} \\\\
                       &= \dfrac {50 \times \cancel {7}} {\cancel {7}} \\\\
                       &= 50
\end{aligned}
$
