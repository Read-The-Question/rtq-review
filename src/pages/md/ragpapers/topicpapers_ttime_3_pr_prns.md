<div class='question question'>

Jacqueline needs to catch a flight early tomorrow morning. She needs to get out of bed at $3.15 \ \text{am}$, but wants to make sure that she has at least $7\dfrac {1} {2}$ hours of sleep. What is the
                                
latest time that Jacqueline can go to sleep this evening? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {latest time she can go to sleep} &= 3.15 \ \text{am} - 7\dfrac {1} {2} \ \text {hours} \\\\
                                        &= 3:15 \ \text{am} - 7 \ \text {hours} \ 30 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
                                        &= 8:46 \ \text{am}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$8:46 \ \text{am}$

</div>
</div>
