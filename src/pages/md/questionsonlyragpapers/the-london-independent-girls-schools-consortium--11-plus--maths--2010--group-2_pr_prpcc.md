arithmetic using the symbol $\mho$.

For example, $5 \ \mho \ 3$ means double $5$ then subtract $3$.

So

$
\begin{aligned}
5 \ \mho \ 3 &= 2 × 5 - 3   \\
             &= 10 - 3      \\
             &= 7
\end{aligned}
$
