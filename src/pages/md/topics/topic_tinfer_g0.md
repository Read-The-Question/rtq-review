**Question**

Which three letter English word can be inserted to complete the sum below (adding with the product value of $\text{BAG}$ to
make the product value of $\text{GALA}?$

$\text{BAG} + \ldots = \text{GALA}$

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

Let the letter $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {BAG} + a &= \text {GALA} \\\\
a               &= \text {GALA} - \text {BAG} \\\\
                &= 7 \times 1 \times 12 \times 1 - 2 \times 1 \times 7 \\\\
                &= 84 - 14 \\\\
                &= 70 \\\\
                &= 2 \times 5 \times 7 \\\\
                &= \text {BEG}
\end{aligned}
$

The three letters are $\text {B}$, $\text {E}$, $\text {G}$.
</div>
