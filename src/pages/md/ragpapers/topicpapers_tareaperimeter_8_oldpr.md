<div class='question question'>

A rectangle measures $4.0\,cm$ by $7.1\,cm$. What is its area?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area}             &= 4.0 \times 7.1 \\\\
                        &= 28.4 \ \text{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
