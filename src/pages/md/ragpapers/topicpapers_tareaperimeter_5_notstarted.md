I have a square piece of paper.

The diagram shows information about this square labelled $A$.

![sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-00.png](/assets/sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-00.png "sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-00.png")

I fold square A in half to make rectangle $B$.

![sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-01.png](/assets/sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-01.png "sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-01.png")

Then I fold rectangle B in half to make square $C$.

![sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-02.png](/assets/sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-02.png "sevenoaks-school--11-plus--maths--2010--sample-paper-1/section-1-question-17-subquestion-1-02.png")

Complete the table below to show the area and perimeter of each shape.

|             |      Area      |   Perimeter  |
|:-------------:|:--------------:|:------------:|
| Square A    | $\cdots \ \text {cm}^2$ | $\cdots \ \text {cm}$ |
| Rectangle B | $\cdots \ \text {cm}^2$ | $\cdots \ \text {cm}$ |
| Square C    | $\cdots \ \text {cm}^2$ | $\cdots \ \text {cm}$ |

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Area of square} = \text{side} \times \text{side}$


ABBR: $\text{Area of rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$


ABBR: $\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$


ABBR: $\text{Perimeter of square} = 4 \times \text{side}$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area of A}        &= 8 \times 8 \\\\
                        &= 64 \ \text{cm}^2 \\\\
\text{Perimeter of A}   &= 4 \times 8 \\\\
                        &= 32 \ \text{cm}  \\\\
\text{Area of B}        &= 8 \times 4 \\\\
                        &= 32 \ \text{cm}^2 \\\\
\text{Perimeter of B}   &= 2 \times (4 + 8) \\\\
                        &= 12 \times 2 \\\\
                        &= 24 \ \text{cm}  \\\\
\text{Area of c}        &= 4 \times 4 \\\\
                        &= 16 \ \text{cm}^2 \\\\
\text{Perimeter of C}   &= 4 \times 4 \\\\
                        &= 16 \ \text{cm}  \\\\
\end{aligned}
$
