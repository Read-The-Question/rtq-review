<div class='question question'>

Eleven pencils cost $\pounds 4.95$. How much would seven pencils cost?

- $\pounds 3.50$
- $\pounds 3.20$
- $\pounds 4.00$
- $\pounds 3.15$
- $\pounds 4.15$ 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{number of pencils}      &:& \text{cost of pencils} \\\\
= & 11                            &:& \pounds 4.95 \\\\
= & 1                             &:& \dfrac{4.95} {11} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{4.95 \times 7} {11} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{495 \times 7} {11 \times 100} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{11 \times 45 \times 7} {11 \times 100} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{\cancel {11} \times 45 \times 7} {\cancel {11} \times 100} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{45 \times 7} {100} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \dfrac{315} {100} \\\\
= & 7                             &:& \pounds 3.15
\end{array}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>D</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 3.15$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Find the cost of downloading $5$ apps if each one costs $\pounds 2.98$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost of downloading five apps}        &= \text{Cost of downloading one app} \times \text{Number of apps} \\\\
                                            &= 2.98 \times 5 \\\\
                                            &= \dfrac {298} {100} \times 5 \\\\
                                            &= \dfrac {298 \times 5} {100} \\\\
                                            &= \dfrac {1490} {100} \\\\
                                            &= \pounds 14.9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
