How many **pages** are in his book?

a                        &= \dfrac {120 \times 3} {3} \\\\

\end{aligned}

$3a + 2b = 30$

2b                                  &= 0 \\\\
b                                   &= 0
\end{aligned}

<div class='question question'>

$p$ and $q$ are whole numbers. $p$ is $150$ greater than $q$.

The following statement is also true
                       
$\large p + q = 1000$

Calculate the numbers $p$ and $q$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$p = 150 + q$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
p + q         &= 1000 \\\\
150 + q + q   &= 1000 \\\\
150 + 2 q     &= 1000 \\\\
2 q           &= 1000 - 150 \\\\
2 q           &= 850 \\\\
q             &= \dfrac {850} {2} \\\\
q             &= \dfrac {425 \times 2} {2} \\\\
q             &= \dfrac {425 \times \cancel {2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
q             &= 425
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
p  &= 150 + q \\\\
p  &= 150 + 425 \\\\
p  &= 575
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$p = 575, q= 425$

</div>
</div>

<div class='workingcodeblock'>

As per the question,
