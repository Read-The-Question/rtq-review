On the planet Arithmetica, the natives have a special sort of arithmetic using the symbol $\spadesuit$.

For example, $5 \spadesuit 3$ means double $5$ then subtract $3$.

So,

$
\begin{aligned} 
5 \spadesuit 3 &= 2 \times 5 - 3 \\\\
&= 10 - 3 \\\\
&=7
\end{aligned}
$
