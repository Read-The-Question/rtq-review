\end{aligned}

</div>

\begin{aligned}

&= \dfrac {360} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {40 \times 9} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {40 \times \cancel{9}} {\cancel{9}} \\\\
&= 40
\end{aligned}

Let's use the answer above.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {4} {9} \ \text{of} \ 360 \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {9} \times 360 \\\\
&= 4 \times \dfrac {1} {9} \times 360 \\\\
&= 4 \times 40 \\\\
&= 160
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$40$
