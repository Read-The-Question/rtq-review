<div class='question question'>

If $432.6 \times 7 = 3028.2$, what is $43.26 \times 0.7%$ ?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 43.26 \times 0.7 \\\\
&= \dfrac {43.26 \times 10 \times 0.7 \times 10} {10 \times 10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {432.6 \times 7} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3028.2} {100} \text{ (As per the given information)}\\\\
&= 30.282
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
