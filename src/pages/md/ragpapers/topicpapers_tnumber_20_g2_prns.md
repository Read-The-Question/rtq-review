\end{aligned}

&= (8 + 4) \times (6 - 5) \\\\
&= 12 \times 1 \\\\
&= \red{12} \neq 67
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Option C}
&= (8 + 4) \times 6 - 5 \\\\
&= 12 \times 6  - 5 \\\\
&= 72 - 5 \\\\
&= \green{67}
\end{aligned}
$

Answer $= \text{C}$

<div class='option'>
<p>C</p>
