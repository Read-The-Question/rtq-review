How many pounds was $\text{\textdollar} 1000$ worth?

1 : 2.50                &= p : 1000 \\\\

<div class='question question'>

Calculators normally cost $\pounds 6$ and protractors normally cost $30 \ \text{p}$.

In a sale, calculators have been reduced by a quarter and protractors are half price.

David bought $8$ calculators and $20$ protractors.

How much did he have to pay? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

If the calculator cost is reduced by one quarter, then the new cost will be $1 - \dfrac {1} {4} = \dfrac {3} {4}$ of the original cost.

If the protractor cost is reduced by half, then the new cost will be $1 - \dfrac {1} {2} = \dfrac {1} {2}$ of the original cost.

Using that, we get

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cost}   &= 8 \times \dfrac {3} {4} \times \pounds 6 + 20 \times \dfrac {1} {2} \times 30 \ \text{p} \\\\
                    &= 8 \times \dfrac{3}{4} \times \pounds 6 + 20 \times \dfrac {1} {2} \times \pounds 0.30 \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{8 \times 3 \times 6}{4} + \dfrac {20 \times 0.3}{2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{4 \times 2 \times 3 \times 6}{4} + \dfrac {10 \times 2 \times 0.3}{2} \\\\
                    &= \dfrac{\cancel{4} \times 2 \times 3 \times 6}{\cancel{4}} + \dfrac {10 \times \cancel{2} \times 0.3}{\cancel{2}} \\\\
                    &= 2 \times 3 \times 6 + 10 \times 0.3 \\\\
                    &= 36 + 3 \\\\
                    &= \pounds 39
\end{aligned}
$

$\pounds 39$

<div class='question question'>

Jordan eats $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a pizza and Anna eats $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of what is left.

Rohit, Guhesh and Matilda share equally the remainder.

What fraction of the pizza did Matilda eat? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>

\text {Anna ate}    &= \dfrac {1} {3} \ \text {of} \ \left(1 - \dfrac {1} {4} \right) \\\\

                    &= \dfrac {1} {3} \times \left( \dfrac {4} {4} - \dfrac {1} {4} \right) \\\\

                    &= \dfrac {2} {4}
