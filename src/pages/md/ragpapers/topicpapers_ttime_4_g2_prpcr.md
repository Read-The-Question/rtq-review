<div class='question subquestion'>

How many minutes did the crossing from Dover to Calais actually 
take?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text{Time taken} &=  09{:}11 - 08{:}02 \\\\
&= 1 \ \text{hour} \ 9 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 1 \times 60 \ \text{minutes} \ 9 \ \text{minutes} \\\\
&= 60 + 9 \\\\
&= 69 \ \text{minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>
