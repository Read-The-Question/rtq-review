\dfrac{7000}{100}                            &= \dfrac{700}{10} \\\\
\dfrac{70 \times 100}{100}                   &= \dfrac{70 \times 10}{10} \\\\
\dfrac{70 \times \cancel{100}}{\cancel{100}} &= \dfrac{70 \times \cancel{10}}{\cancel{10}} \\\\
70                                           &= 70
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
