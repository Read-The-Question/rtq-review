Albert has five times as many apples as Bertie. If Albert gives Bertie $26$ apples, they will each have the same number of apples.

How many apples do they have in total between them?

b                       &= 13
a                       &= 13 \times 4 \\\\
a                       &= 52 \\\\
a + b                   &= 52 + 13 \\\\
a + b                   &= 65
\end{aligned}
