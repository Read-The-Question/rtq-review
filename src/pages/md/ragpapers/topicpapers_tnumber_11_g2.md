What  number   is half  way   between **3.2** and **5.6**?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times (3.2 + 5.6) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times 8.8 \\\\
&= \dfrac {8.8} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4.4 \times 2} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4.4 \times \cancel{2}} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
&= 4.4
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4.4$

</div>
</div>
