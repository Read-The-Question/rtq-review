\end {aligned}

Malcolm buys $7$ packets of biscuits at $73 \text{p}$ each and $9$ large cakes at $\pounds l.13$ each. How much change will he receive from a $$\pounds 20$ note?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Money spent}     &= 7 \times 73 \text {p} + 9 \times \pounds 1.13 \\\\
                        &= 7 \times \pounds 0.73 + \pounds 10.17 \\\\
                        &= 5.11 + 10.17 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 15.28 \\\\
\text {Change received} &= 20 - 15.28 \\\\
                        &= \pounds 4.72
\end {aligned}
$
