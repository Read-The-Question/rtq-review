<div class='question question'>

A shop sells coffee from Ethiopia.

The prices for coffee beans and ground coffee are shown below.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


![missing table](/papers/missing_table.svg)


How much more does it cost to buy $250 \text{g}$ of ground coffee than $250 \text{g}$ of coffee beans?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of ground coffee = a

Let the cost of coffee beans = b

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a                               &= \dfrac {2.10} {100} \times 250 \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {2.10} {50 \times 2} \times 50 \times 5\\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {2.10} {\cancel {50} \times 2} \times \cancel{50} \times 5\\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {2.10}{2} \times 5 \\\\
a                               &= \pounds 5.250 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$



$
\begin {aligned}
b                               &= \dfrac {9.40} {1000} \times 250 \\\\
b                               &= \dfrac {9.40} {250 \times 4} \times 250 \\\\
b                               &= \dfrac {9.40} {\cancel {250} \times 4} \times \cancel{250} \\\\
b                               &= \dfrac {9.40} {4} \\\\
b                               &= \pounds 2.350 \\\\
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Difference in cost} &= a - b \\\\
                          &= 5.250 - 2.350 \\\\
                          &= \pounds 2.900
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 2.900$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Sandy needs $12$ pizzas to feed $30$ people.

How many pizzas will she need to feed $35$ people?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of pizza for one person $=a$

Number of pizza to feed 35 people $= 35 a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a                              &= \dfrac {12} {30} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {3 \times 4} {3 \times 10} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {\cancel 3 \times 4} {\cancel 3 \times 10} \\\\
a                              &= \dfrac {4}{10} \\\\
a                              &= 0.4 \\\\
\end{aligned}
$


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of pizza to feed 35 people} &= 35 \times a \\\\
                                         &= 35 \times 0.4 \\\\
                                         &= 14 
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$14$

</div>
