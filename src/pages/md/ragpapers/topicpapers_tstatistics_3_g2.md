<div class='question subquestion'>

Two numbers have a mean of $12$ and a range of $6$. 

What are the two numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Let the second number $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
\dfrac {a + b} {2}      &= 12 \\\\
a + b                   &= 12 \times 2 \\\\
a + b                   &= 24 & (1) \\\\
b - a                   &= 6  & (2)
\end {aligned}
$

Let's use elimination to reduce it to just one variable. Add the two equations to eliminate $a$.

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b                   &= 24 \\\\
b - a                   &= 6  \\\\
a + b + b -a            &= 24 + 6 \\\\
2b                      &= 30 \\\\
b                       &= \dfrac {30} {2} \\\\
b                       &= 15
\end {aligned}
$

Let's substitute the value of $b$ in one of the initial equations to find the value of $a$.

$
\begin{aligned}
a + b                       &= 24  \\\\
a + 15                      &= 24 \\\\
a                           &= 24 - 15 \\\\
a                           &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
