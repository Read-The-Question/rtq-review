**Multiplication and Division**

Here is the correct answer to a multiplication problem:

$6 \times 5678 \times 10 = 340680$

Use this information to help you work out the answer to this division problem:

$340680 \div 5678$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 340680 \div 5678 \\\\
&= \dfrac{340680}{5678} \\\\
&= \dfrac{ 6 \times 5678 \times 10}{5678} \\\\
&= \dfrac{\cancel{5678} \times 6 \times 10}{\cancel{5678}} \\\\
&= 6 \times 10 \\\\
&= 60
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$60$

</div>
</div>
