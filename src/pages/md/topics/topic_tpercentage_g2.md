<div class='question question'>

$60\%$ of a number is $240$. What is the number? 

A: $60$ 

B: $144$ 

C: $240$ 

D: $300$ 

E: $400$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
60 \% \ \text {of} \ a               &=  240 \\\\
\dfrac {60} {100} \times a           &=  240 \\\\
a                                    &=  240 \times \dfrac {100} {60} \\\\
a                                    &=  \dfrac {240 \times 100} {60} \\\\
a                                    &=  \dfrac {60 \times 4 \times 100} {60} \\\\
a                                    &=  \dfrac {\cancel {60} \times 4 \times 100} {\cancel {60}} \\\\
a                                    &=  4 \times 100 \\\\
a                                    &=  400
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>E</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

$400$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Find $5\%$ of $24$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 5 \% \text{ of } 24 \\\\
&= \dfrac {5} {100} \times 24 \\\\
&= \dfrac {5 \times 24} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 6 \times 10} {2 \times 5 \times 10} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 6 \times \cancel {10}} {\cancel {2} \times 5 \times \cancel {10}} \\\\
&= \dfrac {6} {5}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {6} {5}$
