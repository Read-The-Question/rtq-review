<div class='question question'>

Think of two integers that have a product of $18$ and a difference 
that is the same as one of the two integers that you are thinking of. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first number $= a$

Let the second number $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times b            &= 18 \\\\
a - b                 &= b \\\\
a                     &= 2 \times b
\end{aligned}
$

We have got $2$ variables and we have $2$ equations to solve them. Let's eliminate to reduce it to just one variable.

Substituting the value of $a$ from second equation to first equation,

$
\begin{aligned}
a \times b            &= 18 \\\\
2 \times b \times b   &= 18 \\\\
2 b^2                 &= 18 \\\\
b ^ 2                 &= \dfrac {18} {2} \\\\
b ^ 2                 &= \dfrac {2 \times 9} {2} \\\\
b ^ 2                 &= \dfrac {\cancel {2} \times 9} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
b ^ 2                 &= 9 \\\\
b                     &= \sqrt {9} \\\\
b                     &= 3
\end{aligned}
$

Substituting the value of $b$ in second equation.

$
\begin{aligned}
a - b &= b \\\\
a - 3 &= 3 \\\\
a     &= 3 + 3 \\\\
a     &= 6
\end{aligned}
$

The two integers are $3$ and $6$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Three friends went to a burger restaurant.

Tom had a burger and chips and he paid $\pounds 8.00$.

Fred had two burgers and a drink. Fred paid $\pounds 15.00$.

Jo had a burger and a drink. Jo paid $\pounds 8.50$.

Work out the cost of the burger, the chips and the drink. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the cost of a burger $= b$

Let the cost of chips $= c$

Let the cost of a drink $= d$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Tom paid}    &= \pounds 8 \\\\
b + c               &= 8 \\\\
\text {Fred paid}   &= \pounds 15 \\\\
2b + d              &= 15 \\\\
\text {Jo paid}     &= \pounds 8.50 \\\\
b + d               &= 8.50
\end{aligned}
$

Using the above two equations we get,

$
\begin{aligned} 
2b + d - b - d  &= 15 - 8.50 \\\\
b               &= \pounds 6.50
\end{aligned}
$

Cost of the burger $= \pounds 6.50$

$
\begin {aligned}
b + c       &= 8 \\\\
6.50 + c    &= 8 \\\\
c           &= 8 - 6.50 \\\\
c           &= \pounds 1.50
\end {aligned}
$

Cost of the chips $= \pounds 1.50$

$
\begin {aligned}
b + d       &= 8.50 \\\\
6.50 + d    &= 8.50 \\\\
d           &= 8.50 - 6.50 \\\\
d           &= \pounds 2
\end {aligned}
$

Cost of the drink $= \pounds 2$

</div>
</div>

</div>
<div class='answer'>

</div>
