<div class='question question'>

$4$ girls have an average height of $1.4 \ \text{m}$. A fifth girl of height $1.2 \ \text{m}$ joins them.
What is the average height of all five girls?

-  $2.6 \ \text{m}$
-  $1.3 \ \text{m}$
-  none of these
-  $1.36 \ \text{m}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total height of 4 girls}     &= 4 \times 1.4 \\\\
                                    &= 5.6 \ \text {m} \\\\
\text {Average height of 5 girls}   &= \dfrac {5.6 + 1.2} {5} \\\\
                                    &= \dfrac {6.8} {5} \\\\
                                    &= 1.36 \ \text {m}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

a                                       &= 28 - 18 \\\\

\end {aligned}
