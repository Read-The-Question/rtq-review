<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

I plant some seeds and get three white flowers. Write down how many blue flowers I should expect to grow.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the expected number of blue flowers $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
1 \ \text{white} : 3 \ \text{blue}              &= 3 \ \text{white} : a \ \text{blue} \\\\
\dfrac{1} {3}                                   &= \dfrac {3} {a} \\\\
1 \times a                                      &= 3 \times 3 \\\\
a                                               &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

Expected number of blue flowers $= 9$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{white}                  &:& \text{blue} \\\\
= & 1                             &:& 3 \\\\
= & 1 \times 3                    &:& 3 \times 3 \\\\
= & 3                             &:& 9
\end{array}
$

Expected number of blue flowers $= 9$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$9$

</div>
</div>

<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

I plant a total of $30$ seeds in another patch of the garden. Calculate how many white flowers I would expect to grow in this patch.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the expected number of white flowers $= a$

Expected number of pink flowers $= 2a$

Expected number of blue flowers $= 3a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 2a + 3a         &= 30 \\\\
6a                  &= 30 \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {30} {6} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {6 \times 5} {6} \\\\
a                   &= \dfrac {\cancel 6 \times 5} {\cancel 6} \\\\
a                   &= 5
\end{aligned}
$

Expected number of white flowers $= 5$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using whole to part ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{seeds}                                      &:& \text{white} \\\\
= & 1 + 2 + 3                                         &:& 1 \\\\
= & 6                                                 &:& 1 \\\\
= & 6 \times 5                                        &:& 1 \times 5 \\\\
= & 30                                                &:& 5
\end{array}
$

Expected number of white flowers $= 5$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$5$

<div class='question subquestion'>

In another area of the garden, $24$ blue flowers are grown. Calculate how many pink flowers I should expect to find here.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the expected number of pink flowers $= a$

Using equivalent ratio,

$
\begin{aligned}
3 \ \text{blue} : 2 \ \text{pink}               &= 24 \ \text{blue} : a \ \text{pink} \\\\
\dfrac{3}{2}                                    &= \dfrac {24} {a} \\\\
3 \times a                                      &= 24 \times 2 \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {24 \times 2} {3} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {8 \times 3 \times 2} {3} \\\\
a                                               &= \dfrac {8 \times \cancel 3 \times 2} {\cancel 3} \\\\
a                                               &= 8 \times 2 \\\\
a                                               &= 16
\end{aligned}
$

Expected number of pink flowers $= 16$

</div>
<div class='working'>

Using ratio,

$
\begin{array}{cccccccccccccc}
  & \text{blue}                               &:& \text{pink} \\\\
= & 3                                         &:& 2 \\\\
= & 3 \times 8                                &:& 2 \times 8 \\\\
= & 24                                        &:& 16
\end{array}
$

Expected number of pink flowers $= 16$

</div>
