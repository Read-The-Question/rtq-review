<div class='question question'>

I have a painting in a frame. The frame is $75 \ \text{cm}$ wide and $65 \ \text{cm}$ high. There is a border $15 \ \text{cm}$ wide around the painting. Find the width and height of the painting.

![reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-5/section-1-question-29-00.png](/assets/reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-5/section-1-question-29-00.png "reigate-grammar-school--11-plus--maths--2018--sample-paper-5/section-1-question-29-00.png")

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the picture,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Width of the painting}                        &= \text{Total width} - 2 \times \text{Frame height} \\\\
                                                    &= 75 \ \text{cm} - 2 \times 15 \ \text{cm} \\\\
                                                    &= 75 - 30 \\\\
                                                    &= 45 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$


Let's find the height of the painting.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Height of the painting}                       &= \text{Total height} - 2 \times \text{Frame height} \\\\
                                                    &= 65 \ \text{cm} - 2 \times 15 \ \text{cm} \\\\
                                                    &= 65 - 30 \\\\
                                                    &= 35 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Width $= 45 \ \text{cm}$

Height $= 35 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

Width $= 45 \ \text{cm}$

Height $= 35 \ \text{cm}$

</div>
</div>
