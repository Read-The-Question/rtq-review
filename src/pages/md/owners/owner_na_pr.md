<div class='question question'>

A child’s heart beats $80$ times per minute. How many times will it beat between $8.30\ \text{am}$ and $3.30\ \text{pm}$ on the same day? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Time difference}  &= 3 {:} 30 \ \text {pm} - 8 {:} 30 \ \text {am} \\\\
                         &= 15 {:} 30 - 8 {:} 30 \\\\
                         &= 7 \ \text {hours} \\\\
                         &= 7 \times 60 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                         &= 420 \ \text {minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Total heartbeat}  &= \text {(Time difference)} \times 80 \ \text {bpm} \\\\
                         &= 420 \times 80 \\\\
                         &= 33600 \ \text {times}
\end{aligned}
$

The child's heart beat 33600 times between $8.30 \ \text {am}$ and $3.30 \ \text {pm}$.

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

$33600$

A sequence follows the rule:
Treble and add one
Explain why if the first term is $\frac{-2} {9}$ the second term is $\frac{1} {3}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

The next number is tripled and added by one.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {-2} {9} \times 3 + 1 \\\\
&= \dfrac {-2 \times 3} {9} + 1 \\\\
&= \dfrac {-6} {9} + \dfrac {1 \times 9} {1 \times 9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {-6} {9} + \dfrac {9} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {-6 + 9} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3} {3 \times 3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {3}} {\cancel {3} \times 3} \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {3}
\end{aligned}
$

The second term = $\dfrac {1} {3}$
