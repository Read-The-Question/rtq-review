<div class='question question'>

Work out $80\%$ of $\pounds535$

A: $\pounds107$

B: $\pounds372$

C: $\pounds400$

D: $\pounds418$

E: $\pounds428$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
&= \dfrac {80} {100} \times 535 \\\\
&= \dfrac {80 \times 535} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {20 \times 4 \times 5 \times 107} {20 \times 5 } \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{20} \times 4 \times \cancel{5} \times 107} {\cancel{20} \times \cancel{5} } \\\\
&= 4 \times 107  \\\\
&=  \pounds {428}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 428$
