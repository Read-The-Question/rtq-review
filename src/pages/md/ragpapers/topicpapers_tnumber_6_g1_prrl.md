<div class='question question'>

Add brackets to this calculation to that the answer is $5$.

$22 - 10 + 8 \div 4$

-  $22 -(10 + 8) \div 4$
-  $(22 - 10) + 8 \div 4$
-  $(22 - 10 + 8) \div 4$
-  $22 - 10 + (8 \div 4)$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{A}    &= 22 - (10 + 8)\div 4 \\\\
            &= 22 - 18\div 4 \\\\
            &= 22 - 4.5 \\\\
            &= \red{17.5} \neq 5 \\\\
\text{B}    &=(22 - 10) + 8 \div 4\\\\
            &= 12 + 2 \\\\
            &= \red{14} \neq 5 \\\\
\text{C}    &= (22 - 10 + 8) \div 4 \\\\
            &= 20 \div 4 \\\\
            &= \green{5} \\\\
\text{D}    &= 22 - 10 + (8 \div 4) \\\\
            &= 12 + 2 \\\\
            &= \red{14} \neq 5
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
