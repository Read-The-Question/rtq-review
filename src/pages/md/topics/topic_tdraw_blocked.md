Which is smallest? Circle your answer

- $\dfrac{(2+3)}{(4+6)}$
- $\dfrac{(2\div3)}{(4\div6)}$
- $\dfrac{23}{46}$
- $\dfrac{(2-3)}{(4-6)}$
- $\dfrac{(2\times3)}{(4\times6)}$ 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{(2+3)}{(4+6)}                                        &= \dfrac{5}{11} \\\\
\dfrac{(2\div3)}{(4\div6)}                                  &= 1 \\\\
\dfrac{23}{46}                                              &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\\
\dfrac{(2-3)}{(4-6)}                                        &= \dfrac{-1}{-2} \\\\
\dfrac{(2\times3)}{(4\times6)}                              &= \dfrac{6}{24} \\\
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac{5}{11}$
