<div class="question" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Question**

A red light flashes four times a minute and a green light flashes six times a minute. If both lights start flashing at the same instant, how many times do they flash at the same instant in one hour including the first and the last time?

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">
<strong style="display: block; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Question Raw</strong>
</div>
<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex">A red light flashes four times a minute and a green light flashes six times a minute. If both lights start flashing at the same instant, how many times do they flash at the same instant in one hour including the first and the last time?
</code></pre>
</div>
<div class="workings">
<div class="tips placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Tips**

<div class="tip">
%empty%

<div class="formulas placeholder" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Formulas**

<div class="formula">
%empty%
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Interval at which red light flashes}         &= \dfrac {60} {4} \\\\
                                                   &= \dfrac {15 \times 4} {4} \\\\
                                                   &= \dfrac {15 \times \cancel 4} {\cancel 4} \\\\
                                                   &= 15 \ \text{s} \\\\
\text{Interval at which blue light flashes}        &= \dfrac {60} {6} \\\\
                                                   &= \dfrac {10 \times 6} {6} \\\\
                                                   &= \dfrac {10 \times \cancel 6} {\cancel 6} \\\\
                                                   &= 10 \ \text{s}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Interval at which both will flash}        &= \text{LCM of 15 and 10} \\\\
                                                &= 30 \ \text{s}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Number of times they flash together}     &= \dfrac {60 \times 60} {30} + 1 \\\\
                                                &= \dfrac {30 \times 2 \times 60} {30} + 1 \\\\
                                                &= \dfrac {\cancel {30} \times 2 \times 60} {\cancel {30}} + 1 \\\\
                                                &= 2 \times 60 + 1 \\\\
                                                &= 120 + 1 \\\\
                                                &= 121
\end{aligned}
$
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$121$

</div>
