A multipack of $12$ cans of Ned's Cola costs $\pounds 6.95$. 

A single can of Ned's Cola costs $65 \ \text{pence}$ if bought individually. 

Calculate how much cheaper it is to buy a multipack of $12$ cans than to buy $12$ cans individually, giving your answer in pence.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Cost of 12 cans}                 &= 12 \times 65 \\\\
                                        &= 780 \ \text{p} \\\\
\text {Cheaper by}                      &= 780 \ \text{p} - \pounds 6.95 \\\\
                                        &= 780 \ \text{p} - 695 \ \text{p} \\\\
                                        &= 85 \ \text{p}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$85 \ \text{p}$

</div>

A multipack of $15$ packets of crisps costs $\pounds 3.90$.

A packet of crisps costs $35 \ \text{pence}$ if they are bought individually.

Calculate how much cheaper it is to buy a multipack of $15$ packets of crisps than to buy $15$ packets individually.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required
.

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Cost of 15 packets}  &= 15 \times 35 \\\\
                            &= 525 \ \text{p} \\\\
\text {Cheaper by}          &= 525 \ \text{p} - \pounds 3.90 \\\\
                            &= 525 \ \text{p} - 390 \ \text{p} \\\\
                            &= 135 \ \text{p}
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

How many $26 \ \text{p}$ stamps can Antonia buy for $\pounds 10$ and how much change will she receive? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Total stamps}    &= \dfrac {\pounds 10} {26 \ \text{p}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {1000 \ \text{p}} {26 \ \text{p}} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {1000} {26} \\\\
                        &= 38 \ \text {remainder} \ 12 \\\\
                        &\approx 38 \\\\
\text {Change received} &= 1000 - 38 \times 26 \\\\
                        &= 1000 - 988 \\\\
                        &= 12 \ \text{p}
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
