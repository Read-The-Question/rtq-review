and $z$.

$
\begin {aligned}
(2 \ \clubsuit \ 3) \ \clubsuit \ 4 &= (2^2 + 3) \ \clubsuit \ 4 \\
&= 7 \ \clubsuit \ 4 \\
&= 7^2 + 4 \\
&= 49 + 4 \\
&= 53 
\end {aligned}
$
