<div class='question subquestion'>

Which is bigger:

$3^4$ or $4^3$?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's calculate the first value.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3 ^ 4 \\\\
&= 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \\\\
&= 9 \times 9 \\\\
&= 81
\end{aligned}
$

Let's calculate the second value.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 4 ^ 3 \\\\
&= 4 \times 4 \times 4 \\\\
&= 16 \times 4 \\\\
&= 64
\end{aligned}
$

Using the calculation above,

$3 ^ 4$ is bigger than $4 ^ 3$.

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3 ^ 4$

</div>
</div>
