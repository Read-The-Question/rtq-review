<div class='question question'>

Which  of these is the largest number? (You must show your working)

- $2 + 0 \times 1 + 9$

- $2 \times 0 + 1 + 9$

- $2 + 0 \times 1 \times 9$

- $2 \times 0 + 1 + 9$

- $2 \times 0 \times 1 \times 9$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS
,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{a}    &= 2 + 0 \times 1 + 9 \\\\
            &= 2 + 0 + 9 \\\\
            &= 11 \\\\
            &= \green{11} \\\\
\text{b}    &=2 \times 0 + 1 + 9\\\\
            &= 0 + 1 + 9 \\\\
            &= \red{10} \\\\
\text{c}    &= 2 + 0 \times 1 \times 9\\\\
            &= 2 + 0 \times 9 \\\\
            &= 2 + 0 \\\\
            &= \red{2} \\\\
\text{d}    &= 2 \times 0 + 1 + 9 \\\\
            &= 0 + 1 + 9 \\\\
            &= \red{10} \\\\
\text{e}    &= 2 \times 0 \times 1 \times 9 \\\\
            &= 0 \times 1 \times 9 \\\\
            &= 0  \times 9 \\\\
            &= \red{0}
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='option'>
<p>a</p>
</div>
<div class='answer'>

 $2 + 0 \times 1 + 9$
