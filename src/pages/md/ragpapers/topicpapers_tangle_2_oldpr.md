<div class='question question'>

In a triangle, one of the angles is $45$ degrees. The other two angles in the triangle are in the ratio $4 : 5$. How big is the largest angle in the triangle? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4x  + 5x + 45                    &= 180 \\\\
9x + 45                          &= 180 \\\\
9x                               &= 180 - 45 \\\\
9x                               &= 135 \\\\
x                                &= \dfrac {135} {9} \\\\
x                                &= \dfrac {9 \times 15} {9} \\\\
x                                &= \dfrac {\cancel {9} \times 15} {\cancel {9}} \\\\
x                                &= 15 \\\\
5x                               &= 5 \times 15 \\\\
5x                               &= 75 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$75 \degree$

</div>
