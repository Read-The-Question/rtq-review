<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Fraction (Shaded)} &=  \text{No. of shaded grids / Total grids}

\begin{aligned}

                            &= \dfrac {28 \times 100} {100} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {28 \times \cancel{100} } {\cancel{100} } \\\\

\end{aligned}
