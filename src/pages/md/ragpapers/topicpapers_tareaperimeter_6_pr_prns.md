<div class='question question'>

A cuboid has faces with areas of $24 \ \text{cm}^{2}, 32 \ \text{cm}^{2}$ and $48 \ \text{cm}^{2}$.

What are the lengths of its edges? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the length of first edge of cuboid $= l$

Let the length of second edge of cuboid $= w$

Let the length of third edge cuboid $= h$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
l \times w                                      &= 24 \\\\
w                                               &= \dfrac {24} {l} \\\\
w \times h                                      &= 32 \\\\
\dfrac {24} {l} \times h                        &= 32 \\\\
\dfrac {24h} {l}                                &= 32 \\\\
24h                                             &= 32 \times l \\\\
h                                               &= \dfrac {32 \times l} {24} \\\\
l \times h                                      &= 48 \\\\
l \times \dfrac{32 \times l} {24}               &= 48 \\\\
\dfrac{l \times 32 \times l} {24}               &= 48 \\\\
\dfrac{32 \times l^2} {24}                      &= 48 \\\\
32 \times l^2                                   &= 48 \times 24 \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {48 \times 24} {32} \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {16 \times 3 \times 2 \times 12} {16 \times 2} \\\\
l^2                                             &= \dfrac {\cancel {16} \times 3 \times \cancel {2} \times 12} {\cancel {16} \times \cancel {2}} \\\\
l^2                                             &= 3 \times 12 \\\\
l^2                                             &= 36 \\\\
l                                               &= \sqrt {36} \\\\
l                                               &= 6 \ \text {cm} \\\\
w                                               &= \dfrac {24} {6} \\\\
w                                               &= \dfrac {6 \times 4} {6} \\\\
w                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel {6} \times 4} {\cancel {6}} \\\\
w                                               &= 4 \ \text {cm} \\\\
l \times h                                      &= 48 \\\\
6 \times h                                      &= 48 \\\\
h                                               &= \dfrac {48} {6} \\\\
h                                               &= \dfrac {6 \times 8} {6} \\\\
h                                               &= \dfrac {\cancel {6} \times 8} {\cancel {6}} \\\\
h                                               &= 8 \ \text {cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Length of the edges of the cuboid are $6 \ \text {cm}, \ 4 \ \text {cm}, \ 8 \ \text {cm}$.

</div>
</div>

$6 \ \text {cm}, \ 4 \ \text {cm}, \ 8 \ \text {cm}$
