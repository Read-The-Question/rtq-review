There are four unknown positive whole numbers. 
The mean of the two smallest numbers is $3$.
The mean of the three smallest numbers is $5$. 
The mean of all four numbers is $7$.
All the numbers are different and odd. 
What are the four numbers?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: $\text{Mean} = \dfrac {\text{Total of items}} {\text{Number of items}}$


Let the four unknown numbers $= a, b, c, d$

$
\begin {aligned}
\dfrac {a + b} {2}                          &= 3 \\\\
\text {a + b}                               &= 3 \times 2 \\\\
a + b                                       &= 6 \\\\
\dfrac {a + b + c} {3}                      &= 3 \\\\
\text {a + b + c}                           &= 5 \times 3 \\\\
a + b + c                                   &= 15 \\\\
\dfrac {a + b + c + d} {4}                  &= 3 \\\\
\text {a + b + c + d}                       &= 7 \times 4 \\\\
a + b + c + d                               &= 28
\end {aligned}
$

Let's subtract second question from third to get the value of d.

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b + c + d - (a + b + c)       &= 28 - 15 \\\\
a - a + b - b + c - c + d         &= 13 \\\\
d                                 &= 13
\end {aligned}
$

Let's subtract first question from second to get the value of c.

$
\begin {aligned}
a + b + c - (a + b)       &= 15 - 6 \\\\
a - a + b - b + c         &= 9 \\\\
c                         &= 9
\end {aligned}
$

Let's infer the value of a and b using trial and error method.

|   a   |      b      |   Different  |
|:-----:|:-----------:|:------------:|
|  $1$  | $6 - 1 = 5$ |     Yes      |
|  $3$  | $6 - 3 = 3$ |     No       |

Value of a $= 1$

Value of b $= 5$
