<div class='question question'>

A pile of hay can feed $12$ horses for $15$ days.

For how long could the same pile of hay feed $20$ horses? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of days}     &= \dfrac {12 \times 15} {20} \\\\
                          &= \dfrac {4 \times 3 \times 5 \times 3} {5 \times 4} \\\\
                          &= \dfrac {\cancel 4 \times 3 \times \cancel 5 \times 3} {\cancel 5 \times \cancel 4} \\\\
                          &= 3 \times 3 \\\\
                          &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$9$

</div>
</div>
