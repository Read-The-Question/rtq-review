<div class='question question'>

Gurveen is buying paint and there are three sizes of can to choose from:

One $2 \ \text{litre}$ can cost $\pounds 15$

One $3 \ \text{litre}$ can cost $\pounds20$

One $4 \ \text{litre}$ can cost $\pounds25$

Which size is the best value for money?

Justify your answer carefully.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let's calculate the cost per litre for three cans,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {First can}   &= \dfrac {15} {2} \\\\
                    &= \pounds 7.50 \\\\
\text {Second can}  &= \dfrac {20} {3} \\\\
                    &= \pounds 6.33 \\\\
\text {Third can}   &= \dfrac {25} {4} \\\\
                    &= \pounds 6.25
\end {aligned}
$

Using the calculation above,

Best value $= 4 \ \text {litre can}$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4 \ \text {litre can}$

</div>

                            &= \dfrac {4 \times 125} {4 \times 7} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 125} {\cancel {4} \times 7} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {125} {7} \\\\

                            &\approx 17
\end {aligned}
