<div class='question question'>

Sam thinks of a number. 
If you double the number and add $6$ you get the same answer as multiplying it by $3$ and subtracting $3$.

Find the number that Sam was thinking of. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number $=a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
3a - 3  &= 2a + 6 \\\\
3a - 2a &= 6 + 3 \\\\
a       &= 9
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$9$

</div>
