Choose from the list:

<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

a fraction which is greater than $1$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {3} {4}   &= 0.75 \\\\
\dfrac {5} {8}   &= 0.62 \\\\
\dfrac {15} {11} &= 1.36 \\\\
\dfrac {8} {12}  &= 0.66 \\\\
\dfrac {4} {5}   &= 0.80
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {15} {11}$

</div>
</div>

</div>
</li>
<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

a fraction equivalent to $80\%$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
80\%             &= \dfrac {80} {100} \\\\
                 &= 0.80 \\\\
\dfrac {4} {5}   &= 0.80
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {4} {5}$

</div>
</div>

</div>
</li>
<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {3} {4}$

</div>
</div>

</div>
</li>
<li>
<div class='question_envelope rag_not_found rag_not_found subquestion'>
<div class='question subquestion'>

a fraction which is not in its simplest form.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac {8} {12}   &= \dfrac {4 \times 2} {4 \times 3} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {\cancel{4} \times 2} {\cancel{4} \times 3} \\\\
                  &= \dfrac {2} {3}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {8} {12}$

</div>
</div>
