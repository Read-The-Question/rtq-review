What is $1 \dfrac {1} {2}$ times $5.6$?

<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}

&= \dfrac {3} {2} \times 5.6 \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 5.6} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times 2 \times 2.8} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {3 \times \cancel {2}  \times 2.8} {\cancel {2}} \\\\
&= 3 \times 2.8 \\\\
&= 8.4
\end{aligned}
$
