The rule $a * b$ means $(a \times a) + (b \times b)$
 
That is, you multiply the first number by the first number, the second number by the second number and then you add your answers together.

For example, 

$
\begin{aligned}
6 * 4 &= (6 \times 6) + (4 \times 4) \\
      &= 36           + 16           \\
      &= 52     
\end{aligned}
