<div class='question question'>

A ball is dropped and bounces up to a height that is $75\%$ of the height from which it
was dropped. It then bounces again to a height that is $75\%$ of the previous height and 
so on. How many bounces does it make before it bounces to a height that is less than 
$25\%$ of the original height?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


$
\begin{aligned}
\text{let bounces} &= x \\\\
\text{let height} &= h \\\\
\text{new height} &= 0.75 \times h \\\\
0.25 \times h &= (0.75)^x \times h \\\\
x &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

For example, $5 \ \spadesuit \ 3$ means double $5$ then subtract $3$.

5 \ \spadesuit \ 3  &= 2 \times 5 - 3 \\\\

14 - 2n                             &= 8 \\\\
14 - 8                              &= 2n \\\\
2n                                  &= 6 \\\\
n                                   &= 3
\end {aligned}
