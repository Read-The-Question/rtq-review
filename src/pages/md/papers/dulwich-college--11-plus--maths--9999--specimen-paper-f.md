Simon and Fiona are sharing a cupboard. If Simon’s things take up $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the space and Fiona’s things take up 
$\dfrac{2}{7}$ of the space, what fraction of the cupboard is empty? 

</div>

\text{Empty Space}      &= \text{Total space} - \text{Used space} \\\\

                        &= 1 - \bigg(\dfrac {2 \times 7} {5 \times 7} + \dfrac {2 \times 5} {7 \times 5}\bigg) \\\\
                        &= 1 - \bigg(\dfrac {14} {35} + \dfrac {10} {35}\bigg) \\\\
                        &= 1 - \bigg(\dfrac {14 + 10} {35}\bigg) \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {1} {1} - \dfrac {24} {35} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {1 \times 35} {1 \times 35} - \dfrac {24} {35} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {35 - 24} {35} \\\\
                        &= \dfrac {11} {35}
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

Jon has $30\%$ more money than Miguel and together they have $\pounds 46$. Calculate how much money Jon has. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let Miguel has (in $\pounds$) $= a$

Jon has (in $\pounds$) $= a + 0.3 \times a = 1.3a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 1.3a                        &= 46 \\\\
2.3a                            &= 46 \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {46} {2.3} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {46 \times 10} {23} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {23 \times 2 \times 10} {23} \\\\
a                               &= \dfrac {\cancel{23} \times 2 \times 10} {\cancel{23}} \\\\
a                               &= 2 \times 10 \\\\
a                               &= \pounds 20 \\\\
\text {Jon has}                 &= 1.3 \times a \\\\
                                &= 1.3 \times 20 \\\\
                                &= \pounds 26
\end{aligned}
$
