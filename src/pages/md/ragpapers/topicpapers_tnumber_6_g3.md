What is $36 \div (9 + 3)?$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

ABBR: Using BIDMAS


$
\begin{aligned}
&= 36 \div (9 + 3) \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {12} \\\\
&= \dfrac {12 \times 3} {12} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel {12} \times 3} {\cancel {12}} \\\\
&= 3
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3$
