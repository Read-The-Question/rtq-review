It was reported recently that in an average lifetime of $70$ years, each
human is likely to swallow about $8$ spiders while sleeping. Supposing
that the population of UK is $60$ millions, what is the best
estimate of number of spiders that are consumed in this way in the 
UK each year?

- $600000$
- $7000000$
- $80000000$
- $900000000$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of spiders consumed per year} &= \dfrac {60000000 \times 8} {70} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {480000000} {70} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {48000000 \cancel 0} {7 \cancel 0} \\\\
                                           &= \dfrac {48000000} {7} \\\\
                                           &= 6857142 \text{ remainder } 6 \\\\
                                           &\approx 7000000
\end{aligned}
$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

A swimming pool has a length that is four times of its width.

The length and width are both multiples of $5$.

The perimeter is less than $150 \ \text{m}$.

How many different widths could it have?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the length $= l$

Let the width $= w$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
l                &= 4b \\\\
2 \times (l + b) &< 150 \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac{150} {2} \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac {75 \times 2} {2} \\\\
l + b            &< \dfrac {75 \times  \cancel 2} {\cancel 2} \\\\
l + b            &< 75 \\\\
4b + b           &< 75 \\\\
5b               &< 75 \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {75} {5} \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {5 \times 15} {5} \\\\
b                &< \dfrac {\cancel 5 \times 15} {\cancel 5} \\\\
b                &< 15
\end{aligned}
$

Length and width are multiples of $5$

Possible values of b $= 5, 10$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$5, 10$

</div>
