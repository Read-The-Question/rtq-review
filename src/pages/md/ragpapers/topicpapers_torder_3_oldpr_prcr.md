$250 \ \text{m} \quad  0.52 \ \text{km}    \quad 0.5 \ \text{km} \quad 2.05 \ \text{km} \quad 502 \ \text{m}$

$
\begin{aligned}

$

&= 8\% , \quad 9 \ \text{hundredths}, \quad \dfrac {3} {4} , \quad \dfrac {4} {5} , \quad 0.802 , \quad 0.82
\end{aligned}
$
