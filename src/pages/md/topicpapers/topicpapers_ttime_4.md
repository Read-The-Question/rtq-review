<div class='question question'>

A child’s heart beats $80$ times per minute. How many times will it beat between $8.30\ \text{am}$ and $3.30\ \text{pm}$ on the same day? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Time difference}  &= 3 {:} 30 \ \text {pm} - 8 {:} 30 \ \text {am} \\\\
                         &= 15 {:} 30 - 8 {:} 30 \\\\
                         &= 7 \ \text {hours} \\\\
                         &= 7 \times 60 \ \text {minutes} \\\\
                         &= 420 \ \text {minutes}
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {Total heartbeat}  &= \text {(Time difference)} \times 80 \ \text {bpm} \\\\
                         &= 420 \times 80 \\\\
                         &= 33600 \ \text {times}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$33600$

</div>

The opening hours of my local supermarket are $7 \ \text{am}$ to $9 \ \text{pm}$ Mondays to Saturdays and 
$10{:}30 \ \text{am}$ to $4{:}30 \ \text{pm}$ on Sundays. For how many hours does this supermarket open each week?

<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Opening hours from Monday to Saturday} &= 9{:}00 \ \text{pm} - 7{:}00 \ \text{am} \\\\
                                             &= 14 \ \text{hours} \\\\
\text{Opening hours on Sunday}               &= 4{:}30 \ \text{pm} - 10{:}30 \ \text{am} \\\\
                                             &= 6 \ \text{hours}
\end{aligned}
