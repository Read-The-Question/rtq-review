Thrse are the start and finish times on a DVD recorder:

$\bold{START\:\:\:\: 14{:}48}$

$\bold{FINISH\:\:\:\: 17{:}25}$

For how long was the video recording? 

</div>

\end{aligned}
$

</div>

\end{aligned}
