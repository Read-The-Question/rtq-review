<div class='question question'>

It costs $\pounds 400$ to carpet a room measuring $2\, metres$ by $3\, metres$. How much would it costto carpet another room measuring $4\, metres$ by $6\, metres$ with the same carpet? 

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)
                                                     

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost per area}          &= \dfrac {400} {2 \times 3} \\\\
\text{Total cost}             &= (4 \times 6) \times \dfrac {400} {2 \times 3} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {4 \times 6 \times 400} {2 \times 3} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {4 \times 2 \times 3 \times 400} {2 \times 3} \\\\
                              &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel 2 \times \cancel 3 \times 400} {\cancel 2 \times \cancel 3} \\\\
                              &= 4 \times 400 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 1600
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\pounds 1600$

</div>
</div>

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)
