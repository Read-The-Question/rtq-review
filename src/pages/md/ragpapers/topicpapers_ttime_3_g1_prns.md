<div class='question question'>

Subtract the number of $\text{hours}$ in a day from the number of $\text{seconds}$ in a $\text{minute}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \text{Number of seconds in a minute} - \text{Number of hours in a day} \\\\
&= 60 - 24 \\\\
&= 36
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$36$
