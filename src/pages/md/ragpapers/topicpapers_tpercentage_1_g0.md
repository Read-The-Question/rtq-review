<div class='question question'>

What percentage of this shape is shaded?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the diagram,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Percentage shaded}   &= \dfrac{3}{5}\ \text{of}\ 100 \% \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3} 5 \times 100 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3 \times 100} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3 \times 20 \times 5} {5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {3 \times 20 \times \cancel {5}} {\cancel {5}} \\\\
                            &= 3 \times 20 \\\\
                            &= 60 \%
\end {aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

What is $60\%$ of $10\%$ of $$900$ ? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 60 \% \ \text {of} \ 10 \% \ \text {of} \ 900 \\\\
&= \dfrac {60} {100} \times \dfrac {10} {100} \times 900 \\\\
&= \dfrac {60 \times 10 \times 900} {100 \times 100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {540000} {10000} \\\\
&= \dfrac {54 \cancel {0000} } {1 \cancel {0000}} \\\\
&= 54
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$54$

</div>
</div>

What is $10\%$ of $\pounds 60$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 10\% \ \text{of} \ \pounds 60 \\\\
&= \dfrac {10} {100} \times 60 \\\\
&= \dfrac {10 \times 60} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {600} {100} \\\\
&= \dfrac {6 \cancel {00}} {1 \cancel {00}} \\\\
&= \pounds 6
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
