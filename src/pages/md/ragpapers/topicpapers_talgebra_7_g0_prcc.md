<div class='question question'>

When a glass is full of water the total mass is $400 \ \text{g}$. 
When the glass is half full of water the total mass is $320 \ \text{g}$. Find the mass of the glass.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


Let the mass of water $= w$

Let the mass of glass $= g$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
w + g                                              &= 400 \\\\
\dfrac{w}{2} + g                                   &= 320 \\\\
\dfrac{w}{2} + \dfrac{g \times 2}{1 \times 2}      &= 320 \\\\
w + \dfrac{2g}{2}                                  &= 320 \\\\
w + 2g                                             &= 320 \times 2 \\\\
w + 2g                                             &= 720
\end {aligned}
$

We have got 2 variables and we have 2 equations to solve them. Lets eliminate to reduce it to just one variable.

Subtract the first equation from the second to reduce it to one variable.

$
\begin {aligned}
w + 2g - w - g                &= 720 - 400 \\\\
g                             &= 240 \ \text{g}
\end {aligned}
$

Mass of glass $= 240 \ \text{g}$

</div>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{mass of half water} &= 400 - 320 \\\\
                          &= 80 \\\\
\text{mass of water}      &= 2 \times 80 \\\\
                          &= 160 \\\\
\text{Mass of glass}      &= \text{Total mass} - \text{Mass of water} \\\\
                          &= 400 -  160 \\\\
                          &= 240 \ \text{g}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$240 \ \text{g}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Mrs Jones prints books.

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


Jon pays $\bold{\pounds 4.35}$ for his book, **including the cover**.

How many **pages** are in his book?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the number of pages $=a$

ABBR: It is really important to use the right units in calculations and convert where required.

Let's convert pounds in pence.

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
3 \times a + 75 \text{p} &= \pounds 4.35 \\\\
3 \times a + 75 \text{p} &= 4.35 \times 100 \text{p} \\\\
3 \times a + 75          &= 435 \\\\
3 \times a               &= 435 - 75 \\\\
3 \times a               &= 360 \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {360} {3} \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {120 \times 3} {3} \\\\
a                        &= \dfrac {120 \times \cancel {3}} {\cancel {3}} \\\\
a                        &= 120
\end{aligned}
$

Number of pages in the book = $120$.
