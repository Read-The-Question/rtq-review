ABBR: $\text {Exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the two interior opposite angles}$.

ABBR: $\text{Sum of angles in a triangle} = 180 \degree$


ABBR: $\text{Angles on a straight line add up to} = 180 \degree$


Let the angle in the triangle FGH $= a$

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
v + 64      &= 107 \\\\
v           &= 107 - 64 \\\\
v           &= 43 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $v$ to find $a$.

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + v + 90          &= 180 \\\\
a + 43 + 90         &= 180 \\\\
a + 133             &= 180 \\\\
a                   &= 180 - 133 \\\\
a                   &= 47 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $a$ to find $w$.

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
w + a       &= 61 \\\\
w + 47      &= 61 \\\\
w           &= 61 - 47 \\\\
w           &= 14 \degree
\end{aligned}
$

Let's use the value of $w$ to find $u$.

As per the diagram,

$
\begin{aligned}
u + 61      &= w + 64 \\\\
u + 47      &= 14 + 64 \\\\
u + 47      &= 78 \\\\
u           &= 78 - 47 \\\\
u           &= 31 \degree
\end{aligned}
$
