<div class='question question'>

What percentage of the shape below is shaded?

![missing image](/papers/missing_image.svg)


</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the diagram,

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {Percentage shaded}   &= \dfrac {\text {Shaded triangles}} {\text {Total triangles}} \times 100 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {15} {20} \times 100 \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {15 \times 100} {20} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {15 \times 5 \times 20} {20} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac {15 \times 5 \times \cancel {20}} {\cancel {20}} \\\\
                            &= 15 \times 5 \\\\
                            &= 75 \%
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$75 \%$

</div>
