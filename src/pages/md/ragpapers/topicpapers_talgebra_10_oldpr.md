                              &= \pounds 6 \\\\
\text{Total money}            &= 60 + 30 + 6 \\\\
                              &= \pounds 96
\end{aligned}
$

If they received $\pounds 160$ altogether in May, how much did __John__ receive that month?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the money John received (in $\pounds$) $= a$

Money Helen received $= 5a$

Money Peter received $= 10a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
a + 5a + 10a      &= \pounds 160 \\\\
16a               &= 160 \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {160} {16} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {10 \times 16} {16} \\\\
a                 &= \dfrac {10 \times \cancel {16}} {\cancel {16}} \\\\
a                 &= \pounds 10
\end{aligned}
$

Money John received $= \pounds 10$

<div class='question subquestion'>

There is a number, $x$. When I add $7$ to $x$ then multiply the answer by $8$, the overall result is the same as just multiplying $x$ by 22. What is $x$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 7) \times 8  &= x \times 22 \\\\
8x + 56           &= 22x \\\\
8x - 22x          &= -56 \\\\
{}-14x            &= -56 \\\\
\cancel {-} 14x   &= \cancel {-} 56 \\\\
14x               &= 56 \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {56} {14} \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {4 \times 14} {14} \\\\
x                 &= \dfrac {4 \times \cancel {14}} {\cancel {14}} \\\\
x                 &= 4
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$4$
