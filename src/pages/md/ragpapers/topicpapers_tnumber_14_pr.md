One kilometre is five eighths of a mile. How many miles do I live from St Anselm’s? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>


$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3.2 \ \text{km} \\\\
&= 3.2 \times \dfrac {5} {8} \ \text{miles} \\\\
&=  8 \times 0.4 \times \dfrac {5} {8} \\\\
&=  \cancel {8} \times 0.4 \times \dfrac {5} {\cancel {8}}  \\\\
&= 5 \times 0.4 \\\\
&= 2 \ \text {miles}
\end{aligned}
$


</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$2 \ \text {miles}$

Write the three prime numbers which multiply to make $231$:

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Prime numbers $= 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, \ldots$

As the unit digit has $1$, we can rule out $2$ and $5$ .

Let's try $3$, $7$ and $11$.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3 \times 7 \times 11 \\\\
&= 21 \times 11 \\\\
&= 231
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3, 7, 11$

</div>
</div>
