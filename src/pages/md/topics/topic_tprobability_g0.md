**Question**

I have a bag containing red, blue and white counters. A counter is chosen at random from the bag. There are $24$ red counters in the bag and the probability of choosing a red counter is $\dfrac {1} {6}$ . The bag contains $15$ blue counters. How many white counters are there in the bag? 

</div>
<div class="questioncodeblock" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">
<div class="questioncodeblock_title">
<strong style="display: block; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Question Raw</strong>
</div>
<pre class="language-latex"><code class="language-latex">I have a bag containing red, blue and white counters. A counter is chosen at random from the bag. There are $24$ red counters in the bag and the probability of choosing a red counter is $\dfrac {1} {6}$ . The bag contains $15$ blue counters. How many white counters are there in the bag? 
</code></pre>
</div>

**Formulas**

<div class="formula">
- ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$
</div>
</div>
<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

Let the white counters $= a$

Total counters $= 24 + 15 + a$

$
\begin{aligned}
\text {P(red counter)}                           &= \dfrac {24} {24 + 15 + a} \\\\
\dfrac {1} {6}                                   &= \dfrac {24} {24 + 15 + a} \\\\
24 + 15 + a                                      &= 24 \times 6 \\\\
a + 39                                           &= 144 \\\\
a                                                &= 144 - 39 \\\\
a                                                &= 105
\end{aligned}
$

There are $105$ white counters in the bag.
</div>
</div>
<div class="answers">
<div class="answer" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Answer**

$105$

**Question**

A bag contains coloured counters of just **__two__** colours.  There are twenty green counters numbered $1$ to $20$ and thirty blue counters numbered $1$ to $30$. What is the probability of picking a counter from the bag which is a multiple of four?

<emptyline>

<div class="formula">
- ABBR: $\text{Probability}  = \dfrac { \text{ Favourable outcomes} } { \text{Total outcomes} }$
</div>

<div class="working" style="margin: 1rem 0; padding: 0.85rem 1rem; border: 1px solid #9ea7b3; border-radius: 6px;">

**Working**

$
\begin{aligned} 
\text{Multiples of } 4 \text{ in green counter}    &= 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 \\\\
\text{Multiples of } 4 \text{ in blue counter}     &= 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28 \\\\
\text{P(Multiple of 4)}                             &= \dfrac {5 + 7} {50} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {12} {50} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {2 \times 6} {2 \times 25} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 6} {\cancel{2} \times 25} \\\\
                                                    &= \dfrac {6} {25}
\end{aligned}
$
