<div class='question question'>

Jane is making a spiral out of wire. She bends the wire 
after $1 \ \text{cm}$, then bends the wire 
after $2 \ \text{cm}$, then $3 \ \text{cm}$ and continues in this manner.
 
After $4$ bends she has used  $15 \ \text{cm}$ of wire.

![the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-31-00.png](/assets/the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-31-00.png "the-london-independent-girls-schools-consortium--11-plus--maths--2011--group-2/section-1-question-31-00.png")

Complete the table

|Number of Bends         |$1$|$2$|$3$|$4$ |
|:----------------------:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:--:|
|Centimetres of wire used|   |   |   |$15$|


How much wire will she have used after $6$ bends?
 
How many bends will Jane have made if she uses $66 \ \text{cm}$ of wire?
