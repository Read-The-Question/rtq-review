What is $4 - ((4 + 4) \div 4)$? 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&=   4 - ( (4 + 4) \div 4) \\\\
&=   4 - (8 \div 4) \\\\
&=   4 - \dfrac {4 \times 2} {4} \\\\
&=   4 - \dfrac {\cancel {4} \times 2} {\cancel {4} } \\\\
&=   4 - 2 \\\\
&=  2
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$2$

</div>
</div>
