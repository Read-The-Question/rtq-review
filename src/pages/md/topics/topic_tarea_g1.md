<div class='question question'>

Find the area of a square whose perimeter is $20 \ \text{cm}$.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the side of the square (in $\text{cm}$) $= a$

As per the question,

$
\begin{aligned}
4 \times a  &= 20 \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {20} {4}  \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {5 \times 4} {4}  \\\\
a           &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel {4}} {\cancel {4}}  \\\\
a           &= 5 \ \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$

Now, let's find the area of the square.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Area}     &= a \times a \\\\
                &= 5 \times 5 \\\\
                &= 25 \ \text{cm}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$25 \ \text{cm}^{2}$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

Adrian’s garden measures $10 \ \text{m}$ by $6 \ \text{m}$. 

Paving stones measure $0.5 \ \text{m}$ by $0.5 \ \text{m}$.

How many paving stones does he need to pave his garden?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Number of paving stones} &= \dfrac {\text{Area of the garden}} {\text{Area of one stone}} \\\\
                               &= \dfrac {10 \times 6} {0.5 \times 0.5} \\\\
                               &= \dfrac {10 \times 6 \times 10 \times 10} {0.5 \times 10 \times 0.5 \times 10} \\\\
                               &= \dfrac {5 \times 2 \times 6 \times 5 \times 2 \times 10} {5 \times 5} \\\\
                               &= \dfrac {\cancel {5} \times 2 \times 6 \times \cancel {5} \times 2 \times 10} {\cancel {5} \times \cancel {5}} \\\\
                               &= 2 \times 6 \times 20 \\\\
                               &= 240
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$240$

</div>

<div class='question question'>

A piece of wire $30 \ \text{cm}$ long is bent into the shape of a rectangle. 
If the rectangle is twice as long as it is wide, what is its area?  

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the width of the rectangle $= a$.

As per the question,

Length of the rectangle $= 2a$.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Perimeter of the rectangle}         &= 2 \times (\text {length} + \text {width}) \\\\
30                                        &= 2 \times (2a + a) \\\\
30                                        &= 2 \times 3a \\\\
6a                                        &= 30 \\\\
a                                         &= \dfrac {30} {6} \\\\
a                                         &= \dfrac {5 \times 6} {6} \\\\
a                                         &= \dfrac {5 \times \cancel {6}} {\cancel {6}} \\\\
a                                         &= 5 \ \text {cm}
