Which fraction is bigger, four fifths or eight ninths?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

LCM of $5 , 9 = 45$

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \text{four fifths or eight ninths} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4 \times 9} {5 \times 9} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8 \times 5} {9 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ < \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ < \ \dfrac {8} {9}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {8} {9}$

</div>

Write down a fraction between nine fifths and two.

&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times ( \dfrac {19} {5} ) \\\\
&= \dfrac {19} {10} \\\\
&= 1 \dfrac {9} {10} 
\end{aligned}

Write down a $\underline{decimal}$ between $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$

<div class='working'>

Half-way between two numbers is the same as finding the average of two numbers.

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {\dfrac {2} {5} + \dfrac {1} {2}} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\dfrac {2 \times 2} {5 \times 2} + \dfrac {1 \times 5} {2 \times 5}} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\dfrac {4} {10} + \dfrac {5} {10}} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\dfrac {4 + 5} {10}} {2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9} {10 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {9} {20}\\\\
&= 0.45
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

Find $\text{one ninth of 360}$

Use your answer to find $\text{four ninths of 360}$

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {9} \ \text{of} \ 360 \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {9} \times 360 \\\\
&= \dfrac {360} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {40 \times 9} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {40 \times \cancel{9}} {\cancel{9}} \\\\
&= 40
\end{aligned}
$

Using the above answer to find $\dfrac {4} {9} \ \text{of} \ 360$.

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{four ninths of 360} &= 4 \times \text{one ninth of 360} \\\\
                          &= 4 \times 40 \\\\
                          &= 160
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {9} \text{ of } 360 \ = \ 40 \\\ \\\\ \dfrac {4} {9} \text{ of } 360 \ = \ 160$
