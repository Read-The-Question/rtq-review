<div class='question question'>

The rule  $a * b$ means $a \times a + b \times b$

That is, you multiply the first number by itself, multiply the second number by itself and then add the answers together.

For example, 

$
\begin{aligned}
2 * 3 &= 2 \times 2 + 3 \times 3 \\
      &= 4          + 9          \\
      &= 13     
\end{aligned}
$

And

$
\begin{aligned}
1 * 2 &= 1 \times 1 + 2 \times 2 \\
      &= 1          + 4          \\
      &= 5     
\end{aligned}
$

<div class='question question'>

Find two whole numbers which multiply together to give $18$ and a difference of $7$. 

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

Let the first whole number $= a$

Let the second whole number $= b$

As per the question,

$
\begin {aligned}
a \times b  &= 18 \\\\
a - b       &= 7 \\\\
a           &= 7 + b
\end {aligned}
$

We have got $2$ variables and we have $2$ equations to solve. Let's reduce it to just $1$ equation,

$
\begin {aligned}
a \times b              &= 18 \\\\
(7 + b) \times b        &= 18 \\\\
7b + b ^ 2              &= 18 \\\\
b ^ 2 + 7b - 18         &= 0 \\\\
b ^ 2 + 9b - 2b - 18    &= 0 \\\\
b (b + 9) -2 (b + 9)    &= 0 \\\\
(b - 2) (b + 9)         &= 0 \\\\
b -2                    &= \dfrac {0} {b - 9} \\\\
b - 2                   &= 0 \\\\
b                       &= 2
\end {aligned}
$

$
\begin {aligned}
\text {The second whole number}     &= 2 \\\\
\text {The first whole number}      &= 7 + b \\\\
                                    &= 7 + 2 \\\\
                                    &= 9
\end {aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

$2$

a                                                     &= \dfrac {\cancel {3} \times 11} {\cancel {3}} \\\\

\end {aligned}
