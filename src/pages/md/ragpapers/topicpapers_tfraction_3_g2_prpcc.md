Which fraction is bigger, four fifths or eight ninths?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8} {9} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4 \times 9} {5 \times 9} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {8 \times 5} {9 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ \text{or} \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {36} {45} \ < \ \dfrac {40} {45} \\\\
&= \dfrac {4} {5} \ < \ \dfrac {8} {9}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {8} {9}$

<div class='question subquestion'>

Calculate two thirds of five and a quarter.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {2} {3} \text{ of } 5 \dfrac {1} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2} {3} \times 5 \dfrac {1} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2} {3} \times \dfrac {21} {4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 21} {3 \times 4} \\\\
&= \dfrac {2 \times 7 \times 3} {3 \times 2 \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac {\cancel{2} \times 7 \times \cancel{3}} {\cancel{3} \times \cancel{2} \times 2} \\\\
&= \dfrac{7} {2} \\\\
&= 3 \dfrac {1} {2}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$3 \dfrac {1} {2}$

<div class='question subquestion'>

Four cakes are divided equally amongst seven people. One fifth of a cake is left over. What fraction of a cake does each person get?

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Each persons share}    &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( 4 - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{4 \times 5}{5}  - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{20}{5}  - \dfrac{1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times \bigg ( \dfrac{20 - 1}{5} \bigg ) \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{1}{7} \times  \dfrac{19}{5} \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{19}{7 \times 5}  \\\\
                            &= \dfrac{19}{35}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac{19}{35}$

</div>
</div>

Write down a fraction between nine fifths and two.

</div>
<div class='workings'>
<div class='working'>

$
\begin{aligned}
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg ( \dfrac {9} {5} + 2\bigg ) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg ( \dfrac {9} {5} + \dfrac {2 \times 5} {1 \times 5} \bigg ) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg ( \dfrac {9} {5} + \dfrac {10} {5} \bigg ) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times \bigg ( \dfrac {9 + 10} {5} \bigg ) \\\\
&= \dfrac {1} {2} \times  \dfrac {19} {5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {19} {2 \times 5} \\\\
&= \dfrac {19} {10}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>
<div class='answers'>
<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {19} {10}$

What fraction of $2.4 \ \text{litres}$ is $400 \ \text{ml}$?

\text{Fraction} &= \dfrac {400  \ \text{ml}} {2.4 \ \text{litres}} \\\\\
                &= \dfrac {400  \ \text{ml}} {2.4 \times 1000 \ \text{ml}} \\\\\
                &= \dfrac {400} {24 \times 100} \\\\\
                &= \dfrac {4 \times 100} {4 \times 6 \times 100} \\\\\
                &= \dfrac {\cancel{4} \times 1\cancel{0}\cancel{0}} {\cancel{4} \times 6 \times 1\cancel{0}\cancel{0}} \\\\\
                &= \dfrac{1}{6}
\end{aligned}
$

</div>
</div>

<div class='answer'>

$\dfrac {1} {6}$
